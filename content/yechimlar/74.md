# 74. Teskari trigonometrik funksiyalar

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
$\arcsin a$ — sinusi $a$ ga teng va $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ oraliqda yotgan burçak.
$\sin\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, demak $\arcsin\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

### 2 [A]
$\arccos a$ — kosinusi $a$ ga teng va $[0;\pi]$ oraliqda yotgan burçak.
$\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2}=0$, demak $\arccos0=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 3 [B]
$\operatorname{arctg}a$ — tangensi $a$ ga teng va $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ oraliqdagi burçak.
$\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$, demak javob $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

### 4 [A]
Formula: $\operatorname{arcctg}(-a)=\pi-\operatorname{arcctg}a$.
$\operatorname{arcctg}(-1)=\pi-\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.

### 5 [C]
$\arcsin\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\arccos\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=\pi-\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
$2\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

### 6 [B]
$\operatorname{arctg}(-\sqrt3)=-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\operatorname{arcctg}(-\sqrt3)=\pi-\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.
$-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+\dfrac{5\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

### 7 [B]
$\arccos\dfrac12=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\arcsin\left(-\dfrac12\right)=-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\operatorname{arctg}(-1)=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{4\pi-2\pi+3\pi}{12}=\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.

### 8 [D]
Ayniyat: $\arcsin a+\arccos a=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. $\operatorname{arcctg}\sqrt3=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

### 9 [C]
Ayniyat: $\operatorname{arctg}a+\operatorname{arcctg}a=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $\arccos(-1)=\pi$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\pi=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 10 [D]
$\operatorname{arctg}\sqrt3=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\operatorname{arcctg}\sqrt3=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, ayirma $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
$\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 11 [A]
$\theta=\arcsin\left(-\dfrac7{25}\right)\in\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, bu oraliqda $\cos\theta\ge0$.
$\cos\theta=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{49}{625}}=\dfrac{24}{25}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{24}{25}$.

### 12 [B]
$\theta=\arccos\left(-\dfrac23\right)\in(0;\pi)$, $\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\dfrac49}=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{3}$.
$\operatorname{ctg}\theta=\dfrac{-2/3}{\sqrt5/3}=-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}=-\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{5}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$.

### 13 [A]
$\theta=\arccos\dfrac35$: $\cos\theta=\dfrac35$, $\sin\theta=\dfrac45$.
$\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta=2\cdot\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac{24}{25}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{24}{25}$.

### 14 [D]
Formula: $\cos3\theta=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta$, $\cos\theta=\dfrac23$.
$4\cdot\dfrac{8}{27}-3\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{32}{27}-\dfrac{54}{27}=-\dfrac{22}{27}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{22}{27}$.

### 15 [B]
$\theta=\operatorname{arctg}\sqrt8$ (I çorak): $\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+8}}=\dfrac13$.
$\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{1+\frac13}{2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac23}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$.

### 16 [A]
$\arcsin\dfrac35$ burçakning kosinusi $\dfrac45$, tangensi $\dfrac34$.
$\operatorname{tg}(a+b)=\dfrac{\frac12+\frac34}{1-\frac12\cdot\frac34}=\dfrac{5/4}{5/8}=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 17 [C]
Keltiriş formulasi: $\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\theta\right)=\cos\theta$.
$\theta=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac34$ uçun $\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+\frac9{16}}}=\dfrac45$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{5}$.

### 18 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}(a+b)=\dfrac{4+\frac53}{1-4\cdot\frac53}=\dfrac{17/3}{-17/3}=-1$.
Ikkala burçak $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ dan katta va $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ dan kiçik, demak yiğindi $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2};\pi\right)$ da.
Bu oraliqda tangensi $-1$ bölgan burçak $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.

### 19 [D]
Arkkosinus faqat $[-1;1]$ dagi sonlar uçun aniqlangan:
$-1\le\dfrac{2x+1}{3}\le1\Rightarrow-3\le2x+1\le3\Rightarrow-2\le x\le1$.

Javob: $[-2; 1]$.

### 20 [C]
Şart: $-1\le\dfrac{x+1}{x-3}\le1$.
$\dfrac{x+1}{x-3}-1=\dfrac{4}{x-3}\le0\Rightarrow x<3$.
$\dfrac{x+1}{x-3}+1=\dfrac{2x-2}{x-3}\ge0\Rightarrow x\le1$ yoki $x>3$.
Kesişma: $x\le1$.

Javob: $(-\infty; 1]$.

### 21 [A]
$a=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $b=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $c=\arcsin\dfrac45<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}<\dfrac{2\pi}{3}<\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, demak $c<a<b$.

Javob: $c<a<b$.

### 22 [A]

Javob: $[-2; 2]$.

### 23 [C]

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4}; \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$.

### 24 [C]

Javob: $-50^\circ$.

### 25 [B]

Demak, $\arccos(\cos5)=2\pi-5$.

Javob: $2\pi-5$.

### 26 [D]
$\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{38\pi}{9}=\operatorname{ctg}\left(4\pi+\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\right)=\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{2\pi}{9}=\operatorname{tg}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\right)=\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{5\pi}{18}$.
$\dfrac{5\pi}{18}\in\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, demak javob $\dfrac{5\pi}{18}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{18}$.

### 27 [C]
$\sin\dfrac{21\pi}{10}=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{10}$, $\cos\dfrac{41\pi}{10}=\cos\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.
$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(\sin\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\cos\dfrac{\pi}{10}\right)=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{20}$.
$\dfrac{7\pi}{20}\in\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, demak javob $\dfrac{7\pi}{20}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7\pi}{20}$.

### 28 [A]
$\arccos x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\arcsin x$ ni qöyamiz: $\arcsin x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.
$\arcsin x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 29 [D]
Formula: $\operatorname{arctg}a+\operatorname{arctg}b=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac{a+b}{1-ab}$ ($ab<1$, $a,b>0$).
$\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac13+\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac15=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac{8/15}{14/15}=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac47$.
$\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac17+\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac18=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac{15/56}{55/56}=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac3{11}$.
$\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac47+\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac3{11}=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac{65/77}{65/77}=\operatorname{arctg}1=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

### 30 [C]
Arksinus $[-1;1]$ da ösuvçi, şuning uçun: $2x-1<x$, $-1\le2x-1\le1$, $-1\le x\le1$.
Bulardan $x<1$ va $0\le x\le1$, ya'ni $0\le x<1$.

Javob: $[0; 1)$.

### 14 [B]

### 15 [B]

### 26 [D]
$\cos\dfrac{23\pi}{5}=\cos\left(4\pi+\dfrac{3\pi}{5}\right)=\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{5}=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{3\pi}{5}\right)=\sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{10}\right)$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{10}\in\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, demak javob $-\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.

### 27 [C]
Yiğindi sinusi formulasi: qavs içi $\sin\left(\dfrac{19\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi}{5}\right)=\sin\dfrac{21\pi}{10}=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{10}\in\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, demak javob $\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{10}$.

### 28 [A]
$\arccos x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\arcsin x$: $3\arcsin x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\arcsin x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
$4\arcsin x=\pi$, $\arcsin x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $x=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 29 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}\left(2\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac13\right)=\dfrac{2/3}{1-1/9}=\dfrac34$.
$\operatorname{tg}\left(\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac34+\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac17\right)=\dfrac{\frac34+\frac17}{1-\frac3{28}}=\dfrac{25/28}{25/28}=1$.
Burçak I çorakda, demak $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

### 30 [A]
$t=x^2-\dfrac32$ deymiz va $\arccos t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\arcsin t$ ni qöyamiz: $3\arcsin t+\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\pi$.
$\arcsin t=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $t=\dfrac12$.
$x^2-\dfrac32=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x^2=2\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt2$.

Javob: $\pm\sqrt2$.
