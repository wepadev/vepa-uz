# 86. Aniq integral va egri çiziqli trapetsiya yuzi

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Nyuton–Leybnits formulasi: $\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x)dx=F(b)-F(a)$.
$F(x)=x^4+x^3-x^2+x$.
$F(2)=16+8-4+2=22$, $F(-1)=1-1-1-1=-2$; $22-(-2)=24$.

Javob: $24$.

### 2 [B]
$x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$, demak integral ostida $x+3$.
$\left[\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x\right]_0^2=2+6=8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 3 [C]
$\displaystyle\int8(2x-1)^3dx=8\cdot\dfrac{(2x-1)^4}{2\cdot4}=(2x-1)^4$.
$\left[(2x-1)^4\right]_1^2=81-1=80$.

Javob: $80$.

### 4 [A]
$\displaystyle\int6(3x+1)^{-2}dx=6\cdot\dfrac{(3x+1)^{-1}}{3\cdot(-1)}=-\dfrac{2}{3x+1}$.
$\left[-\dfrac{2}{3x+1}\right]_0^1=-\dfrac24+2=\dfrac32$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

### 5 [A]
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac32}dx=\dfrac25x^{\frac52}$.
$\dfrac25\left(4^{\frac52}-1\right)=\dfrac25(32-1)=\dfrac{62}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{62}{5}$.

### 6 [C]
$\displaystyle\int4(2x+2)^{\frac13}dx=4\cdot\dfrac{(2x+2)^{\frac43}}{2\cdot\frac43}=\dfrac32(2x+2)^{\frac43}$.
$x=3$ da $8^{\frac43}=16$, $x=-1$ da $0$: $\dfrac32\cdot16=24$.

Javob: $24$.

### 7 [C]
$x\sqrt x=x^{\frac32}$, $\displaystyle\int_0^4x^{\frac32}dx=\dfrac25\cdot4^{\frac52}=\dfrac25\cdot32=\dfrac{64}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{64}{5}$.

### 8 [B]
$\displaystyle\int e^{4x}dx=\dfrac{e^{4x}}{4}$.
$\left[\dfrac{e^{4x}}{4}\right]_0^1=\dfrac{e^4-1}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{e^{4}-1}{4}$.

### 9 [D]
$\displaystyle\int2^xdx=\dfrac{2^x}{\ln2}$.
$\dfrac{4-1}{\ln2}=\dfrac{3}{\ln2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{\ln 2}$.

### 10 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{2x+1}=\dfrac12\ln|2x+1|$.
$\dfrac12(\ln9-\ln3)=\dfrac12\ln3$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}\ln 3$.

### 11 [C]
$\dfrac{2x+1}{x+1}=2-\dfrac{1}{x+1}$.
$\left[2x-\ln(x+1)\right]_1^3=(6-\ln4)-(2-\ln2)=4-\ln2$.

Javob: $4-\ln 2$.

### 12 [A]
$\displaystyle\int6\sin x\,dx=-6\cos x$.
$-6\left(\cos\dfrac\pi3-\cos0\right)=-6\left(\dfrac12-1\right)=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 13 [A]
$\displaystyle\int6\cos4x\,dx=\dfrac64\sin4x=\dfrac32\sin4x$.
$\dfrac32\left(\sin\dfrac\pi2-\sin0\right)=\dfrac32$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

### 14 [B]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2}{\cos^2x}dx=2\operatorname{tg}x$.
$2\left(\operatorname{tg}\dfrac\pi3-\operatorname{tg}\dfrac\pi4\right)=2(\sqrt3-1)=2\sqrt3-2$.

Javob: $2\sqrt{3}-2$.

### 15 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{1+x^2}=\operatorname{arctg}x$.
$\operatorname{arctg}\sqrt3-\operatorname{arctg}(-1)=\dfrac\pi3+\dfrac\pi4=\dfrac{7\pi}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7\pi}{12}$.

### 16 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x$.
$\arcsin\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}-\arcsin\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac\pi4+\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.

### 17 [D]
$y=|x-1|$ grafigi ostidagi yuza ikki uçburçakdan iborat: $[-1;1]$ da asosi $2$, balandligi $2$; $[1;3]$ da ham xuddi şunday.
$\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2+\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 18 [B]
Har birini alohida hisoblaymiz (uçburçaklar yuzi orqali).
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^4|x|dx=\dfrac12+8=\dfrac{17}{2}$.
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^4|x-2|dx=\dfrac{9}{2}+2=\dfrac{13}{2}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{17}{2}+\dfrac{13}{2}=15$.

Javob: $15$.

### 19 [C]
$[0;\frac\pi2]$ da $\cos x\ge0$, $[\frac\pi2;\frac{3\pi}{2}]$ da $\cos x\le0$.
$\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac\pi2}\cos x\,dx=1$, $\displaystyle\int_{\frac\pi2}^{\frac{3\pi}{2}}(-\cos x)dx=-(-1-1)=2$.
Jami: $3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 20 [A]
$x^2+1>0$, demak yuza $S=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^2(x^2+1)dx$.
$\left[\dfrac{x^3}{3}+x\right]_{-1}^2=\left(\dfrac83+2\right)-\left(-\dfrac13-1\right)=\dfrac{14}{3}+\dfrac43=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 21 [C]
$S=\displaystyle\int_{\frac13}^3\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln3-\ln\dfrac13=2\ln3$.

Javob: $2\ln 3$.

### 22 [D]
$[-1;0]$ da $x^3\le0$, şuning uçun bu qismni minus bilan olamiz.
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^0(-x^3)dx=\dfrac14$, $\displaystyle\int_0^2x^3dx=4$.
$S=\dfrac14+4=\dfrac{17}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{17}{4}$.

### 23 [C]
Kesişiş nuqtalari: $x^2-1=2x+2 \Rightarrow x^2-2x-3=0 \Rightarrow x=-1$, $x=3$.
Çizmadan körinadiki, bu oraliqda töğri çiziq parabola ustida.
$S=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^3(2x+2-x^2+1)dx=\left[-\dfrac{x^3}{3}+x^2+3x\right]_{-1}^3=9-\left(-\dfrac53\right)=\dfrac{32}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{32}{3}$.

### 24 [B]
$\sin2x=\cos2x$ bölganda $x=\dfrac\pi8$ va $x=\dfrac{5\pi}{8}$; orasida $\sin2x\ge\cos2x$.
$S=\displaystyle\int(\sin2x-\cos2x)dx=\left[-\dfrac{\cos2x+\sin2x}{2}\right]_{\frac\pi8}^{\frac{5\pi}{8}}$.
$=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}-\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=\sqrt2$.

Javob: $\sqrt{2}$.

### 25 [C]
Çizmadan körinadiki, $S_1$ $Ox$ öqidan pastda ($-2<x<1$), $S_2$ yuqorida ($1<x<3$).
Pastdagi soha integralga minus işora bilan kiradi:
$\displaystyle\int_{-2}^3f(x)dx=-S_1+S_2=-5+7=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 26 [A]
$\displaystyle\int_a^b4\,dx=4(b-a)=8 \Rightarrow b-a=2$.
$a+b=10$ bilan qöşamiz: $2b=12 \Rightarrow b=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 27 [B]
Integral çiziqli: $\displaystyle\int(3f+g)dx=3\int f\,dx+\int g\,dx$.
$3\cdot5+3=18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 28 [D]
Toq funksiya uçun $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^1f(x)dx=0$.
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^4f\,dx=\int_{-1}^1f\,dx+\int_1^4f\,dx=0+\int_1^4f\,dx=-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 29 [D]
$\dfrac{2x+3}{x+2}=2-\dfrac{1}{x+2}$.
$\left[2x-\ln(x+2)\right]_0^2=4-\ln4+\ln2=4-\ln2$.
$a=4$, $b=1$, $a+b=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 30 [B]
$f(x)=3x^2$ ($x<0$) va $f(x)=x-2$ ($x\ge0$), integralni ikkiga bölamiz.
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^0x\cdot3x^2dx=\left[\dfrac{3x^4}{4}\right]_{-1}^0=-\dfrac34$.
$\displaystyle\int_0^3(x^2-2x)dx=\left[\dfrac{x^3}{3}-x^2\right]_0^3=9-9=0$.
Jami: $-\dfrac34$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{4}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [A]
$F(x)=\dfrac{x^4}{4}-\dfrac{3x^2}{2}+2x$.
$F(3)=\dfrac{81}{4}-\dfrac{27}{2}+6=\dfrac{51}{4}$, $F(1)=\dfrac14-\dfrac32+2=\dfrac34$.
$\dfrac{51}{4}-\dfrac34=12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 2 [A]
$2x^2-x-1=(2x+1)(x-1)$, integral ostida $2x+1$.
$\left[x^2+x\right]_2^4=20-6=14$.

Javob: $14$.

### 3 [D]
$\displaystyle\int6(x+3)^5dx=(x+3)^6$.
$\left[(x+3)^6\right]_{-2}^{-1}=64-1=63$.

Javob: $63$.

### 4 [B]
$\displaystyle\int2(x+1)^{-3}dx=-(x+1)^{-2}$.
$\left[-\dfrac{1}{(x+1)^2}\right]_1^3=-\dfrac{1}{16}+\dfrac14=\dfrac{3}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{16}$.

### 5 [B]
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac13}dx=\dfrac34x^{\frac43}$.
$27^{\frac43}=81$, demak $\dfrac34\cdot81=\dfrac{243}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{243}{4}$.

### 6 [A]
$\displaystyle\int6(3x+1)^{\frac12}dx=6\cdot\dfrac{(3x+1)^{\frac32}}{3\cdot\frac32}=\dfrac43(3x+1)^{\frac32}$.
$\dfrac43\left(16^{\frac32}-1\right)=\dfrac43(64-1)=84$.

Javob: $84$.

### 7 [C]
$x^2\sqrt[4]{x}=x^{\frac94}$, $\displaystyle\int_0^1x^{\frac94}dx=\dfrac{4}{13}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{13}$.

### 8 [A]
$\displaystyle\int e^{2x+2}dx=\dfrac{e^{2x+2}}{2}$.
$\dfrac{e^2-e^0}{2}=\dfrac{e^2-1}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{e^{2}-1}{2}$.

### 9 [D]
$5^{2x}=25^x$, $\displaystyle\int25^xdx=\dfrac{25^x}{\ln25}$.
$\dfrac{25-1}{2\ln5}=\dfrac{12}{\ln5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{12}{\ln 5}$.

### 10 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2\,dx}{4x+1}=\dfrac24\ln|4x+1|=\dfrac12\ln|4x+1|$.
$\dfrac12(\ln5-\ln1)=\dfrac12\ln5$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}\ln 5$.

### 11 [C]
$\dfrac{x+4}{x+1}=1+\dfrac{3}{x+1}$.
$\left[x+3\ln(x+1)\right]_0^3=3+3\ln4$.

Javob: $3+3\ln 4$.

### 12 [B]
$\displaystyle\int2\cos x\,dx=2\sin x$.
$2\left(1-\dfrac12\right)=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 13 [D]
$\displaystyle\int3\sin3x\,dx=-\cos3x$.
$-\left(\cos\pi-\cos\dfrac\pi2\right)=-(-1-0)=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 14 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{4}{\sin^2x}dx=-4\operatorname{ctg}x$.
$-4\left(\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac\pi3-\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac\pi6\right)=-4\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}-\sqrt3\right)=\dfrac{8\sqrt3}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{8\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

### 15 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{4+x^2}=\dfrac12\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac x2$.
$\dfrac12(\operatorname{arctg}1-0)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac\pi4=\dfrac\pi8$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$.

### 16 [B]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{4-x^2}}=\arcsin\dfrac x2$.
$\arcsin\dfrac12-0=\dfrac\pi6$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

### 17 [D]
$|x+1|$ ning noli $x=-1$: ikki uçburçak.
$[-2;-1]$ da $\dfrac12\cdot1\cdot1=\dfrac12$, $[-1;4]$ da $\dfrac12\cdot5\cdot5=\dfrac{25}{2}$.
Jami: $13$.

Javob: $13$.

### 18 [C]
$\displaystyle\int_0^6|x-2|dx=\dfrac12\cdot2^2+\dfrac12\cdot4^2=2+8=10$.
$\displaystyle\int_0^6|x-5|dx=\dfrac12\cdot5^2+\dfrac12\cdot1^2=13$.
Yiğindi: $23$.

Javob: $23$.

### 19 [C]
$|\cos2x|$ ning $[0;\pi]$ dagi grafigi $4$ ta bir xil yarim tölqindan iborat, har biri $\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac\pi4}\cos2x\,dx=\dfrac12$.
Jami: $4\cdot\dfrac12=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 20 [B]
$-x^2+4x=0$ da $x=0$ va $x=4$; orasida funksiya musbat.
$S=\displaystyle\int_0^4(4x-x^2)dx=\left[2x^2-\dfrac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^4=32-\dfrac{64}{3}=\dfrac{32}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{32}{3}$.

### 21 [D]
$[2;6]$ da $-\dfrac1x<0$, yuza modul bilan olinadi:
$S=\displaystyle\int_2^6\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln6-\ln2=\ln3$.

Javob: $\ln 3$.

### 22 [C]
$x^2-4$ $[0;2]$ da manfiy, $[2;3]$ da musbat.
$\displaystyle\int_0^2(4-x^2)dx=8-\dfrac83=\dfrac{16}{3}$, $\displaystyle\int_2^3(x^2-4)dx=\dfrac{19}{3}-4=\dfrac73$.
$S=\dfrac{16}{3}+\dfrac73=\dfrac{23}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{23}{3}$.

### 23 [A]
Kesişiş: $x^2=2x \Rightarrow x=0$, $x=2$; orasida töğri çiziq yuqorida.
$S=\displaystyle\int_0^2(2x-x^2)dx=4-\dfrac83=\dfrac43$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

### 24 [A]
$\sin\dfrac x2=\cos\dfrac x2$ bölganda $x=\dfrac\pi2$ va $x=\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$; orasida $\sin\dfrac x2\ge\cos\dfrac x2$.
$S=\left[-2\cos\dfrac x2-2\sin\dfrac x2\right]_{\frac\pi2}^{\frac{5\pi}{2}}=2\sqrt2-(-2\sqrt2)=4\sqrt2$.

Javob: $4\sqrt{2}$.

### 25 [C]
Çizmadan körinadiki, $S_1$ $Ox$ dan yuqorida ($-4<x<-1$), $S_2$ pastda ($-1<x<2$).
$\displaystyle\int_{-4}^2f(x)dx=S_1-S_2=3-8=-5$.

Javob: $-5$.

### 26 [C]
$5(b-a)=20 \Rightarrow b-a=4$; $a+b=2$.
Bundan $b=3$, $a=-1$, $ab=-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 27 [D]
$\displaystyle\int(f+2g)dx=-2+2\cdot6=10$.

Javob: $10$.

### 28 [B]
Juft funksiya uçun $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^0f\,dx=\int_0^2f\,dx=3$.
$\displaystyle\int_{-2}^3f\,dx=3+3+\int_2^3f\,dx=10 \Rightarrow \int_2^3f\,dx=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 29 [C]
$\dfrac{4x+1}{x+1}=4-\dfrac{3}{x+1}$.
$\left[4x-3\ln(x+1)\right]_1^3=8-3(\ln4-\ln2)=8-3\ln2$.
$a=8$, $b=3$, $a+b=11$.

Javob: $11$.

### 30 [B]
$\displaystyle\int_{-1}^0x\cdot x^2dx=\left[\dfrac{x^4}{4}\right]_{-1}^0=-\dfrac14$.
$\displaystyle\int_0^2x(3-x)dx=\left[\dfrac{3x^2}{2}-\dfrac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2=6-\dfrac83=\dfrac{10}{3}$.
Jami: $\dfrac{10}{3}-\dfrac14=\dfrac{37}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{37}{12}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [A]
$F(x)=\dfrac{x^4}{2}+2x^3-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x$.
$F(1)=\dfrac12+2-\dfrac12+3=5$, $F(-2)=8-16-2-6=-16$.
$5-(-16)=21$.

Javob: $21$.

### 2 [D]
$\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}=x+3$.
$\left[\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x\right]_{-1}^2=(2+6)-\left(\dfrac12-3\right)=\dfrac{21}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{21}{2}$.

### 3 [D]
$\displaystyle\int4(3x-1)^3dx=4\cdot\dfrac{(3x-1)^4}{12}=\dfrac{(3x-1)^4}{3}$.
$\dfrac{16-1}{3}=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 4 [B]
$\displaystyle\int3(x-1)^{-4}dx=-(x-1)^{-3}$.
$\left[-\dfrac{1}{(x-1)^3}\right]_2^3=-\dfrac18+1=\dfrac78$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{8}$.

### 5 [D]
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac34}dx=\dfrac47x^{\frac74}$, $16^{\frac74}=128$.
$\dfrac47\cdot128=\dfrac{512}{7}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{512}{7}$.

### 6 [B]
$\displaystyle\int5(x+2)^{\frac23}dx=5\cdot\dfrac35(x+2)^{\frac53}=3(x+2)^{\frac53}$.
$3\left(8^{\frac53}-1^{\frac53}\right)=3(32-1)=93$.

Javob: $93$.

### 7 [C]
$x\sqrt[3]{x}=x^{\frac43}$, $\displaystyle\int_0^8x^{\frac43}dx=\dfrac37\cdot8^{\frac73}=\dfrac37\cdot128=\dfrac{384}{7}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{384}{7}$.

### 8 [B]
$\displaystyle\int e^{-x}dx=-e^{-x}$.
$\left[-e^{-x}\right]_{-2}^0=-1+e^2=e^2-1$.

Javob: $e^{2}-1$.

### 9 [D]

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{\ln 4}$.

### 10 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{5-2x}=-\dfrac12\ln|5-2x|$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\dfrac{7}{3}$.

### 11 [C]

$\left[3x+2\ln(x-1)\right]_2^4=6+2\ln3$.

Javob: $6+2\ln 3$.

### 12 [C]

$-3\left(\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4}-\cos\dfrac\pi4\right)=-3\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=3\sqrt2$.

Javob: $3\sqrt{2}$.

### 13 [A]

$-4\left(\cos\dfrac\pi2-\cos0\right)=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 14 [B]

Javob: $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 15 [B]
$2\left(\operatorname{arctg}\sqrt3-\operatorname{arctg}(-\sqrt3)\right)=2\left(\dfrac\pi3+\dfrac\pi3\right)=\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.

### 16 [C]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{4-x^2}}=\arcsin\dfrac x2$.
$\arcsin\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac\pi4$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

### 17 [A]

Javob: $20$.

### 18 [D]

$\displaystyle\int_{-2}^3|x-2|dx=\dfrac{16}{2}+\dfrac12=\dfrac{17}{2}$.

### 19 [C]
