# 11. Davriy kasrlar

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
Sof davriy kasrda davr suratga, maxrajga davrdagi raqamlar sonicha $9$ yoziladi:
$1{,}(27) = 1 + \dfrac{27}{99} = 1 + \dfrac{3}{11} = \dfrac{14}{11}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{14}{11}$.

### 2 [B]
Aralaş davriy kasr: suratga verguldan keyingi sondan davrgaça bölgan qismni ayiramiz, maxrajga davr uzunligiça $9$ va davrgaça raqamlar soniça $0$ yozamiz:
$0{,}5(18) = \dfrac{518 - 5}{990} = \dfrac{513}{990} = \dfrac{57}{110}$.
$2{,}5(18) = 2 + \dfrac{57}{110} = \dfrac{277}{110}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{277}{110}$.

### 3 [D]
$7$ ni $18$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{7}{18} = 0{,}3888\ldots$, $8$ raqami takrorlanadi.
Demak, $3\dfrac{7}{18} = 3{,}3(8)$.

Javob: $3{,}3(8)$.

### 4 [C]
Sonlarni bir neçta xona bilan yozib solişiramiz:
$a = 0{,}567567\ldots$, $b = 0{,}567777\ldots$, $c = 0{,}567767\ldots$
Birinçi farq beşinçi xonada: $b$ da $7$, $c$ da $6$, $a$ da $5$.
Demak, $b > c > a$.

Javob: $b>c>a$.

### 5 [C]
$0{,}2(7) = \dfrac{27 - 2}{90} = \dfrac{5}{18}$. Surat: $\dfrac{5}{18}\cdot(8{,}4 + 6{,}6) = \dfrac{5}{18}\cdot15 = \dfrac{25}{6}$.
Maxraj: $19{,}2\cdot\dfrac{5}{6} = 16$, $4{,}2:\dfrac{6}{5} = 3{,}5$, $16 - 3{,}5 = 12{,}5$.
$\dfrac{25}{6}:12{,}5 = \dfrac{1}{3}$; $\dfrac{1}{3} + 1\dfrac{2}{3} = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 6 [A]
$0{,}(9) = 1$, demak $k{,}(9) = k + 1$. Surat: $1 + 2 + \ldots + 7 = 28$.
$0{,}(06) = \dfrac{6}{99} = \dfrac{2}{33}$; maxraj: $5\cdot\dfrac{2}{33}:\dfrac{20}{33} = \dfrac{10}{33}\cdot\dfrac{33}{20} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
$28:\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0{,}25 = 56\cdot0{,}25 = 14$.

Javob: $14$.

### 7 [D]
Surat: $\dfrac{0{,}75}{0{,}25} + \dfrac{0{,}56}{0{,}14} = 3 + 4 = 7$.
$\dfrac{0{,}(36)}{0{,}(12)} = \dfrac{36/99}{12/99} = 3$. $0{,}2(7) = \dfrac{5}{18}$, $0{,}(925) = \dfrac{925}{999} = \dfrac{25}{27}$, $\dfrac{5}{18}:\dfrac{25}{27} = \dfrac{3}{10}$, $\dfrac{3}{10}:0{,}1 = 3$.
Maxraj: $\dfrac{1}{6}(3 + 3) = 1$. Natija: $7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 8 [B]
$\dfrac{1}{13} = 0{,}(076923)$, davr uzunligi $6$.
$2026 = 6\cdot337 + 4$, demak $2026$-raqam davrning $4$-raqamiga teng: $0, 7, 6, 9$ — bu $9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 9 [C]
$\dfrac{8}{27} = 8\cdot\dfrac{1}{27} = 8\cdot0{,}(037)$.
Davrni $8$ ga köpaytiramiz: $037\cdot8 = 296$ (uç xonali bölib qoladi).
Demak, $\dfrac{8}{27} = 0{,}(296)$.

Javob: $0{,}(296)$.

### 10 [A]
$132 = 2^2\cdot3\cdot11$. $2^2$ köpaytuvçi davrgaça $2$ ta raqam beradi, davr uzunligini $3\cdot11 = 33$ belgilaydi.
$10^k - 1$ soni $33$ ga bölinadigan eng kiçik $k$: $10^1 - 1 = 9$ — yöq, $10^2 - 1 = 99 = 3\cdot33$ — ha.
Davr uzunligi $2$.

Javob: $2$.

## 2-test

### 1 [D]
Sof davriy kasr: davr suratga, maxrajga $99$:
$3{,}(45) = 3 + \dfrac{45}{99} = 3 + \dfrac{5}{11} = \dfrac{38}{11}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{38}{11}$.

### 2 [A]

$0{,}4(27) = \dfrac{427 - 4}{990} = \dfrac{423}{990} = \dfrac{47}{110}$.
$3{,}4(27) = 3 + \dfrac{47}{110} = \dfrac{377}{110}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{377}{110}$.

### 3 [C]
$11$ ni $30$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{11}{30} = 0{,}3666\ldots$, $6$ takrorlanadi.
Demak, $2\dfrac{11}{30} = 2{,}3(6)$.

Javob: $2{,}3(6)$.

### 4 [A]
Sonlarni bir neçta xona bilan yozib solişiramiz:
$a = 0{,}312212\ldots$, $b = 0{,}312312\ldots$, $c = 0{,}312222\ldots$
Törtinçi xonada $b$ da $3$, $a$ va $c$ da $2$; beşinçi xonada $c$ da $2$, $a$ da $1$.
Demak, $b > c > a$.

Javob: $b>c>a$.

### 5 [B]
$0{,}4(1) = \dfrac{41 - 4}{90} = \dfrac{37}{90}$. Surat: $\dfrac{37}{90}\cdot(6{,}75 + 3{,}25) = \dfrac{37}{9}$.
Maxraj: $\dfrac{74}{5}\cdot\dfrac{37}{45} - \dfrac{24}{5}\cdot\dfrac{37}{45} = \dfrac{37}{45}\cdot\dfrac{50}{5} = \dfrac{74}{9}$ (bunda $4{,}8:1\dfrac{8}{37} = 4{,}8\cdot\dfrac{37}{45}$).
$\dfrac{37}{9}:\dfrac{74}{9} = \dfrac{1}{2}$; $\dfrac{1}{2} + 2\dfrac{1}{2} = 3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 6 [B]
$0{,}(9) = 1$, demak surat: $1 + 2 + \ldots + 8 = 36$.
$0{,}(18) = \dfrac{18}{99} = \dfrac{2}{11}$; maxraj: $4\cdot\dfrac{2}{11}:\dfrac{16}{11} = \dfrac{8}{11}\cdot\dfrac{11}{16} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
$36:\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0{,}25 = 72\cdot0{,}25 = 18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 7 [D]
Surat: $\dfrac{0{,}81}{0{,}27} + \dfrac{0{,}96}{0{,}16} = 3 + 6 = 9$.
$\dfrac{0{,}(45)}{0{,}(15)} = 3$. $0{,}3(6) = \dfrac{33}{90} = \dfrac{11}{30}$, $1{,}(2) = \dfrac{11}{9}$, $\dfrac{11}{30}:\dfrac{11}{9} = \dfrac{3}{10}$, $\dfrac{3}{10}:0{,}1 = 3$.
Maxraj: $\dfrac{1}{6}(3 + 3) = 1$. Natija: $9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 8 [C]
$\dfrac{5}{27} = 0{,}(185)$, davr uzunligi $3$.
$2027 = 3\cdot675 + 2$, demak $2027$-raqam davrning $2$-raqami: $8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 9 [A]
$\dfrac{7}{11} = 7\cdot0{,}(09)$.
Davrni $7$ ga köpaytiramiz: $09\cdot7 = 63$.
Demak, $\dfrac{7}{11} = 0{,}(63)$.

### 10 [B]

## 3-test

### 1 [C]
Sof davriy kasr: davr suratga, maxrajga $99$:
$4{,}(18) = 4 + \dfrac{18}{99} = 4 + \dfrac{2}{11} = \dfrac{46}{11}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{46}{11}$.

### 2 [B]
Aralaş davriy kasr qoidasi bö'yiça:
$0{,}7(45) = \dfrac{745 - 7}{990} = \dfrac{738}{990} = \dfrac{41}{55}$.
$1{,}7(45) = 1 + \dfrac{41}{55} = \dfrac{96}{55}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{96}{55}$.

### 3 [A]
$13$ ni $45$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{13}{45} = 0{,}2888\ldots$, $8$ takrorlanadi.
Demak, $4\dfrac{13}{45} = 4{,}2(8)$.

Javob: $4{,}2(8)$.

### 4 [D]
Sonlarni bir neçta xona bilan yozib solişiramiz:
$a = 0{,}681111\ldots$, $b = 0{,}681681\ldots$, $c = 0{,}681181\ldots$
Törtinçi xonada $b$ da $6$, qolganlarida $1$; beşinçi xonada $c$ da $8$, $a$ da $1$.
Demak, $b > c > a$.

Javob: $b>c>a$.

### 5 [C]
$0{,}1(2) = \dfrac{12 - 1}{90} = \dfrac{11}{90}$. Surat: $\dfrac{11}{90}\cdot(5{,}6 + 3{,}4) = \dfrac{11}{10}$.
Maxraj: $4{,}25:2\dfrac{8}{11} = 4{,}25\cdot\dfrac{11}{30}$, demak $\dfrac{11}{30}\cdot(13{,}25 - 4{,}25) = \dfrac{11}{30}\cdot9 = \dfrac{33}{10}$.
$\dfrac{11}{10}:\dfrac{33}{10} = \dfrac{1}{3}$; $\dfrac{1}{3} + 3\dfrac{2}{3} = 4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 6 [A]
$0{,}(9) = 1$, demak surat: $1 + 2 + \ldots + 9 = 45$.
$0{,}(15) = \dfrac{15}{99} = \dfrac{5}{33}$; maxraj: $6\cdot\dfrac{5}{33}:\dfrac{20}{11} = \dfrac{10}{11}\cdot\dfrac{11}{20} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
$45:\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0{,}3 = 90\cdot0{,}3 = 27$.

Javob: $27$.

### 7 [B]
Surat: $\dfrac{0{,}88}{0{,}22} + \dfrac{0{,}63}{0{,}09} = 4 + 7 = 11$.
$\dfrac{0{,}(56)}{0{,}(14)} = 4$. $0{,}2(4) = \dfrac{22}{90} = \dfrac{11}{45}$, $0{,}6(1) = \dfrac{55}{90} = \dfrac{11}{18}$, $\dfrac{11}{45}:\dfrac{11}{18} = \dfrac{2}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{5}:0{,}1 = 4$.
Maxraj: $\dfrac{1}{8}(4 + 4) = 1$. Natija: $11$.

Javob: $11$.

### 8 [D]

### 9 [C]

Demak, $\dfrac{13}{37} = 0{,}(351)$.

### 10 [D]
