# 59. Körsatkiçli tenglama

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
$81$ ni $3$ ning darajasi körinişida yozamiz: $81=3^4$.
$3^x=3^4\Rightarrow x=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 2 [D]
Öng tomonni soddalaştiramiz: $\sqrt[3]{(3^2)^3}=\sqrt[3]{3^6}=3^2$.
$3^x=3^2\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 3 [C]
Öng tomon: $\Bigl(\dfrac12\Bigr)^{2x+2}=\Bigl(\dfrac14\Bigr)^{x+1}$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac15\Bigr)^{x+1}=\Bigl(\dfrac14\Bigr)^{x+1}$ — asoslari har xil, daraja teng bölişi uçun $x+1=0$.
$x=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 4 [C]
$\dfrac27=\Bigl(\dfrac72\Bigr)^{-1}$, demak öng tomon $\Bigl(\dfrac72\Bigr)^{5-x}$.
$2x-1=5-x\Rightarrow3x=6\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 5 [A]
Aniqlaniş sohasi: $x\ge3$.
Köpaytma nolga teng: $\sqrt{x-3}=0\Rightarrow x=3$ yoki $3^x=27\Rightarrow x=3$.
Ikkala holda ham $x=3$.

Javob: $\{3\}$.

### 6 [B]
Aniqlaniş sohasi: $4-x>0$, ya'ni $x<4$.
$x^2-2x-8=0\Rightarrow x=4$ yoki $x=-2$; $x=4$ sohaga kirmaydi.
$3^x=27\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $\{-2,3\}$.

### 7 [D]
Şartlar: $x\ge-2$, $x^2-4\ne0$, ya'ni $x>-2$ va $x\ne2$.
$2^{x+1}=2^4\Rightarrow x=3$ — mos keladi.
$\sqrt{x+2}=0\Rightarrow x=-2$ — maxrajni nolga aylantiradi.

Javob: $\{3\}$.

### 8 [A]
$t=3^x>0$ belgilaymiz: $t^2-10t+9=0\Rightarrow t=1$ yoki $t=9$.
$3^x=1\Rightarrow x=0$; $3^x=9\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $\{0,2\}$.

### 9 [A]
$3^{4-x}=\dfrac{81}{3^x}$. $t=3^x$: $t+\dfrac{81}{t}=30\Rightarrow t^2-30t+81=0$.
$t=3$ yoki $t=27$, ya'ni $x=1$ yoki $x=3$.

Javob: $\{1,3\}$.

### 10 [D]
$\sqrt{9^x}=3^x$, $\sqrt{9^{3-x}}=3^{3-x}=\dfrac{27}{3^x}$.
$t=3^x$: $t+\dfrac{27}{t}=12\Rightarrow t^2-12t+27=0\Rightarrow t=3$ yoki $t=9$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=2$.

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 11 [B]
$t=3^{x^2-2}>0$: $t^2+t-90=0\Rightarrow t=9$ ($t=-10$ mos emas).
$x^2-2=2\Rightarrow x^2=4\Rightarrow x=\pm2$.

Javob: $\{-2,2\}$.

### 12 [A]
$5^{x-2}$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz: $5^{x-2}(5^3-4)=121\cdot5^{x-2}=3025$.
$5^{x-2}=25\Rightarrow x-2=2\Rightarrow x=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 13 [C]
$3^{2x-4}(3^2+1)=90\Rightarrow3^{2x-4}=9$.
$2x-4=2\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 14 [B]
Ikkala tomonni $7^{x-2}$ ga bölamiz: $\Bigl(\dfrac37\Bigr)^{x-2}=1$.
$x-2=0\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 15 [A]
$10^{2x+2}=5^{2x+2}\cdot2^{2x+2}$. Tenglamani $5^{2x+2}\cdot2^{x+1}$ ga bölamiz:
$2^{x+1}=5^{x+1}\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac25\Bigr)^{x+1}=1\Rightarrow x=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 16 [C]
Öxşaş hadlarni yiğamiz: $3^x+7\cdot3^x=28\cdot2^x-2^x\Rightarrow8\cdot3^x=27\cdot2^x$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^x=\dfrac{27}{8}=\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^3\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 17 [D]
$t=x^2-5$: $3^t+15\cdot3^t=82\cdot2^t-2^t\Rightarrow16\cdot3^t=81\cdot2^t$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^t=\dfrac{81}{16}\Rightarrow t=4$.
$x^2=9\Rightarrow x=\pm3$.

Javob: $\{-3,3\}$.

### 18 [C]
Bir jinsli tenglama: $4^x$ ga bölamiz va $u=\Bigl(\dfrac52\Bigr)^x$ belgilaymiz ($10^x/4^x=u$, $25^x/4^x=u^2$).
$4u^2-29u+25=0\Rightarrow u=1$ yoki $u=\dfrac{25}{4}$.
$x=0$ yoki $x=2$.

Javob: $\{0,2\}$.

### 19 [B]
$4^x$ ga bölamiz, $u=\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^x$: $36u^2-97u+36=0$.
$D=97^2-4\cdot36^2=9409-5184=4225=65^2$, $u=\dfrac{97\pm65}{72}$: $u=\dfrac94$ yoki $u=\dfrac49$.
$x=2$ yoki $x=-2$.

Javob: $\{-2,2\}$.

### 20 [B]
Guruhlaymiz: $3^x(5^x-5)-9(5^x-5)=(3^x-9)(5^x-5)=0$.
$3^x=9\Rightarrow x=2$; $5^x=5\Rightarrow x=1$.

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 21 [D]
Guruhlaymiz: $3^x(5^x-5)-3(5^x-5)=(3^x-3)(5^x-5)=0$.
Ikkala qavsdan ham $x=1$ çiqadi.

Javob: $1$.

### 22 [A]
$(3+\sqrt8)(3-\sqrt8)=9-8=1$, demak $t=(3+\sqrt8)^x$ bölsa, ikkinçi had $\dfrac1t$.
$t+\dfrac1t=34\Rightarrow t^2-34t+1=0\Rightarrow t=17\pm12\sqrt2$.
$(3+2\sqrt2)^2=17+12\sqrt2$, demak $x=2$ yoki $x=-2$.

Javob: $\{-2,2\}$.

### 23 [C]
$7^x$ ga bölamiz: $\Bigl(\dfrac37\Bigr)^x+\Bigl(\dfrac47\Bigr)^x=1$.
Çap tomon kamayuvçi funksiya, demak ildiz bitta. $x=1$ da $\dfrac37+\dfrac47=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 24 [C]
$13^x$ ga bölamiz: $\Bigl(\dfrac{5}{13}\Bigr)^x+\Bigl(\dfrac{12}{13}\Bigr)^x=1$.
Çap tomon qat'iy kamayuvçi, demak ildiz köpi bilan bitta; $x=2$ da $25+144=169$ — ildiz.

Javob: $1$.

### 25 [D]
$a^b=1$ uç holda: $a=1$; $b=0$ va $a\ne0$; $a=-1$ va $b$ juft.
$x-4=1\Rightarrow x=5$.
$x^2-x-2=0\Rightarrow x=2$ yoki $x=-1$ (asos $-2$ va $-5$, noldan farqli).
$x-4=-1\Rightarrow x=3$, daraja $9-3-2=4$ juft — mos.
Yiğindi: $5+2-1+3=9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 26 [B]
$|x-3|=1\Rightarrow x=4$ yoki $x=2$.
Daraja nol: $3x^2-15x-18=0\Rightarrow x^2-5x-6=0\Rightarrow x=6$ yoki $x=-1$ (asos $3$ va $4$, nol emas).
Yiğindi: $4+2+6-1=11$.

Javob: $11$.

### 27 [A]
Çap tomon ösuvçi, öng tomon kamayuvçi, demak ildiz köpi bilan bitta.
$x=1$: $3^1=3=4-1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 28 [D]
Kvadratga kötaramiz: $4^x=12x-8$.
$x=1$: $4=4$; $x=2$: $16=16$.
Qavariq $4^x$ va töğri çiziq köpi bilan ikki nuqtada kesişadi. Yiğindi $1+2=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 29 [C]
$9^y=3^{2y}$, demak $x=2y$.
$3\cdot2y-y=5\Rightarrow y=1$, $x=2$.
$x+y=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 30 [A]
Tenglamalarni köpaytiramiz: $2^{x+y}\cdot5^{x+y}=40\cdot250=10000$.
$10^{x+y}=10^4\Rightarrow x+y=4$.

Javob: $4$.

## 2-test

### 1 [D]
$125=5^3$, demak $x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 2 [C]
$\sqrt{(5^2)^2}=5^2$, demak $x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 3 [D]
Çap tomon: $\Bigl(\dfrac13\Bigr)^{3(x-1)}=\Bigl(\dfrac1{27}\Bigr)^{x-1}$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac1{27}\Bigr)^{x-1}=\Bigl(\dfrac15\Bigr)^{x-1}$ faqat $x-1=0$ da.

Javob: $1$.

### 4 [B]
$\dfrac53=\Bigl(\dfrac35\Bigr)^{-1}$: $3x+2=-(2x+3)$.
$5x=-5\Rightarrow x=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 5 [A]
Aniqlaniş sohasi: $x\ge-1$.
$\sqrt{x+1}=0\Rightarrow x=-1$; $5^x=0{,}2=5^{-1}\Rightarrow x=-1$.

Javob: $\{-1\}$.

### 6 [D]
Şart: $x<6$.
$x^2-8x+7=0\Rightarrow x=1$ yoki $x=7$; $x=7$ mos emas.
$2^x=4\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 7 [B]
Şart: $x\ge0$ va $x\ne0$, ya'ni $x>0$.
$\sqrt x=0$ dan çiqadigan $x=0$ mos emas.
$5^{x-1}=5^2\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $\{3\}$.

### 8 [A]
$t=5^x$: $t^2-30t+125=0\Rightarrow t=5$ yoki $t=25$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=2$.

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 9 [C]
$t=2^x$: $t+\dfrac{64}{t}=20\Rightarrow t^2-20t+64=0\Rightarrow t=4$ yoki $t=16$.
$x=2$ yoki $x=4$.

Javob: $\{2,4\}$.

### 10 [B]
$\sqrt{4^x}=2^x$, $\sqrt{4^{7-x}}=\dfrac{128}{2^x}$.
$t=2^x$: $t^2-36t+128=0\Rightarrow t=4$ yoki $t=32$.
$x=2$ yoki $x=5$.

Javob: $\{2,5\}$.

### 11 [D]
$t=2^{x^2-3}>0$: $t^2+t-4160=0\Rightarrow t=64$ ($t=-65$ mos emas).
$x^2-3=6\Rightarrow x=\pm3$.

Javob: $\{-3,3\}$.

### 12 [A]
$2^{x-1}(2^4-3)=13\cdot2^{x-1}=208\Rightarrow2^{x-1}=16$.
$x-1=4\Rightarrow x=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 13 [C]
$2^{2x-4}(2^4+1)=68\Rightarrow2^{2x-4}=4$.
$2x-4=2\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 14 [B]
$\Bigl(\dfrac49\Bigr)^{x+1}=1\Rightarrow x+1=0\Rightarrow x=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 15 [A]
$15^{2x+4}=5^{2x+4}\cdot3^{2x+4}$. Tenglamani soddalaştiramiz:
$3^{2x+4-x-7}=5^{3x+1-2x-4}\Rightarrow3^{x-3}=5^{x-3}$.
$x-3=0\Rightarrow x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 16 [C]
$7^x+3\cdot7^x=50\cdot2^x-2^x\Rightarrow4\cdot7^x=49\cdot2^x$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac72\Bigr)^x=\dfrac{49}{4}\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 17 [D]
$t=x^2-1$: $27\cdot7^t=343\cdot3^t\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac73\Bigr)^t=\dfrac{343}{27}\Rightarrow t=3$.
$x^2=4\Rightarrow x=\pm2$.

Javob: $\{-2,2\}$.

### 18 [B]
$9^x$ ga bölamiz, $u=\Bigl(\dfrac53\Bigr)^x$: $3u^2-8u+5=0$.
$u=1$ yoki $u=\dfrac53$, ya'ni $x=0$ yoki $x=1$.

Javob: $\{0,1\}$.

### 19 [D]
$9^x$ ga bölamiz, $u=\Bigl(\dfrac53\Bigr)^x$: $15u^2-34u+15=0$.
$u=\dfrac{34\pm16}{30}$: $u=\dfrac53$ yoki $u=\dfrac35$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=-1$.

Javob: $\{-1,1\}$.

### 20 [A]
$2^x(5^x-5)-8(5^x-5)=(2^x-8)(5^x-5)=0$.
$x=3$ yoki $x=1$.

Javob: $\{1,3\}$.

### 21 [C]
$2^x(7^x-343)-8(7^x-343)=(2^x-8)(7^x-343)=0$.
Ikkala qavsdan $x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 22 [C]
$(4+\sqrt{15})(4-\sqrt{15})=1$. $t=(4+\sqrt{15})^x$: $t+\dfrac1t=8$.
$t^2-8t+1=0\Rightarrow t=4\pm\sqrt{15}$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=-1$.

Javob: $\{-1,1\}$.

### 23 [A]
$9^x$ ga bölamiz: $\Bigl(\dfrac49\Bigr)^x+\Bigl(\dfrac59\Bigr)^x=1$.
Çap tomon kamayuvçi, ildiz yagona: $x=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 24 [A]
$\Bigl(\dfrac{7}{25}\Bigr)^x+\Bigl(\dfrac{24}{25}\Bigr)^x=1$ — çap tomon kamayuvçi, ildiz bittadan oşmaydi.
$x=2$: $49+576=625$ — ildiz.

Javob: $1$.

### 25 [B]
$x-5=1\Rightarrow x=6$.
$x^2-2x=0\Rightarrow x=0$ yoki $x=2$ (asos $-5$, $-3$ — nol emas).
$x-5=-1\Rightarrow x=4$, daraja $16-8=8$ juft — mos.
Yiğindi: $6+0+2+4=12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 26 [B]
$|x-5|=1\Rightarrow x=6$ yoki $x=4$.
$x^2-9x+8=0\Rightarrow x=1$ yoki $x=8$ (asos $4$ va $3$).
Yiğindi: $6+4+1+8=19$.

Javob: $19$.

### 27 [A]
Çap tomon ösuvçi, öng tomon kamayuvçi — ildiz yagona.
$x=3$: $8=11-3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 28 [D]
Kvadratga kötaramiz: $9^x=8x+1$.
$x=0$: $1=1$; $x=1$: $9=9$. Boşqa ildiz yöq (qavariq funksiya va töğri çiziq).
Yiğindi $0+1=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 29 [C]
$25^y=5^{2y}$: $x+5=2y$.
$y=2x+4$ ni qöyamiz: $x+5=4x+8\Rightarrow x=-1$, $y=2$.
$x+y=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 30 [B]
Köpaytiramiz: $15^{x+y}=1125\cdot675=759375=15^5$.
$x+y=5$.

Javob: $5$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
$49=7^2\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 2 [D]
$\sqrt[3]{(2^3)^2}=\sqrt[3]{2^6}=2^2\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 3 [B]
$\Bigl(\dfrac12\Bigr)^{2(x-3)}=\Bigl(\dfrac14\Bigr)^{x-3}=\Bigl(\dfrac17\Bigr)^{x-3}$.
Bu faqat $x-3=0$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $3$.

### 4 [A]
$\dfrac4{11}=\Bigl(\dfrac{11}4\Bigr)^{-1}$: $x-2=-(3x-6)$.
$4x=8\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 5 [C]
Şart $x\ge4$. $\sqrt{x-4}=0\Rightarrow x=4$; $2^x=16\Rightarrow x=4$.

Javob: $\{4\}$.

### 6 [D]
Şart $x<5$.
$x^2-2x-15=0\Rightarrow x=5$ yoki $x=-3$; faqat $-3$ mos.
$5^x=625\Rightarrow x=4$.

Javob: $\{-3,4\}$.

### 7 [D]
Şartlar: $x\ge2$, $x\ne\pm2$, ya'ni $x>2$.
$2^{x+3}=2^8\Rightarrow x=5$; $\sqrt{x-2}=0$ dan $x=2$ mos emas.

Javob: $\{5\}$.

### 8 [B]
$t=3^x$: $t^2-30t+81=0\Rightarrow t=3$ yoki $t=27$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=3$.

Javob: $\{1,3\}$.

### 9 [A]
$t=5^x$: $t+\dfrac{125}{t}=30\Rightarrow t^2-30t+125=0\Rightarrow t=5$ yoki $t=25$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=2$.

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 10 [C]
$\sqrt{25^x}=5^x$, $\sqrt{25^{4-x}}=\dfrac{625}{5^x}$.
$t=5^x$: $t^2-130t+625=0\Rightarrow t=5$ yoki $t=125$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=3$.

Javob: $\{1,3\}$.

### 11 [B]
$t=5^{x^2-1}>0$: $t^2+t-30=0\Rightarrow t=5$.
$x^2-1=1\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt2$.

Javob: $\{-\sqrt2,\sqrt2\}$.

### 12 [A]
$7^{x-1}(7^2-6)=43\cdot7^{x-1}=2107\Rightarrow7^{x-1}=49$.
$x=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 13 [C]
$5^{2x-3}(5^2+1)=130\Rightarrow5^{2x-3}=5$.
$2x-3=1\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 14 [B]
$\Bigl(\dfrac{5}{11}\Bigr)^{x-4}=1\Rightarrow x=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 15 [D]
$14^{2x-2}=7^{2x-2}\cdot2^{2x-2}$, tenglamani soddalaştiramiz:
$2^{x-1}=7^{x-1}\Rightarrow x-1=0$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [C]
$2\cdot5^x=5\cdot2^x\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac52\Bigr)^x=\dfrac52$.

Javob: $1$.

### 17 [D]
$t=x^2-3$: $9\cdot5^t=25\cdot3^t\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac53\Bigr)^t=\dfrac{25}{9}\Rightarrow t=2$.
$x^2=5\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt5$.

Javob: $\{-\sqrt5,\sqrt5\}$.

### 18 [B]
$9^x$ ga bölamiz, $u=\Bigl(\dfrac73\Bigr)^x$: $9u^2-58u+49=0$.
$u=\dfrac{58\pm40}{18}$: $u=\dfrac{49}{9}$ yoki $u=1$.
$x=2$ yoki $x=0$.

Javob: $\{0,2\}$.

### 19 [A]

### 20 [A]

Javob: $\{1,2\}$.

### 21 [C]

Javob: $2$.

### 22 [C]

### 23 [D]

$x=1$: $\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{7}{12}=1$.

### 24 [C]
