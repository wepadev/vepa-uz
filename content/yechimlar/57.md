# 57. Funksiya 3-qism

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
$Ox$ öqiga nisbatan simmetriyada $(x;y)$ nuqta $(x;-y)$ ga ötadi, ya'ni yangi funksiya $y=-f(x)$.
$y=-(-2x+5)=2x-5$.

Javob: $y=2x-5$.

### 2 [D]
$Oy$ öqiga nisbatan simmetriyada $x$ örniga $-x$ qöyiladi: $y=f(-x)$.
$y=-3(-x)+7=3x+7$.

Javob: $y=3x+7$.

### 3 [B]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan simmetriyada $(x;y)\to(-x;-y)$, ya'ni $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=-6x-3$, demak $y=6x+3$.

Javob: $y=6x+3$.

### 4 [B]
$y=x$ ga nisbatan simmetrik grafik — teskari funksiya grafigi.
$y=2x-5\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{y+5}{2}$, harflarni almaştiramiz: $y=\dfrac12x+\dfrac52$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac12x+\dfrac52$.

### 5 [A]
$x=a$ töğri çiziqqa nisbatan simmetriyada $x\to2a-x$, ya'ni $y=f(2a-x)$. Bu yerda $2a=-2$.
$y=-3(-2-x)+7=6+3x+7=3x+13$.

Javob: $y=3x+13$.

### 6 [C]
$y=b$ töğri çiziqqa nisbatan simmetriyada $y\to2b-y$, ya'ni yangi funksiya $y=2b-f(x)$. Bu yerda $2b=-4$.
$y=-4-(3x-5)=-3x+1$.

Javob: $y=-3x+1$.

### 7 [C]
$(a;b)$ nuqtaga nisbatan simmetriyada $(x;y)\to(2a-x;2b-y)$, ya'ni $y=2b-f(2a-x)$.
$a=-1$, $b=3$: $f(-2-x)=-2(-2-x)+5=2x+9$.
$y=6-(2x+9)=-2x-3$.

Javob: $y=-2x-3$.

### 8 [B]
$Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=-f(x)$.
$y=-(2x^2+3x-5)=-2x^2-3x+5$.

Javob: $y=-2x^2-3x+5$.

### 9 [A]
$Oy$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=f(-x)$.
$y=2(-x)^2-3(-x)+4=2x^2+3x+4$.

Javob: $y=2x^2+3x+4$.

### 10 [D]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=2x^2-3x-4$, demak $y=-2x^2+3x+4$.

Javob: $y=-2x^2+3x+4$.

### 11 [D]
$x=-2$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=f(-4-x)$.
$y=2(x+4)^2+3(-4-x)-5=2x^2+16x+32-12-3x-5=2x^2+13x+15$.

Javob: $y=2x^2+13x+15$.

### 12 [C]
$y=-1$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=-2-f(x)$.
$y=-2-(2x^2-4x+7)=-2x^2+4x-9$.

Javob: $y=-2x^2+4x-9$.

### 13 [B]
$(-1;3)$ nuqtaga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=6-f(-2-x)$.
$f(-2-x)=2(x+2)^2+3(-2-x)-4=2x^2+8x+8-6-3x-4=2x^2+5x-2$.
$y=6-(2x^2+5x-2)=-2x^2-5x+8$.

Javob: $y=-2x^2-5x+8$.

### 14 [D]
$\vec a=(m;n)$ vektor böyiça köçirişda $y=f(x-m)+n$.
$y=3(x-4)-6-1=3x-19$.

Javob: $y=3x-19$.

### 15 [D]
$\vec d=(2;-4)$: $y=f(x-2)-4$.
$f(x-2)=\dfrac{(x-2)^2-2(x-2)+5}{x+1}=\dfrac{x^2-6x+13}{x+1}$.
$y=\dfrac{x^2-6x+13-4(x+1)}{x+1}=\dfrac{x^2-10x+9}{x+1}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{x^2-10x+9}{x+1}$.

### 16 [C]
$1$ birlik çapga: $x\to x+1$; $2$ birlik pastga: $-2$.
$y=2(x+1)^2-3(x+1)+5-2=2x^2+4x+2-3x-3+3=2x^2+x+2$.

Javob: $y=2x^2+x+2$.

### 17 [A]
$1$ birlik öngga: $x\to x-1$; $4$ birlik yuqoriga: $+4$.
$y=\dfrac{3(x-1)+1}{x-3}+4=\dfrac{3x-2+4x-12}{x-3}=\dfrac{7x-14}{x-3}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{7x-14}{x-3}$.

### 18 [B]
$x$ örniga $x-3$ qöyamiz:
$f(x-3)=\dfrac{3(x-3)+5}{2(x-3)-1}=\dfrac{3x-4}{2x-7}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{3x-4}{2x-7}$.

### 19 [A]
$f$ ga $x$ örniga $g(x)$ ni qöyamiz:
$f(g(x))=-2\cdot\dfrac{3x-1}{2x+4}+5=-\dfrac{3x-1}{x+2}+5=\dfrac{-3x+1+5x+10}{x+2}=\dfrac{2x+11}{x+2}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{2x+11}{x+2}$.

### 20 [C]
$3x=t$ belgilaymiz, $x=\dfrac t3$:
$f(t)=\dfrac{\frac{2t}{3}-1}{\frac t3+5}=\dfrac{2t-3}{t+15}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{2x-3}{x+15}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [C]
$Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=-f(x)=-5x-4$.

Javob: $y=-5x-4$.

### 2 [B]
$Oy$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $y=f(-x)=2(-x)-6=-2x-6$.

Javob: $y=-2x-6$.

### 3 [D]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan: $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=-3x+8$, demak $y=3x-8$.

Javob: $y=3x-8$.

### 4 [D]
$y=x$ ga nisbatan simmetrik grafik — teskari funksiya.
$x=\dfrac{y-7}{4}$, harflarni almaştiramiz: $y=\dfrac14x-\dfrac74$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac14x-\dfrac74$.

### 5 [A]
$x=3$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(6-x)$.
$y=4(6-x)+1=-4x+25$.

Javob: $y=-4x+25$.

### 6 [A]
$y=5$ ga nisbatan: $y=10-f(x)$.
$y=10-(2x+7)=-2x+3$.

Javob: $y=-2x+3$.

### 7 [B]
$(3;-2)$ ga nisbatan: $y=-4-f(6-x)$.
$f(6-x)=4(6-x)+1=25-4x$.
$y=-4-25+4x=4x-29$.

Javob: $y=4x-29$.

### 8 [D]
$Ox$ ga nisbatan: $y=-f(x)=x^2-6x-2$.

Javob: $y=x^2-6x-2$.

### 9 [C]
$Oy$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(-x)=-2x^2-5x-1$.

Javob: $y=-2x^2-5x-1$.

### 10 [A]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan: $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=-x^2-4x+7$, demak $y=x^2+4x-7$.

Javob: $y=x^2+4x-7$.

### 11 [C]
Parabola simmetriya öqi $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}=\dfrac{6}{2}=3$.
Demak grafik $x=3$ ga nisbatan özi-özigä simmetrik va özgarmaydi. Tekşiruv: $f(6-x)=-(6-x)^2+6(6-x)+2=-x^2+6x+2$.

Javob: $y=-x^2+6x+2$.

### 12 [B]
$y=4$ ga nisbatan: $y=8-f(x)$.
$y=8-(-x^2+3x+5)=x^2-3x+3$.

Javob: $y=x^2-3x+3$.

### 13 [C]
$(3;-2)$ ga nisbatan: $y=-4-f(6-x)$.
$f(6-x)=-(6-x)^2+4(6-x)+2=-x^2+8x-10$.
$y=-4+x^2-8x+10=x^2-8x+6$.

Javob: $y=x^2-8x+6$.

### 14 [A]
$\vec a=(-3;-4)$: $y=f(x+3)-4$.
$y=5(x+3)+2-4=5x+13$.

Javob: $y=5x+13$.

### 15 [A]
$\vec d=(-2;2)$: $y=f(x+2)+2$.
$f(x+2)=\dfrac{(x+2)^2+4(x+2)+1}{x+1}=\dfrac{x^2+8x+13}{x+1}$.
$y=\dfrac{x^2+8x+13+2x+2}{x+1}=\dfrac{x^2+10x+15}{x+1}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{x^2+10x+15}{x+1}$.

### 16 [C]
$3$ birlik çapga: $x\to x+3$; $2$ birlik yuqoriga: $+2$.
$y=-(x+3)^2+6(x+3)-4+2=-x^2-6x-9+6x+18-2=-x^2+7$.

Javob: $y=-x^2+7$.

### 17 [B]
$3$ birlik öngga: $x\to x-3$; $2$ birlik pastga: $-2$.
$y=\dfrac{2x-9}{7-x}-2=\dfrac{2x-9-14+2x}{7-x}=\dfrac{4x-23}{7-x}=\dfrac{-4x+23}{x-7}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{-4x+23}{x-7}$.

### 18 [B]
$x$ örniga $x+1$: $f(x+1)=\dfrac{x+1-4}{3(x+1)+2}=\dfrac{x-3}{3x+5}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{x-3}{3x+5}$.

### 19 [D]
$f(g(x))=4\cdot\dfrac{x+5}{3x-1}+2=\dfrac{4x+20+6x-2}{3x-1}=\dfrac{10x+18}{3x-1}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{10x+18}{3x-1}$.

### 20 [A]
$4x=t$, $x=\dfrac t4$:
$f(t)=\dfrac{-\frac t4+2}{\frac{3t}{4}-2}=\dfrac{-t+8}{3t-8}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{-x+8}{3x-8}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [C]
$Ox$ ga nisbatan: $y=-f(x)=4x+3$.

Javob: $y=4x+3$.

### 2 [B]
$Oy$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(-x)=-6(-x)-1=6x-1$.

Javob: $y=6x-1$.

### 3 [B]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan: $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=2x-7$, demak $y=-2x+7$.

Javob: $y=-2x+7$.

### 4 [C]
$y=x$ ga nisbatan — teskari funksiya.
$y=-2x-9\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{y+9}{2}$, harflarni almaştiramiz.

Javob: $y=-\dfrac12x-\dfrac92$.

### 5 [A]
$x=4$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(8-x)$.
$y=-2(8-x)-5=2x-21$.

Javob: $y=2x-21$.

### 6 [D]
$y=1$ ga nisbatan: $y=2-f(x)$.
$y=2-(-5x-1)=5x+3$.

Javob: $y=5x+3$.

### 7 [B]
$(2;4)$ ga nisbatan: $y=8-f(4-x)$.
$f(4-x)=-5(4-x)-4=5x-24$.
$y=8-5x+24=-5x+32$.

Javob: $y=-5x+32$.

### 8 [A]
$Ox$ ga nisbatan: $y=-f(x)=-3x^2+2x+4$.

Javob: $y=-3x^2+2x+4$.

### 9 [A]
$Oy$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(-x)=3x^2-4x-6$.

Javob: $y=3x^2-4x-6$.

### 10 [D]
Koordinatalar boşiga nisbatan: $y=-f(-x)$.
$f(-x)=3x^2+2x+1$, demak $y=-3x^2-2x-1$.

Javob: $y=-3x^2-2x-1$.

### 11 [B]
$x=2$ ga nisbatan: $y=f(4-x)$.
$y=3(4-x)^2-2(4-x)-4=48-24x+3x^2-8+2x-4=3x^2-22x+36$.

Javob: $y=3x^2-22x+36$.

### 12 [C]
$y=2$ ga nisbatan: $y=4-f(x)$.
$y=4-3x^2-2x+6=-3x^2-2x+10$.

Javob: $y=-3x^2-2x+10$.

### 13 [A]
$(1;4)$ ga nisbatan: $y=8-f(2-x)$.
$f(2-x)=3(2-x)^2-2(2-x)-5=3x^2-10x+3$.
$y=8-3x^2+10x-3=-3x^2+10x+5$.

Javob: $y=-3x^2+10x+5$.

### 14 [D]
$\vec a=(1;6)$: $y=f(x-1)+6$.
$y=-2(x-1)-5+6=-2x+3$.

Javob: $y=-2x+3$.

### 15 [B]
$\vec d=(3;1)$: $y=f(x-3)+1$.
$f(x-3)=\dfrac{(x-3)^2-(x-3)-6}{x-7}=\dfrac{x^2-7x+6}{x-7}$.
$y=\dfrac{x^2-7x+6+x-7}{x-7}=\dfrac{x^2-6x-1}{x-7}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{x^2-6x-1}{x-7}$.

### 16 [D]
$2$ birlik çapga: $x\to x+2$; $5$ birlik pastga: $-5$.
$y=3(x+2)^2+2(x+2)+1-5=3x^2+12x+12+2x+4-4=3x^2+14x+12$.

Javob: $y=3x^2+14x+12$.

### 17 [C]
$2$ birlik öngga: $x\to x-2$; $1$ birlik yuqoriga: $+1$.
$y=\dfrac{4(x-2)+5}{3(x-2)+1}+1=\dfrac{4x-3}{3x-5}+1=\dfrac{7x-8}{3x-5}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{7x-8}{3x-5}$.

### 18 [A]
$x$ örniga $x+4$: $f(x+4)=\dfrac{-2(x+4)+7}{x+4-5}=\dfrac{-2x-1}{x-1}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{-2x-1}{x-1}$.

### 19 [D]
$f(g(x))=2\cdot\dfrac{-2x+7}{x+3}-3=\dfrac{-4x+14-3x-9}{x+3}=\dfrac{-7x+5}{x+3}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{-7x+5}{x+3}$.

### 20 [C]
$5x=t$, $x=\dfrac t5$:
$f(t)=\dfrac{\frac{3t}{5}+4}{-\frac{2t}{5}+1}=\dfrac{3t+20}{-2t+5}=\dfrac{-3t-20}{2t-5}$.

Javob: $y=\dfrac{-3x-20}{2x-5}$.
