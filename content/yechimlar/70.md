# 70. Qöşiş formulalari

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Qöşiş formulalari: $\cos(\alpha\pm\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta\mp\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.
Yiğindida $\sin\alpha\sin\beta$ hadlari qisqaradi: $2\cos\alpha\cos\beta$.

Javob: $2\cos\alpha\cos\beta$.

### 2 [A]
$\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.
$\sin\alpha\sin\beta$ ni qöşsak, $\cos\alpha\cos\beta$ qoladi.

Javob: $\cos\alpha\cos\beta$.

### 3 [B]
$\sin a\cos b+\cos a\sin b=\sin(a+b)$ formulasidan: $\sin(4x+6x)=\sin10x$.

Javob: $\sin10x$.

### 4 [A]
$\sin a\cos b-\cos a\sin b=\sin(a-b)$: $\sin(2x-7x)=\sin(-5x)=-\sin5x$.

Javob: $-\sin5x$.

### 5 [A]
Sinuslar yiğindisi formulasidan: $\sin(50^\circ+10^\circ)=\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 6 [D]
$\sin\Bigl(\dfrac{4\pi}{15}+\dfrac{\pi}{15}\Bigr)=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 7 [B]
$\sin82^\circ=\cos8^\circ$, $\cos53^\circ=\sin37^\circ$:
$\cos8^\circ\cos37^\circ-\sin8^\circ\sin37^\circ=\cos45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 8 [D]
$\sqrt2\cos\Bigl(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\sqrt2\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cos\alpha-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\sin\alpha\Bigr)=\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha$.
$\sin\alpha$ ni qöşsak: $\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $\cos\alpha$.

### 9 [A]
Surat: $\cos x\cos y+\sin x\sin y-2\sin x\sin y=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y=\cos(x+y)$.
Maxraj: $2\sin x\cos y-\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y=\sin(x+y)$.
Nisbat: $\operatorname{ctg}(x+y)$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}(x+y)$.

### 10 [B]
Surat: $\cos\alpha-2\Bigl(\dfrac12\cos\alpha-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha\Bigr)=\sqrt3\sin\alpha$.
Maxraj: $2\Bigl(\dfrac12\cos\alpha+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha\Bigr)-\sqrt3\sin\alpha=\cos\alpha$.
Nisbat: $\sqrt3\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

Javob: $\sqrt3\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

### 11 [A]
$\cos165^\circ=-\cos15^\circ$, $\cos15^\circ=\cos(45^\circ-30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$.

### 12 [D]
II çorakda $\sin\alpha=\sqrt{1-\dfrac9{25}}=\dfrac45$.
$\cos\Bigl(\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\alpha\Bigr)=\dfrac12\cos\alpha-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{3}{10}-\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{10}=\dfrac{-4\sqrt3-3}{10}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{-4\sqrt{3}-3}{10}$.

### 13 [A]
I çorakda $\cos\alpha=\dfrac45$, $\sin\beta=\dfrac{15}{17}$.
$\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac8{17}-\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac{15}{17}=\dfrac{32-45}{85}=-\dfrac{13}{85}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{13}{85}$.

### 14 [B]
Surat: $\cos64^\circ=\sin26^\circ$, $\sin206^\circ=-\sin26^\circ$, demak $4\sin26^\circ+3\sin26^\circ+2\sin26^\circ=9\sin26^\circ$.
Maxraj: $\cos(78^\circ-14^\circ)=\cos64^\circ=\sin26^\circ$.
Nisbat $9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 15 [A]
$\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}a-\operatorname{tg}b}{1+\operatorname{tg}a\operatorname{tg}b}=\operatorname{tg}(a-b)$: $\operatorname{tg}30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

### 16 [C]
$\operatorname{ctg}195^\circ=\operatorname{ctg}15^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}15^\circ}$.
$\operatorname{tg}15^\circ=\operatorname{tg}(45^\circ-30^\circ)=\dfrac{1-\frac{1}{\sqrt3}}{1+\frac1{\sqrt3}}=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3+1}=2-\sqrt3$.
$\operatorname{ctg}15^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2-\sqrt3}=2+\sqrt3$.

Javob: $2+\sqrt3$.

### 17 [B]
$\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\Bigr)=\dfrac{1+\operatorname{tg}\alpha}{1-\operatorname{tg}\alpha}=\dfrac{1+\frac25}{1-\frac25}=\dfrac{7}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{3}$.

### 18 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{3-\frac14}{1+3\cdot\frac14}=\dfrac{\frac{11}4}{\frac74}=\dfrac{11}{7}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{7}$.

### 19 [A]
III çorakda $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{15}{17}$, $\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{15}{8}$.
$\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\alpha-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha-1}{1+\operatorname{tg}\alpha}=\dfrac{\frac78}{\frac{23}8}=\dfrac{7}{23}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{23}$.

### 20 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac13$.
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{\frac12-\frac13}{1+\frac16}=\dfrac{\frac16}{\frac76}=\dfrac17$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{7}$.

### 21 [C]
$t=\operatorname{tg}\alpha$: $\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}=0{,}4 \Rightarrow 1+t=0{,}4-0{,}4t \Rightarrow 1{,}4t=-0{,}6 \Rightarrow t=-\dfrac37$.
$\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=-\dfrac73$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{7}{3}$.

### 22 [C]
$\beta=\alpha-(\alpha-\beta)$:
$\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac{\frac13-1}{1+\frac13\cdot1}=\dfrac{-\frac23}{\frac43}=-\dfrac12$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 23 [B]
$\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}=-3 \Rightarrow 1+t=-3+3t \Rightarrow t=2$, bunda $t=\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.
Surat va maxrajni $\cos\alpha$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha+1}{2}=\dfrac32$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

### 24 [D]
$\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta=2(\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta)$.
$-3\sin\alpha\sin\beta=\cos\alpha\cos\beta$, ikkala tomonni $\cos\alpha\cos\beta$ ga bölamiz: $\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta=-\dfrac13$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{3}$.

### 25 [A]
Ifoda köpaytmaga ajraladi:
$\dfrac{(\operatorname{tg}\alpha-\operatorname{tg}\beta)(\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{tg}\beta)}{(1+\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta)(1-\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta)}=\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)$.
$\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{6}\cdot\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

### 26 [D]
$2y=(x+y)-(x-y)$:
$\operatorname{tg}2y=\dfrac{3-1}{1+3\cdot1}=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 27 [C]
$\dfrac12\sin\alpha+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha=3\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha-\dfrac12\sin\alpha\Bigr)$.
$2\sin\alpha=\sqrt3\cos\alpha \Rightarrow \operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 28 [C]
$\cos18^\circ+\sin18^\circ=\sqrt2\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cos18^\circ+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\sin18^\circ\Bigr)=\sqrt2\cos(45^\circ-18^\circ)=\sqrt2\cos27^\circ$.
Nisbat $\sqrt2$.

Javob: $\sqrt{2}$.

### 29 [B]
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{1+2}{1-2}=-3$.
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)=\dfrac{-3+5}{1-(-3)\cdot5}=\dfrac{2}{16}=\dfrac18$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{8}$.

### 30 [C]
Çizmadan: balandlik $AH$ burçakni ikki qismga böladi. $\operatorname{tg}\angle CAH=\dfrac{CH}{AH}=\dfrac24=\dfrac12$, $\operatorname{tg}\angle HAB=\dfrac{HB}{AH}=\dfrac64=\dfrac32$.
$\operatorname{tg}\angle CAB=\dfrac{\frac12+\frac32}{1-\frac12\cdot\frac32}=\dfrac{2}{\frac14}=8$.

Javob: $8$.

## 2-test

### 1 [A]
$\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta$. Ayirmada $\sin\alpha\cos\beta$ qisqaradi: $2\cos\alpha\sin\beta$.

Javob: $2\cos\alpha\sin\beta$.

### 2 [B]
$\sin(-\alpha)\cos(-\beta)=-\sin\alpha\cos\beta$.
$\sin\alpha\cos\beta-\cos\alpha\sin\beta-\sin\alpha\cos\beta=-\cos\alpha\sin\beta$.

Javob: $-\cos\alpha\sin\beta$.

### 3 [A]
$\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b=\cos(a-b)$: $\cos(9x-2x)=\cos7x$.

Javob: $\cos7x$.

### 4 [D]
$\cos(3x-8x)=\cos(-5x)=\cos5x$.

Javob: $\cos5x$.

### 5 [C]
$\sin(77^\circ-32^\circ)=\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 6 [A]
$\sin\Bigl(\dfrac{\pi}{8}-\dfrac{3\pi}{8}\Bigr)=\sin\Bigl(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 7 [D]
$\cos130^\circ=-\cos50^\circ$, $\cos80^\circ=\sin10^\circ$:
$-(\cos50^\circ\cos10^\circ-\sin50^\circ\sin10^\circ)=-\cos60^\circ=-\dfrac12$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 8 [D]
$2\sin\Bigl(\alpha-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\Bigr)=2\Bigl(\dfrac12\sin\alpha-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha\Bigr)=\sin\alpha-\sqrt3\cos\alpha$.
$\sqrt3\cos\alpha$ ni qöşsak: $\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin\alpha$.

### 9 [B]
Surat: $\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y-2\cos x\sin y=\sin(x-y)$.
Maxraj: $\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y+2\sin x\sin y=\cos(x-y)$.
Nisbat: $\operatorname{tg}(x-y)$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}(x-y)$.

### 10 [A]
Surat: $2\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha+\dfrac12\sin\alpha\Bigr)-\sqrt3\cos\alpha=\sin\alpha$.
Maxraj: $2\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha-\dfrac12\cos\alpha\Bigr)-\sqrt3\sin\alpha=-\cos\alpha$.
Nisbat: $-\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

### 11 [B]
$\cos105^\circ=\cos(60^\circ+45^\circ)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2-\sqrt6}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt2-\sqrt6}{4}$.

### 12 [D]
III çorakda $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac45$.
$\sin\Bigl(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac75\Bigr)=-\dfrac{7\sqrt2}{10}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$.

### 13 [C]
II çorakda $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac5{13}$; $\sin\beta=\dfrac45$.
$\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{12}{13}\cdot\dfrac35-\Bigl(-\dfrac5{13}\Bigr)\cdot\dfrac45=\dfrac{36+20}{65}=\dfrac{56}{65}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{56}{65}$.

### 14 [D]
Surat: $\cos66^\circ=\sin24^\circ$, $\sin156^\circ=\sin24^\circ$, demak $(5-2+3)\sin24^\circ=6\sin24^\circ$.
Maxraj: $\cos(51^\circ+15^\circ)=\cos66^\circ=\sin24^\circ$.
Nisbat $6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 15 [B]
Ifoda $\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}(26^\circ+19^\circ)}=\operatorname{ctg}45^\circ=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}105^\circ=\operatorname{tg}(60^\circ+45^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{1-\sqrt3}=\dfrac{(\sqrt3+1)^2}{1-3}=-\dfrac{4+2\sqrt3}{2}=-2-\sqrt3$.

Javob: $-2-\sqrt3$.

### 17 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac13$: $\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\Bigr)=\dfrac{1+\frac13}{1-\frac13}=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 18 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{\frac23-\frac14}{1+\frac16}=\dfrac{\frac5{12}}{\frac76}=\dfrac{5}{14}$, demak $\operatorname{ctg}(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{14}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{14}{5}$.

### 19 [D]
I çorakda $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{12}{13}$, $\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{12}{5}$.
$\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{1+\frac{12}5}{1-\frac{12}5}=\dfrac{\frac{17}5}{-\frac75}=-\dfrac{17}{7}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{17}{7}$.

### 20 [B]
$\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac12$: $\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{3+\frac12}{1-\frac32}=\dfrac{\frac72}{-\frac12}=-7$.

Javob: $-7$.

### 21 [A]
$\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}=3 \Rightarrow 1+t=3-3t \Rightarrow t=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 22 [C]
$\beta=(\alpha+\beta)-\alpha$: $\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac{-1-4}{1+(-1)\cdot4}=\dfrac{-5}{-3}=\dfrac53$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{3}$.

### 23 [A]
$\dfrac{t-1}{1+t}=2 \Rightarrow t-1=2+2t \Rightarrow t=-3$, bunda $t=\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.
$\cos\alpha$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{2t+1}{t-1}=\dfrac{-5}{-4}=\dfrac54$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{4}$.

### 24 [B]
$\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta=5(\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta)$.
$6\sin\alpha\sin\beta=4\cos\alpha\cos\beta \Rightarrow \operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac23$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3}$.

### 25 [D]
Ifoda $\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)$ ga teng (köpaytuvçilarga ajratib):
$\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\cdot\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{4}=-\sqrt3$.

Javob: $-\sqrt{3}$.

### 26 [A]
$2y=(x+y)-(x-y)$: $\operatorname{tg}2y=\dfrac{5-(-2)}{1+5\cdot(-2)}=\dfrac{7}{-9}=-\dfrac79$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{7}{9}$.

### 27 [D]
$\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha+\dfrac12\sin\alpha=5\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos\alpha-\dfrac12\sin\alpha\Bigr)$.
$3\sin\alpha=2\sqrt3\cos\alpha \Rightarrow \operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.

### 28 [C]
Surat: $\cos(17^\circ+31^\circ)=\cos48^\circ$.
Maxraj: $2\Bigl(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos18^\circ-\dfrac12\sin18^\circ\Bigr)=2\cos(30^\circ+18^\circ)=2\cos48^\circ$.
Nisbat $\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 29 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}2\beta=\dfrac{2\cdot3}{1-9}=-\dfrac34$.
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+2\beta)=\dfrac{2-\frac34}{1+2\cdot\frac34}=\dfrac{\frac54}{\frac52}=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 30 [D]
Çizmadan: $\operatorname{tg}\angle CAH=\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac13$, $\operatorname{tg}\angle HAB=\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.
$\operatorname{tg}\angle CAB=\dfrac{\frac13+\frac12}{1-\frac16}=\dfrac{\frac56}{\frac56}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
$\cos(\alpha-\beta)-\cos(\alpha+\beta)=(\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta)-(\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta)=2\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.

Javob: $2\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.

### 2 [C]
$\cos(-\alpha)\cos(-\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta$.
$\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta-\cos\alpha\cos\beta=-\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.

Javob: $-\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.

### 3 [B]
$\sin(7x-3x)=\sin4x$.

Javob: $\sin4x$.

### 4 [D]
$\sin(3x-9x)=\sin(-6x)=-\sin6x$.

Javob: $-\sin6x$.

### 5 [A]
$\cos(158^\circ-38^\circ)=\cos120^\circ=-\dfrac12$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 6 [B]
$\dfrac{\pi}{10}=\dfrac{2\pi}{20}$: $\sin\Bigl(\dfrac{3\pi}{20}+\dfrac{2\pi}{20}\Bigr)=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 7 [A]
$\sin72^\circ=\cos18^\circ$, $\sin102^\circ=\cos12^\circ$, $\cos108^\circ=-\sin18^\circ$, $\sin168^\circ=\sin12^\circ$.
$\cos18^\circ\cos12^\circ-\sin18^\circ\sin12^\circ=\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 8 [C]
$\cos\Bigl(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}-\alpha\Bigr)=-\dfrac12\cos\alpha+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha$.
$\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha$ ni ayirsak: $-\dfrac12\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $-\dfrac12\cos\alpha$.

### 9 [A]
Surat: $\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y+2\sin x\sin y=\cos(x-y)$.
Maxraj: $\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y-2\cos x\sin y=\sin(x-y)$.
Nisbat: $\operatorname{ctg}(x-y)$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}(x-y)$.

### 10 [B]
Surat: $2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)-\sqrt2\cos\alpha=\sqrt2\sin\alpha$.
Maxraj: $2\Bigl(\dfrac12\cos\alpha+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha\Bigr)-\sqrt3\sin\alpha=\cos\alpha$.
Nisbat: $\sqrt2\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

Javob: $\sqrt2\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

### 11 [D]
$\sin195^\circ=-\sin15^\circ$, $\sin15^\circ=\sin(45^\circ-30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{4}$.
Demak, $\sin195^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2-\sqrt6}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt2-\sqrt6}{4}$.

### 12 [D]
IV çorakda $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{12}{13}$.
$\cos\Bigl(\alpha-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\Bigr)=\dfrac12\cos\alpha+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin\alpha=\dfrac{5}{26}-\dfrac{12\sqrt3}{26}=\dfrac{5-12\sqrt3}{26}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5-12\sqrt{3}}{26}$.

### 13 [C]
$\sin\alpha=\dfrac{15}{17}$; IV çorakda $\cos\beta=\dfrac35$.
$\cos(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac8{17}\cdot\dfrac35+\dfrac{15}{17}\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac45\Bigr)=\dfrac{24-60}{85}=-\dfrac{36}{85}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{36}{85}$.

### 14 [A]
Surat: $\sin143^\circ=\sin37^\circ$, $\cos127^\circ=-\sin37^\circ$, demak $(2+5+1)\sin37^\circ=8\sin37^\circ$.
Maxraj: $\cos25^\circ=\sin65^\circ$, $\cos78^\circ=\sin12^\circ$, demak $\cos(65^\circ-12^\circ)=\cos53^\circ=\sin37^\circ$.
Nisbat $8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 15 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}(100^\circ+20^\circ)=\operatorname{tg}120^\circ=-\sqrt3$.

Javob: $-\sqrt{3}$.

### 16 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}75^\circ=\operatorname{tg}15^\circ=\operatorname{tg}(45^\circ-30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3+1}=\dfrac{(\sqrt3-1)^2}{2}=2-\sqrt3$.

Javob: $2-\sqrt3$.

### 17 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac25$: $\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\alpha-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{\frac25-1}{1+\frac25}=\dfrac{-\frac35}{\frac75}=-\dfrac37$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{7}$.

### 18 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{\frac13+\frac25}{1-\frac2{15}}=\dfrac{\frac{11}{15}}{\frac{13}{15}}=\dfrac{11}{13}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{13}$.

### 19 [C]
II çorakda $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac35$, $\operatorname{tg}\alpha=-\dfrac43$.
$\operatorname{tg}\Bigl(\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{1-\frac43}{1+\frac43}=\dfrac{-\frac13}{\frac73}=-\dfrac17$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{7}$.

### 20 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac14$: $\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{\frac14+\frac23}{1-\frac14\cdot\frac23}=\dfrac{\frac{11}{12}}{\frac56}=\dfrac{11}{10}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{10}$.

### 21 [B]
$\dfrac{1-t}{1+t}=\dfrac14 \Rightarrow 4-4t=1+t \Rightarrow t=\dfrac35$, bunda $t=\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.
$\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac53$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{3}$.

### 22 [D]
$\beta=\alpha-(\alpha-\beta)$: $\operatorname{tg}\beta=\dfrac{\frac12-3}{1+\frac32}=\dfrac{-\frac52}{\frac52}=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 23 [A]
$\dfrac{1+t}{1-t}=\dfrac12 \Rightarrow 2+2t=1-t \Rightarrow t=-\dfrac13$.
$\cos\alpha$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{t-2}{0{,}5}=2\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac73\Bigr)=-\dfrac{14}{3}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{14}{3}$.

### 24 [C]
$3(\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta)=-(\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta)$.
$4\cos\alpha\cos\beta=2\sin\alpha\sin\beta \Rightarrow \operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 25 [B]
Ifoda $\operatorname{tg}(\alpha+\beta)\operatorname{tg}(\alpha-\beta)$ ga teng:
$\operatorname{tg}75^\circ\cdot\operatorname{tg}15^\circ=\operatorname{ctg}15^\circ\cdot\operatorname{tg}15^\circ=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 26 [B]
$2x=(x+y)+(x-y)$: $\operatorname{tg}2x=\dfrac{\frac12+\frac13}{1-\frac16}=\dfrac{\frac56}{\frac56}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 27 [C]
$\sin(\alpha\pm45^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(\sin\alpha\pm\cos\alpha)$, demak $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha=3(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha)$.
$4\cos\alpha=2\sin\alpha \Rightarrow \operatorname{tg}\alpha=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 28 [A]
$\cos15^\circ-\sqrt3\sin15^\circ=2\Bigl(\dfrac12\cos15^\circ-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin15^\circ\Bigr)=2\cos75^\circ=2\sin15^\circ$.
$2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}$.

### 29 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}2\alpha=\dfrac{2\cdot\frac12}{1-\frac14}=\dfrac43$.
$\operatorname{tg}(2\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{\frac43+\frac14}{1-\frac43\cdot\frac14}=\dfrac{\frac{19}{12}}{\frac23}=\dfrac{19}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{19}{8}$.

### 30 [B]
Çizmadan: $\operatorname{tg}\angle CAH=\dfrac34$, $\operatorname{tg}\angle HAB=\dfrac84=2$.
$\operatorname{tg}\angle CAB=\dfrac{\frac34+2}{1-\frac34\cdot2}=\dfrac{\frac{11}4}{-\frac12}=-\dfrac{11}{2}$ (burçak ötmas).

Javob: $-\dfrac{11}{2}$.
