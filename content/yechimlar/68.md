# 68. Asosiy trigonometrik ayniyatlar

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Kosinus juft, sinus toq funksiya: $\cos(-x)=\cos x$, $\sin(-x)=-\sin x$.
$(\cos x+\cos x)(\sin x+\sin x)=2\cos x\cdot2\sin x=4\sin x\cos x$.

Javob: $4\sin x\cos x$.

### 2 [B]
$\sin(-4x)=-\sin4x$, $\cos(-5x)=\cos5x$:
$\dfrac{-\sin4x-\cos5x}{\cos5x+\sin4x}=\dfrac{-(\sin4x+\cos5x)}{\sin4x+\cos5x}=-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 3 [A]
$f(-x)=\sin(-x)+\cos(-x)=-\sin x+\cos x$.
$f(x)+f(-x)=\sin x+\cos x-\sin x+\cos x=2\cos x$.

Javob: $2\cos x$.

### 4 [B]
$\sin(-30^\circ)=-\dfrac12$, $\operatorname{tg}(-30^\circ)=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, $\cos(-30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$f(-30^\circ)=2\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac12\Bigr)+\sqrt3\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\Bigr)-4\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=-1-1-2\sqrt3=-2\sqrt3-2$.

Javob: $-2\sqrt{3}-2$.

### 5 [B]
Asosiy ayniyat $\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha=1$ dan $1-\sin^2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\cos^2\alpha$.

### 6 [C]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi: $(1-\cos\alpha)(1+\cos\alpha)=1-\cos^2\alpha=\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin^2\alpha$.

### 7 [B]
Umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz:
$1-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2\alpha}=\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha-1}{\sin^2\alpha}=\dfrac{-\cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}=-\operatorname{ctg}^2\alpha$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{ctg}^2\alpha$.

### 8 [B]
Umumiy maxraj $(1-\cos\alpha)(1+\cos\alpha)=\sin^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{1+\cos\alpha+1-\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}=\dfrac{2}{\sin^2\alpha}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{\sin^2\alpha}$.

### 9 [D]
Umumiy maxraj $1-\sin^2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{\cos^2\alpha\bigl((1+\sin\alpha)-(1-\sin\alpha)\bigr)}{\cos^2\alpha}=2\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $2\sin\alpha$.

### 10 [C]
Kublar yiğindisi formulasi:
$\sin^3\alpha+\cos^3\alpha=(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)(\sin^2\alpha-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha+\cos^2\alpha)=(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)(1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)$.
Maxraj bilan qisqartiramiz: $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha$.

### 11 [A]
Kublar ayirmasi formulasidan:
$\dfrac{\cos^3\alpha-\sin^3\alpha}{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}=\cos^2\alpha+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha+\sin^2\alpha=1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.
$1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)^2$.

Javob: $(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)^2$.

### 12 [B]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi: $4-\sin^2\alpha+25-\cos^2\alpha=29-(\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha)=29-1=28$.

Javob: $28$.

### 13 [A]
$\dfrac{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha}=\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\cdot\dfrac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}=\cos^2\alpha$.
$1-\cos^2\alpha=\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin^2\alpha$.

### 14 [C]
$\cos^2\beta-1=-\sin^2\beta$:
$\dfrac{\cos^2\beta}{\sin^2\beta}\cdot(-\sin^2\beta)=-\cos^2\beta$.

Javob: $-\cos^2\beta$.

### 15 [B]
Qavslarni oçamiz: $\sin\alpha+\sin\alpha\cdot\dfrac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}-\cos\alpha=\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha-\cos\alpha=\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin\alpha$.

### 16 [D]
$1+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$, $1+\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$.
Nisbati: $\dfrac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}=\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$.

### 17 [A]
$\operatorname{ctg}(-\alpha)=-\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$, $\cos(-\alpha)=\cos\alpha$, $\sin(-\alpha)=-\sin\alpha$:
$\dfrac{1+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha}{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}=\dfrac{\frac{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}}{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha}$.

### 18 [A]
Kvadratlarni oçamiz: $(1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)+(1-2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 19 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$.
$\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}\cdot\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin^2\alpha$.

### 20 [D]
$\operatorname{ctg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\beta=\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha}+\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\beta}=\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{tg}\beta}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta}$.
Maxrajga bölsak: $\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta}=\operatorname{ctg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{ctg}\beta$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{ctg}\beta$.

### 21 [A]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{9}{25}=\dfrac{16}{25}$. I çorakda $\sin\alpha>0$: $\sin\alpha=\dfrac45$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{5}$.

### 22 [C]
$\cos^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{144}{169}=\dfrac{25}{169}$. II çorakda $\cos\alpha<0$: $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac{5}{13}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{5}{13}$.

### 23 [C]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac12=\dfrac12$. III çorakda $\sin\alpha<0$: $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

### 24 [D]
$1+\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}$: $\cos^2\alpha=\dfrac{1}{16}$. IV çorakda $\cos\alpha=\dfrac14$.
$\sin\alpha=\operatorname{tg}\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha=-\sqrt{15}\cdot\dfrac14=-\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4}$.

### 25 [B]
Surat va maxrajni $\cos\alpha$ ga bölamiz:
$\dfrac{3\operatorname{tg}\alpha+1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha-1}=\dfrac{6+1}{2-1}=7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 26 [D]
Surat va maxrajni $\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=1+\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha=5$:
$\dfrac{3\cdot5+\operatorname{tg}\alpha}{2\cdot5+1}=\dfrac{15+2}{11}=\dfrac{17}{11}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{17}{11}$.

### 27 [C]
$\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=1+\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha=\dfrac54$:
$\dfrac{\frac54+2\cdot\frac12}{1-\frac12}=\dfrac{\frac94}{\frac12}=\dfrac92$.

Javob: $\dfrac{9}{2}$.

### 28 [A]
Tenglikni kvadratga oşiramiz: $1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac14$.
$\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac{\frac14-1}{2}=-\dfrac38$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{8}$.

### 29 [B]
Avvalgi hisobdan $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=-\dfrac38$. Kublar yiğindisi formulasi:
$\sin^3\alpha+\cos^3\alpha=(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)(1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{11}{8}=\dfrac{11}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{16}$.

### 30 [A]
$a=\sin^2x$, $b=\cos^2x$, $a+b=1$. $a^3+b^3=(a+b)^3-3ab(a+b)=1-3ab$.
$1-3\sin^2x\cos^2x+3\sin^2x\cos^2x=1$.

Javob: $1$.

## 2-test

### 1 [C]
$\sin(-x)=-\sin x$, $\cos(-x)=\cos x$:
$(-2\sin x)(2\cos x)=-4\sin x\cos x$.

Javob: $-4\sin x\cos x$.

### 2 [A]
$\cos(-3x)=\cos3x$, $\sin(-x)=-\sin x$:
$\dfrac{2(\cos3x-\sin x)}{\cos3x-\sin x}=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 3 [A]
$f(-x)=\cos x+\operatorname{tg}x$ (kosinus juft, tangens toq).
$f(x)-f(-x)=\cos x-\operatorname{tg}x-\cos x-\operatorname{tg}x=-2\operatorname{tg}x$.

Javob: $-2\operatorname{tg}x$.

### 4 [B]
$\sin(-45^\circ)=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, $\operatorname{tg}(-45^\circ)=-1$, $\cos(-45^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.
$f(-45^\circ)=-1+3+1=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 5 [B]
$\sin^2\alpha-1=-(1-\sin^2\alpha)=-\cos^2\alpha$.

Javob: $-\cos^2\alpha$.

### 6 [C]
$(\sin\alpha-1)(\sin\alpha+1)=\sin^2\alpha-1=-\cos^2\alpha$.

Javob: $-\cos^2\alpha$.

### 7 [A]
$\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}-1=\dfrac{1-\cos^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}=\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}=\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha$.

### 8 [D]
Umumiy maxraj $1-\sin^2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{(1+\sin\alpha)-(1-\sin\alpha)}{\cos^2\alpha}=\dfrac{2\sin\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\sin\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}$.

### 9 [A]
Umumiy maxraj $1-\cos^2\alpha=\sin^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha\bigl((1+\cos\alpha)-(1-\cos\alpha)\bigr)}{\sin^2\alpha}=2\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $2\cos\alpha$.

### 10 [B]
Kublar ayirmasi formulasi:
$\dfrac{(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha)(\sin^2\alpha+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha+\cos^2\alpha)}{\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha}=1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

### 11 [A]
Kublar yiğindisi formulasidan kasr $1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$ ga teng.
$1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha-1=-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

### 12 [D]
$36-\cos^2\alpha+1-\sin^2\alpha=37-1=36$.

Javob: $36$.

### 13 [B]
$\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\cdot\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=\sin^2\alpha$, demak ifoda $1+\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $1+\sin^2\alpha$.

### 14 [D]
$1-\sin^2\beta=\cos^2\beta$: $\dfrac{\sin^2\beta}{\cos^2\beta}\cdot\cos^2\beta=\sin^2\beta$.

Javob: $\sin^2\beta$.

### 15 [C]
$\cos\alpha\operatorname{tg}\alpha-\cos\alpha+\cos\alpha=\cos\alpha\cdot\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin\alpha$.

### 16 [C]
$1-\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$, $1-\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$.
Nisbati: $\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}\cdot\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{-(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)}=-\dfrac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=-\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

### 17 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}(-\alpha)=-\operatorname{tg}\alpha$, $\cos(-\alpha)=\cos\alpha$:
$\dfrac{-\operatorname{tg}\alpha-1}{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{-\frac{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha}}{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}=-\dfrac{1}{\cos\alpha}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{\cos\alpha}$.

### 18 [B]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi: $(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab$.
$4\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $4\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

### 19 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$, teskarisi $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

### 20 [D]
$\operatorname{ctg}\beta-\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\beta}-\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha}=\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha-\operatorname{tg}\beta}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta}$.
Suratni bunga bölsak: $\operatorname{tg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{tg}\beta$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{tg}\beta$.

### 21 [A]
$\cos^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{25}{169}=\dfrac{144}{169}$. I çorakda $\cos\alpha=\dfrac{12}{13}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{12}{13}$.

### 22 [B]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{64}{289}=\dfrac{225}{289}$. II çorakda $\sin\alpha>0$: $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{15}{17}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{15}{17}$.

### 23 [A]
$\cos^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{16}{25}=\dfrac{9}{25}$. III çorakda $\cos\alpha<0$: $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac35$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{5}$.

### 24 [D]
$\cos^2\alpha=\dfrac{1}{1+\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha}=\dfrac1{25}$. III çorakda $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac15$.
$\sin\alpha=\operatorname{tg}\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha=-\dfrac{\sqrt{24}}{5}=-\dfrac{2\sqrt6}{5}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{2\sqrt{6}}{5}$.

### 25 [A]
$\cos\alpha$ ga bölamiz: $\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\alpha-2}{3\operatorname{tg}\alpha+1}=\dfrac{4-2}{12+1}=\dfrac{2}{13}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{13}$.

### 26 [C]
$\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=1+\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha=10$:
$\dfrac{10+2\cdot3}{4\cdot10-9}=\dfrac{16}{31}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{16}{31}$.

### 27 [A]
$\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=5$:
$\dfrac{2\cdot5-2}{4-3\cdot2}=\dfrac{8}{-2}=-4$.

Javob: $-4$.

### 28 [D]
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $1-2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac19$, bundan $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac{1-\frac19}{2}=\dfrac49$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{9}$.

### 29 [D]
$\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac49$ (avvalgi hisob). Kublar ayirmasi formulasi:
$\sin^3\alpha-\cos^3\alpha=(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha)(1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)=\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac{13}{9}=\dfrac{13}{27}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{13}{27}$.

### 30 [C]
Töla kvadrat: $\sin^4x+2\sin^2x\cos^2x+\cos^4x=(\sin^2x+\cos^2x)^2=1$.
$1-3=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

## 3-test

### 1 [C]
$\sin x-\sin(-x)=2\sin x$, $\cos x-\cos(-x)=0$.
$(2\sin x)^2+0=4\sin^2x$.

Javob: $4\sin^2x$.

### 2 [C]
$\cos(-4x)=\cos4x$, $\sin(-x)=-\sin x$:
$\dfrac{\cos4x+\sin x}{3(\cos4x+\sin x)}=\dfrac13$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

### 3 [A]
$f(-x)=\sin^2x-\sin x$.
$f(x)+f(-x)=2\sin^2x$.

Javob: $2\sin^2x$.

### 4 [D]
$\cos(-60^\circ)=\dfrac12$, $\operatorname{ctg}(-60^\circ)=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, $\sin(-60^\circ)=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$f(-60^\circ)=2+1-3=0$.

Javob: $0$.

### 5 [C]
$\cos^2\alpha-1=-(1-\cos^2\alpha)=-\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $-\sin^2\alpha$.

### 6 [D]
$2(1-\cos\alpha)(1+\cos\alpha)=2(1-\cos^2\alpha)=2\sin^2\alpha$.

Javob: $2\sin^2\alpha$.

### 7 [A]
$\dfrac{1-\sin^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}=\dfrac{\cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}=\operatorname{ctg}^2\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}^2\alpha$.

### 8 [C]
Umumiy maxraj $\cos^2\alpha-1=-\sin^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{(\cos\alpha+1)+(\cos\alpha-1)}{-\sin^2\alpha}=-\dfrac{2\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{2\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}$.

### 9 [B]
Umumiy maxraj $1-\sin^2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha$:
$\dfrac{3\cos^2\alpha\bigl((1+\sin\alpha)+(1-\sin\alpha)\bigr)}{\cos^2\alpha}=3\cdot2=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 10 [D]
Kublar yiğindisi: $\dfrac{(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)(1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)}{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}=1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

Javob: $1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$.

### 11 [B]
Kublar ayirmasidan kasr $1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$ ga teng.
$1+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 12 [B]
$49-\sin^2\alpha+4-\cos^2\alpha=53-1=52$.

Javob: $52$.

### 13 [A]
$\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha}{\cos\alpha}+\cos\alpha=\dfrac{\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{1}{\cos\alpha}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{\cos\alpha}$.

### 14 [A]
$1-\cos^2\beta=\sin^2\beta$: $\dfrac{\cos^2\beta}{\sin^2\beta}\cdot\sin^2\beta=\cos^2\beta$.

Javob: $\cos^2\beta$.

### 15 [D]
$\sin\alpha\operatorname{ctg}\alpha+2\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=\cos\alpha+2\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=2\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $2\sin\alpha$.

### 16 [A]
$\operatorname{ctg}\alpha-1=\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$, $1-\operatorname{tg}\alpha=\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$.
Nisbati: $\dfrac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}=\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$.

### 17 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}(-\alpha)=-\operatorname{ctg}\alpha$, $\sin(-\alpha)=-\sin\alpha$:
$\dfrac{-(\operatorname{ctg}\alpha+1)}{-(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}=\dfrac{\frac{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}{\sin\alpha}}{\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha}$.

### 18 [D]
$(\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha)^2=\operatorname{tg}^2\alpha+2\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{ctg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}^2\alpha$, bunda $\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=1$.
Ifoda $2$ ga teng.

Javob: $2$.

### 19 [B]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$, demak köpaytma $1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 20 [A]
$\operatorname{ctg}\alpha-\operatorname{ctg}\beta=\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha}-\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\beta}=\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}\beta-\operatorname{tg}\alpha}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta}$.
Maxrajga bölsak: $\dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tg}\alpha\operatorname{tg}\beta}=\operatorname{ctg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{ctg}\beta$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}\alpha\cdot\operatorname{ctg}\beta$.

### 21 [C]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{64}{289}=\dfrac{225}{289}$. I çorakda $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{15}{17}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{15}{17}$.

### 22 [D]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{9}{25}=\dfrac{16}{25}$. II çorakda $\sin\alpha>0$: $\sin\alpha=\dfrac45$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{5}$.

### 23 [C]
$\sin^2\alpha=1-\dfrac{25}{169}=\dfrac{144}{169}$. III çorakda $\sin\alpha<0$: $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{12}{13}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{12}{13}$.

### 24 [A]
$\cos^2\alpha=\dfrac{1}{1+35}=\dfrac1{36}$. II çorakda $\cos\alpha=-\dfrac16$.
$\sin\alpha=\operatorname{tg}\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha=-\sqrt{35}\cdot\Bigl(-\dfrac16\Bigr)=\dfrac{\sqrt{35}}{6}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{35}}{6}$.

### 25 [A]
Surat va maxrajni $\sin\alpha$ ga bölamiz:
$\dfrac{2+3\operatorname{ctg}\alpha}{1-\operatorname{ctg}\alpha}=\dfrac{2+6}{1-2}=-8$.

Javob: $-8$.

### 26 [D]
$\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=1+25=26$:
$\dfrac{4\cdot26-5}{26+3}=\dfrac{99}{29}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{99}{29}$.

### 27 [B]
$\cos^2\alpha$ ga bölamiz, $\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\alpha}=1+\dfrac1{16}=\dfrac{17}{16}$:
$\dfrac{\frac{17}{16}+4\cdot\frac14}{4\cdot\frac1{16}+\frac14}=\dfrac{\frac{33}{16}}{\frac12}=\dfrac{33}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{33}{8}$.

### 28 [A]
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $1-2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac1{25}$, bundan $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac{12}{25}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{12}{25}$.

### 29 [D]
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac{25}{16}$, $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac{9}{32}$.
$\sin^3\alpha+\cos^3\alpha=(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)(1-\sin\alpha\cos\alpha)=\dfrac54\cdot\dfrac{23}{32}=\dfrac{115}{128}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{115}{128}$.

### 30 [C]

$2(1-3p)-3(1-2p)=2-6p-3+6p=-1$.
