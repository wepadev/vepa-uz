# 23. Algebraik kasrlar (1-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
Koeffitsiyentlarni bölamiz, darajalar bölinganda körsatkiçlar ayiriladi:
$\dfrac{24}{8} = 3$, $m^{4-2} = m^2$, $n^{3-1} = n^2$.

Javob: $3m^2n^2$.

### 2 [C]
Surat: $y(x - 4y)$. Maxraj: $(x - 4y)(x + 4y)$.
$(x - 4y)$ ga qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{y}{x + 4y}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{y}{x+4y}$.

### 3 [A]
Surat töla kvadrat: $(x + 4)^2$. Maxraj: $(x - 4)(x + 4)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x + 4}{x - 4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x+4}{x-4}$.

### 4 [A]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi:
surat $(x + 3y - 2z)(x + 3y + 2z)$, maxraj $(x + 2z - 3y)(x + 2z + 3y)$.
Umumiy köpaytuvçi $(x + 3y + 2z)$ ga qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x + 3y - 2z}{x + 2z - 3y}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x+3y-2z}{x+2z-3y}$.

### 5 [D]
Kublar yiğindisi: $m^3 + 216 = (m + 6)(m^2 - 6m + 36)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{1}{m + 6}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{m+6}$.

### 6 [C]
Surat: $x(x^3 - 27) = x(x - 3)(x^2 + 3x + 9)$. Maxraj: $x(x^2 + 3x + 9)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $x - 3$.

Javob: $x-3$.

### 7 [D]
Bölişni teskari kasrga köpaytiriş bilan almaştiramiz:
$\dfrac{10a^3b}{9x^2y^2} \cdot \dfrac{27xy^3}{25a^2b^2} = \dfrac{10 \cdot 27}{9 \cdot 25} \cdot \dfrac{ay}{bx} = \dfrac{6ay}{5bx}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{6ay}{5bx}$.

### 8 [B]
Köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz:
$\dfrac{(a + 4b)^2}{(a - 4b)(a + 4b)} \cdot \dfrac{a(a - 4b)}{a(a + b)}$.
Qisqartirişdan keyin: $\dfrac{a + 4b}{a + b}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+4b}{a+b}$.

### 9 [C]
Birinçi kasr: $\dfrac{(x^2 - 16y^2)(x^2 + 16y^2)}{x^2 + 16y^2} = x^2 - 16y^2$.
Ikkinçi kasr: $x^6 + (16y^2)^3 = (x^2 + 16y^2)(x^4 - 16x^2y^2 + 256y^4)$, demak u $x^2 + 16y^2$ ga teng.
Yiğindi: $x^2 - 16y^2 + x^2 + 16y^2 = 2x^2$.

Javob: $2x^2$.

### 10 [B]
Maxrajlar: $5(x - 4)$ va $7(x - 4)$, umumiy maxraj $35(x - 4)$.
$\dfrac{4 \cdot 7 + 3 \cdot 5}{35(x - 4)} = \dfrac{43}{35x - 140}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{43}{35x-140}$.

### 11 [A]
Kublar formulalari böyiça kasrlar qisqaradi: $\dfrac{1}{x - 3y} + \dfrac{1}{x + 3y}$.
$\dfrac{x + 3y + x - 3y}{x^2 - 9y^2} = \dfrac{2x}{x^2 - 9y^2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2x}{x^2-9y^2}$.

### 12 [D]
Surat: $(x + 3)^2 - y^2 = (x + 3 - y)(x + 3 + y)$.
Maxraj: $9 - (x + y)^2 = (3 - x - y)(3 + x + y)$.
$(x + y + 3)$ ga qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x + 3 - y}{3 - x - y}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x+3-y}{3-x-y}$.

### 13 [B]
Surat: $(a + 3b)^2 - c^2 = (a + 3b - c)(a + 3b + c)$.
Maxraj: $a^2 - (3b + c)^2 = (a - 3b - c)(a + 3b + c)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{a + 3b - c}{a - 3b - c}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+3b-c}{a-3b-c}$.

### 14 [D]
Surat: $4a(a^3 - 64) = 4a(a - 4)(a^2 + 4a + 16)$.
Maxraj: $(a + 4)^2 - 4a = a^2 + 4a + 16$ va $a^2 - 4a = a(a - 4)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 15 [D]
Surat: $x^4 + 9x^2 + 81 = (x^2 + 9)^2 - 9x^2 = (x^2 - 3x + 9)(x^2 + 3x + 9)$.
Maxraj: $x^3 + 27 = (x + 3)(x^2 - 3x + 9)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x^2 + 3x + 9}{x + 3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x^2+3x+9}{x+3}$.

### 16 [C]
Surat: $x^2(x - a) + 3(x^2 - 2ax + a^2) = x^2(x - a) + 3(x - a)^2 = (x - a)(x^2 + 3x - 3a)$.
Maxraj: $x^4 - 9(x - a)^2 = (x^2 - 3x + 3a)(x^2 + 3x - 3a)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x - a}{x^2 - 3x + 3a}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x-a}{x^2-3x+3a}$.

### 17 [B]
Maxraj: $72^3 - 38^3 = (72 - 38)(72^2 + 72 \cdot 38 + 38^2)$, surat: $(72 - 38)(72 + 38)(72^2 + 72 \cdot 38 + 38^2)$.
Qisqartirişdan keyin $72 + 38 = 110$.

Javob: $110$.

### 18 [A]
Maxraj: $8{,}4^2 + 2 \cdot 8{,}4 \cdot 1{,}6 + 1{,}6^2 - 2 \cdot 1{,}6 \cdot 8{,}4 = 8{,}4^2 + 1{,}6^2$.
Surat: $(8{,}4^2 - 1{,}6^2)(8{,}4^2 + 1{,}6^2)$.
Natija: $8{,}4^2 - 1{,}6^2 = (8{,}4 - 1{,}6)(8{,}4 + 1{,}6) = 6{,}8 \cdot 10 = 68$.

Javob: $68$.

### 19 [C]
$\dfrac{n^2 - 4n - 30}{n} = n - 4 - \dfrac{30}{n}$, demak $n$ soni $30$ ning böluvçisi.
$n = 10$: $3$; $n = 15$: $9$; $n = 30$: $25$ — natural. $n \le 6$ da qiymat manfiy yoki nol.

Javob: $\{10, 15, 30\}$.

### 20 [D]
$\dfrac{n^2 + 5n + 12}{n} = n + 5 + \dfrac{12}{n}$, $n$ soni $12$ ning böluvçisi ($\pm1, \pm2, \pm3, \pm4, \pm6, \pm12$).
$n + \dfrac{12}{n}$ qiymatlari: $\pm13$, $\pm8$, $\pm7$. $5$ qöşsak: $18, 13, 12, -8, -3, -2$.
Yiğindi: $18 + 13 + 12 - 8 - 3 - 2 = 30$.

Javob: $30$.

### 21 [B]
$a = 16^x$, $b = 9^x$ desak: $256^x = a^2$, $81^x = b^2$, $144^x = ab$.
$\dfrac{a^2 - b^2}{ab - b^2} = \dfrac{(a - b)(a + b)}{b(a - b)} = 1 + \dfrac{a}{b} = 1 + \left(\dfrac{16}{9}\right)^3$.
$1 + \dfrac{4096}{729} = \dfrac{4825}{729}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4825}{729}$.

### 22 [A]
$\dfrac{3n + 1}{n - 3} = 3 + \dfrac{10}{n - 3}$. Qiymat natural bölişi uçun $n - 3$ soni $10$ ning musbat böluvçisi bölişi kerak (manfiy böluvçilarda qiymat manfiy).
$n - 3 \in \{1, 2, 5, 10\}$, ya'ni $n \in \{4, 5, 8, 13\}$.

Javob: $\{4, 5, 8, 13\}$.

## 2-test

### 3 [B]

Javob: $\dfrac{x+5}{x-5}$.

### 4 [D]

$(x + 4y + 5z)$ ga qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x + 4y - 5z}{x + 5z - 4y}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x+4y-5z}{x+5z-4y}$.

### 5 [C]
$m^3 + 512 = (m + 8)(m^2 - 8m + 64)$, qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{1}{m + 8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{m+8}$.

### 6 [B]
$\dfrac{x(x - 4)(x^2 + 4x + 16)}{x(x^2 + 4x + 16)} = x - 4$.

Javob: $x-4$.

### 7 [D]
$\dfrac{14a^3b}{15x^2y^2} \cdot \dfrac{25xy^3}{21a^2b^2} = \dfrac{14 \cdot 25}{15 \cdot 21} \cdot \dfrac{ay}{bx} = \dfrac{10ay}{9bx}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{10ay}{9bx}$.

### 8 [A]
$\dfrac{(a + 5b)^2}{(a - 5b)(a + 5b)} \cdot \dfrac{a(a - 5b)}{a(a + b)} = \dfrac{a + 5b}{a + b}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+5b}{a+b}$.

### 9 [C]
Birinçi kasr $x^2 - 25y^2$ ga teng.
$x^6 + (25y^2)^3 = (x^2 + 25y^2)(x^4 - 25x^2y^2 + 625y^4)$, demak ikkinçi kasr $x^2 + 25y^2$.
Yiğindi: $2x^2$.

Javob: $2x^2$.

### 10 [D]
Maxrajlar $6(x - 5)$ va $9(x - 5)$, umumiy maxraj $18(x - 5)$.
$\dfrac{5 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 2}{18(x - 5)} = \dfrac{19}{18x - 90}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{19}{18x-90}$.

### 11 [B]
Kasrlar qisqaradi: $\dfrac{1}{x - 4y} + \dfrac{1}{x + 4y} = \dfrac{2x}{x^2 - 16y^2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2x}{x^2-16y^2}$.

### 12 [A]
Surat: $(x + 4)^2 - y^2 = (x + 4 - y)(x + 4 + y)$.
Maxraj: $16 - (x + y)^2 = (4 - x - y)(4 + x + y)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{x + 4 - y}{4 - x - y}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x+4-y}{4-x-y}$.

### 13 [B]
Surat: $(a + 4b - c)(a + 4b + c)$. Maxraj: $a^2 - (4b + c)^2 = (a - 4b - c)(a + 4b + c)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $\dfrac{a + 4b - c}{a - 4b - c}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+4b-c}{a-4b-c}$.

### 14 [D]
Surat: $5a(a^3 - 125) = 5a(a - 5)(a^2 + 5a + 25)$.
Maxraj: $(a^2 + 5a + 25) \cdot a(a - 5)$.
Qisqartiramiz: $5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 15 [A]
Surat: $(x^2 + 16)^2 - 16x^2 = (x^2 - 4x + 16)(x^2 + 4x + 16)$.
Maxraj: $(x + 4)(x^2 - 4x + 16)$.
Natija: $\dfrac{x^2 + 4x + 16}{x + 4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x^2+4x+16}{x+4}$.

### 16 [C]
Surat: $x^2(x - a) + 4(x - a)^2 = (x - a)(x^2 + 4x - 4a)$.
Maxraj: $x^4 - 16(x - a)^2 = (x^2 - 4x + 4a)(x^2 + 4x - 4a)$.
Natija: $\dfrac{x - a}{x^2 - 4x + 4a}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x-a}{x^2-4x+4a}$.

### 17 [D]
Kublar ayirmasi formulasidan foydalanib qisqartiramiz: natija $84 + 16 = 100$.

Javob: $100$.

### 18 [B]
Maxraj: $7{,}2^2 + 2{,}8^2$ (örta hadlar qisqaradi). Surat: $(7{,}2^2 - 2{,}8^2)(7{,}2^2 + 2{,}8^2)$.
Natija: $(7{,}2 - 2{,}8)(7{,}2 + 2{,}8) = 4{,}4 \cdot 10 = 44$.

Javob: $44$.

### 19 [A]
$n - 6 - \dfrac{24}{n}$, demak $n$ soni $24$ ning böluvçisi.
$n = 12$: $4$; $n = 24$: $17$ — natural. $n = 8$ da $-1$, kiçikroq $n$ larda ham manfiy.

Javob: $\{12, 24\}$.

### 20 [C]
$n + 2 + \dfrac{15}{n}$, $n \in \{\pm1, \pm3, \pm5, \pm15\}$.
$n + \dfrac{15}{n}$: $\pm16$, $\pm8$. $2$ qöşsak: $18, 10, -14, -6$.
Yiğindi: $18 + 10 - 14 - 6 = 8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 21 [D]
$a = 25^x$, $b = 4^x$: $625^x = a^2$, $16^x = b^2$, $100^x = ab$.
Ifoda $1 + \dfrac{a}{b} = 1 + \left(\dfrac{25}{4}\right)^3 = 1 + \dfrac{15625}{64} = \dfrac{15689}{64}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{15689}{64}$.

### 22 [B]
$\dfrac{5n + 3}{n - 4} = 5 + \dfrac{23}{n - 4}$, $n - 4$ soni $23$ ning musbat böluvçisi bölişi kerak.
$n - 4 \in \{1, 23\}$, ya'ni $n \in \{5, 27\}$.

Javob: $\{5, 27\}$.
