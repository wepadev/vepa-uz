# 62. Logarifmik şakl almaştiriş

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Yangi asosga ötiş formulasi: $\log_{ab}(a^2b)=\dfrac{\log_a(a^2b)}{\log_a(ab)}$.
$\log_a(a^2b)=2+\log_ab=7$, $\log_a(ab)=1+\log_ab=6$.
Natija $\dfrac76$.

Javob: $\dfrac76$.

### 2 [B]
$\log_9A=\dfrac12\log_3A=\log_3B\Rightarrow A=B^2$.
$\dfrac{A^4}{B^5}=\dfrac{B^8}{B^5}=B^3$, $AB=B^3$.
$\log_{B^3}B^3=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 3 [D]
$xyz=1$ bölgani uçun $\dfrac1{yz}=x$, $\dfrac1{xz}=y$, $\dfrac1{xy}=z$.
Har bir had $\log_xx=1$ ga teng, yiğindi $3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 4 [D]
Logarifm xossalari böyiça ifoda $\log_3\dfrac{a^4b}{c}$ ga teng.
$\dfrac{a^4b}{c}=27$, demak $\log_327=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 5 [C]
$\log_3\sqrt[3]{a^4}=\dfrac43\log_3a=\dfrac43\cdot1{,}5=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 6 [A]
$\ln\sqrt[3]{\dfrac ab}=\dfrac13(\ln a-\ln b)=\dfrac13(2{,}1-0{,}9)=0{,}4$.

Javob: $\dfrac25$.

### 7 [B]
$16807=7^5$, demak $\log_316807=5\log_37=5a$.

Javob: $5a$.

### 8 [D]
$\log_{125}9=\log_{5^3}3^2=\dfrac23\log_53=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac1a$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3a}$.

### 9 [A]
$54=2\cdot3^3$, demak $\lg54=\lg2+3\lg3=a+3b$.

Javob: $a+3b$.

### 10 [A]
$2$ asosga ötamiz: $\log_{48}12=\dfrac{\log_212}{\log_248}$.
$\log_212=\log_2(4\cdot3)=2+a$, $\log_248=\log_2(16\cdot3)=4+a$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+2}{a+4}$.

### 11 [C]
$\lg40=\lg4+\lg10=2\lg2+1=a\Rightarrow\lg2=\dfrac{a-1}{2}$.
$\lg5=1-\lg2=\dfrac{3-a}{2}$.
$\log_52=\dfrac{\lg2}{\lg5}=\dfrac{a-1}{3-a}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a-1}{3-a}$.

### 12 [B]
$x=\log_35$ belgilaymiz: $\log_3135=3+x$, $\log_345=2+x$, $\log_315=1+x$.
$x(3+x)-(2+x)(1+x)=3x+x^2-2-3x-x^2=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

### 13 [C]
$\dfrac1{\log_{54}3}=\log_354$, $\dfrac1{\log_63}=\log_36$. $y=\log_32$:
$y(3+y)-(2+y)(1+y)=3y+y^2-2-3y-y^2=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

### 14 [A]
$\dfrac{\log_318}{\log_{18}3}=\log_3^218$, $\dfrac{\log_32}{\log_{162}3}=\log_32\cdot\log_3162$. $y=\log_32$:
$(2+y)^2-y(4+y)=4+4y+y^2-4y-y^2=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 15 [C]
$u=\lg4$ desak, $\log_410=\dfrac1u$, $\log_{40}4=\dfrac{1}{\log_440}=\dfrac{1}{1+\frac1u}=\dfrac{u}{u+1}$.
Birinçi qavs: $u+\dfrac1u+2=\dfrac{(u+1)^2}{u}$; ikkinçi qavs: $u-\dfrac{u}{u+1}=\dfrac{u^2}{u+1}$.
Köpaytma: $\dfrac{(u+1)^2}{u}\cdot\dfrac{u^2}{u+1}\cdot\dfrac1u=u+1$.
$u+1-u=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [D]
$a=\log_36$, $b=\log_32$: surat $2a^2-ab-b^2=(2a+b)(a-b)$.
Qisqartirib $a-b=\log_36-\log_32=\log_33=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 17 [B]
$\log_39=2$, $\log_93=\dfrac12$, $\log_3\dfrac93=1$.
$\dfrac{1-8}{\bigl(2+\frac12+1\bigr)\cdot1}=\dfrac{-7}{3{,}5}=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

### 18 [A]
Önli logarifmga ötamiz: $\log_{12}54=\dfrac{\lg54}{\lg12}$.
$\lg54=\lg2+3\lg3=a+3b$, $\lg12=2\lg2+\lg3=2a+b$.

Javob: $\dfrac{a+3b}{2a+b}$.
