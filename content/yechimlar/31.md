# 31. Arifmetik kvadrat ildiz va uning xossalari 1-qism

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
Arifmetik kvadrat ildiz — kvadrati berilgan songa teng manfiy bölmagan son.
$22^2 = 484$, demak $\sqrt{484} = 22$.

Javob: $22$.

### 2 [A]
$\sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$ xossasidan foydalanamiz:
$\sqrt{24 \cdot 54} = \sqrt{1296} = 36$.

Javob: $36$.

### 3 [D]
$\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}$ xossasidan foydalanamiz:
$\sqrt{\dfrac{96}{150}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{16}{25}} = \dfrac{4}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{5}$.

### 4 [C]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi formulasi: $25^2 - 15^2 = (25 - 15)(25 + 15) = 10 \cdot 40 = 400$.
$\sqrt{400} = 20$.

Javob: $20$.

### 5 [A]
$a \ge 0$ uçun $(\sqrt{a})^2 = a$.
$(\sqrt{98})^2 = 98$.

Javob: $98$.

### 6 [D]
Har bir köpaytuvçidan ildiz olamiz ($\sqrt{a^{2n}} = a^n$):
$\sqrt{7^2 \cdot 2^4 \cdot 3^6} = 7 \cdot 2^2 \cdot 3^3 = 7 \cdot 4 \cdot 27 = 756$.

Javob: $756$.

### 7 [D]
Kasrlarni qöşamiz: $\dfrac{1}{64} + \dfrac{1}{225} = \dfrac{225 + 64}{64 \cdot 225} = \dfrac{289}{14400}$.
$\sqrt{\dfrac{289}{14400}} = \dfrac{17}{120}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{17}{120}$.

### 8 [C]
Ildiz ostidan köpaytuvçi çiqaramiz: $\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{98} = 7\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{32} = 4\sqrt{2}$.
$\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{4\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{15}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{15}{4}$.

### 9 [A]
$\sqrt{20} = 2\sqrt{5}$, $\sqrt{80} = 4\sqrt{5}$, şuning uçun qavs içi $2\sqrt{5} + 6\sqrt{5} + 4\sqrt{5} = 12\sqrt{5}$.
$\sqrt{5} \cdot 12\sqrt{5} = 12 \cdot 5 = 60$.

Javob: $60$.

### 10 [C]
Kvadratlarni oçamiz: $(\sqrt{7} - \sqrt{3})^2 = 10 - 2\sqrt{21}$, $(\sqrt{21} + 1)^2 = 22 + 2\sqrt{21}$.
Yiğindi: $10 + 22 = 32$.

Javob: $32$.

### 11 [B]
$\sqrt{9 - \sqrt{61}} \cdot \sqrt{9 + \sqrt{61}} = \sqrt{81 - 61} = \sqrt{20}$.
$\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{20} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.

Javob: $10$.

### 12 [B]
$\dfrac{41^2 - 9^2}{64} = \dfrac{32 \cdot 50}{64} = 25$, $\sqrt{25} = 5$.
$\sqrt{44 + 5} = \sqrt{49} = 7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 13 [A]
Suratdagi qavslarni köpaytiramiz:
$64\sqrt{3} - 8\sqrt{45} + 24\sqrt{5} - 3\sqrt{75} = 64\sqrt{3} - 24\sqrt{5} + 24\sqrt{5} - 15\sqrt{3} = 49\sqrt{3}$.
$\dfrac{49\sqrt{3}}{7\sqrt{3}} = 7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 14 [D]
Maxraj: $(\sqrt{20} - \sqrt{7})^2 = 27 - 2\sqrt{140} = 27 - 4\sqrt{35}$.
Surat: $54 - 8\sqrt{35} = 2(27 - 4\sqrt{35})$.
Demak, kasr $2$ ga teng.

Javob: $2$.

### 15 [B]
Ikkinçi qavsni guruhlaymiz: $7(3 + \sqrt{2}) + \sqrt{13}(3 + \sqrt{2}) = (7 + \sqrt{13})(3 + \sqrt{2})$.
$3 + \sqrt{2}$ qisqaradi: $(7 - \sqrt{13})(7 + \sqrt{13}) = 49 - 13 = 36$.

Javob: $36$.

### 16 [A]
Sonlarni ildiz ostiga kiritamiz: $a = \sqrt{81}$, $b = \sqrt{4 \cdot 21} = \sqrt{84}$, $c = \sqrt{25 \cdot 3} = \sqrt{75}$.
$75 < 81 < 84$, demak $c < a < b$.

Javob: $c < a < b$.

### 17 [C]
$\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$. $3\sqrt{5} = \sqrt{45} > \sqrt{36} = 6$, ya'ni $3\sqrt{5} - 6 > 0$.
Demak, $\sqrt{(3\sqrt{5} - 6)^2} = 3\sqrt{5} - 6$.

Javob: $-6 + 3\sqrt{5}$.

### 18 [B]
Töla kvadratga ajratamiz: $10 + 2\sqrt{21} = (\sqrt{7} + \sqrt{3})^2$, $4 + 2\sqrt{3} = (\sqrt{3} + 1)^2$.
$(\sqrt{7} + \sqrt{3}) - (\sqrt{3} + 1) = \sqrt{7} - 1$.

Javob: $-1 + \sqrt{7}$.

### 19 [C]
$31 \pm 10\sqrt{6} = 25 + 6 \pm 2 \cdot 5\sqrt{6} = (5 \pm \sqrt{6})^2$.
$5 - \sqrt{6} > 0$, şuning uçun yiğindi $(5 + \sqrt{6}) + (5 - \sqrt{6}) = 10$.

Javob: $10$.

### 20 [A]
Içki ildiz: $28 + 10\sqrt{3} = (5 + \sqrt{3})^2$, $\sqrt{28 + 10\sqrt{3}} = 5 + \sqrt{3}$.
Taşqi ildiz ostida: $44 + 13\sqrt{3} - 5 - \sqrt{3} = 39 + 12\sqrt{3} = (6 + \sqrt{3})^2$.
Natija: $6 + \sqrt{3}$.

Javob: $6 + \sqrt{3}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [D]
$25^2 = 625$, demak $\sqrt{625} = 25$.

Javob: $25$.

### 2 [C]
$\sqrt{45 \cdot 80} = \sqrt{3600} = 60$.

Javob: $60$.

### 3 [C]
$\sqrt{\dfrac{175}{252}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{25 \cdot 7}{36 \cdot 7}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{25}{36}} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{6}$.

### 4 [A]
$29^2 - 21^2 = 8 \cdot 50 = 400$, $\sqrt{400} = 20$.

Javob: $20$.

### 5 [B]
$(\sqrt{a})^2 = a$, demak $(\sqrt{135})^2 = 135$.

Javob: $135$.

### 6 [A]
$\sqrt{2^8 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 3^4} = 2^4 \cdot 5 \cdot 3^2 = 16 \cdot 5 \cdot 9 = 720$.

Javob: $720$.

### 7 [D]
$\dfrac{1}{49} + \dfrac{1}{576} = \dfrac{576 + 49}{49 \cdot 576} = \dfrac{625}{28224}$.
$\sqrt{\dfrac{625}{28224}} = \dfrac{25}{168}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{25}{168}$.

### 8 [D]
$\sqrt{20} = 2\sqrt{5}$, $\sqrt{45} = 3\sqrt{5}$, $\sqrt{180} = 6\sqrt{5}$, $\sqrt{125} = 5\sqrt{5}$.
$\dfrac{11\sqrt{5}}{5\sqrt{5}} = \dfrac{11}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{5}$.

### 9 [C]
$3\sqrt{8} = 6\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$, $2\sqrt{18} = 6\sqrt{2}$; qavs içi $17\sqrt{2}$.
$\sqrt{2} \cdot 17\sqrt{2} = 34$.

Javob: $34$.

### 10 [A]
$(\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5})^2 = 11 - 2\sqrt{30}$, $(\sqrt{30} + 1)^2 = 31 + 2\sqrt{30}$.
Yiğindi: $11 + 31 = 42$.

Javob: $42$.

### 11 [B]
$\sqrt{11 - \sqrt{71}} \cdot \sqrt{11 + \sqrt{71}} = \sqrt{121 - 71} = \sqrt{50}$.
$\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{50} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.

Javob: $10$.

### 12 [A]
$\dfrac{50^2 - 14^2}{64} = \dfrac{36 \cdot 64}{64} = 36$, $\sqrt{36} = 6$.
$\sqrt{58 + 6} = \sqrt{64} = 8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 13 [D]
Suratni köpaytiramiz:
$81\sqrt{2} - 18\sqrt{28} + 36\sqrt{7} - 8\sqrt{98} = 81\sqrt{2} - 36\sqrt{7} + 36\sqrt{7} - 56\sqrt{2} = 25\sqrt{2}$.
$\dfrac{25\sqrt{2}}{5\sqrt{2}} = 5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 14 [B]
Maxraj: $(\sqrt{28} - \sqrt{3})^2 = 31 - 2\sqrt{84} = 31 - 4\sqrt{21}$.
Surat: $124 - 16\sqrt{21} = 4(31 - 4\sqrt{21})$, demak kasr $4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 15 [B]
$20 + 5\sqrt{3} + 4\sqrt{7} + \sqrt{21} = 5(4 + \sqrt{3}) + \sqrt{7}(4 + \sqrt{3}) = (5 + \sqrt{7})(4 + \sqrt{3})$.
$4 + \sqrt{3}$ qisqaradi: $(5 - \sqrt{7})(5 + \sqrt{7}) = 25 - 7 = 18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 16 [C]
$a = \sqrt{49}$, $b = \sqrt{9 \cdot 5} = \sqrt{45}$, $c = \sqrt{4 \cdot 13} = \sqrt{52}$.
$45 < 49 < 52$, demak $b < a < c$.

Javob: $b < a < c$.

### 17 [A]
$5\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{75} < \sqrt{81} = 9$, ya'ni $5\sqrt{3} - 9 < 0$.
$\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$, şuning uçun natija $9 - 5\sqrt{3}$.

Javob: $9 - 5\sqrt{3}$.

### 18 [C]
$8 + 2\sqrt{12} = (\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2})^2$, $3 + 2\sqrt{2} = (\sqrt{2} + 1)^2$.
$(\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}) - (\sqrt{2} + 1) = \sqrt{6} - 1$.

Javob: $-1 + \sqrt{6}$.

### 19 [D]
$14 \pm 6\sqrt{5} = 9 + 5 \pm 2 \cdot 3\sqrt{5} = (3 \pm \sqrt{5})^2$, $3 - \sqrt{5} > 0$.
Yiğindi: $(3 + \sqrt{5}) + (3 - \sqrt{5}) = 6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 20 [D]
Içki ildiz: $21 + 8\sqrt{5} = (4 + \sqrt{5})^2$, ya'ni $\sqrt{21 + 8\sqrt{5}} = 4 + \sqrt{5}$.
Taşqi ildiz ostida: $18 + 7\sqrt{5} - 4 - \sqrt{5} = 14 + 6\sqrt{5} = (3 + \sqrt{5})^2$.
Natija: $3 + \sqrt{5}$.

Javob: $3 + \sqrt{5}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [A]
$27^2 = 729$, demak $\sqrt{729} = 27$.

Javob: $27$.

### 2 [D]
$\sqrt{28 \cdot 63} = \sqrt{1764} = 42$.

Javob: $42$.

### 3 [C]
$\sqrt{\dfrac{242}{338}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 \cdot 121}{2 \cdot 169}} = \dfrac{11}{13}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{13}$.

### 4 [B]
$37^2 - 12^2 = 25 \cdot 49$, $\sqrt{25 \cdot 49} = 5 \cdot 7 = 35$.

Javob: $35$.

### 5 [A]
$(\sqrt{162})^2 = 162$.

Javob: $162$.

### 6 [C]
$\sqrt{11^2 \cdot 2^6 \cdot 5^4} = 11 \cdot 2^3 \cdot 5^2 = 11 \cdot 8 \cdot 25 = 2200$.

Javob: $2200$.

### 7 [D]
$\dfrac{1}{400} + \dfrac{1}{441} = \dfrac{441 + 400}{400 \cdot 441} = \dfrac{841}{176400}$.
$\sqrt{\dfrac{841}{176400}} = \dfrac{29}{420}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{29}{420}$.

### 8 [C]
$\sqrt{112} = 4\sqrt{7}$, $\sqrt{252} = 6\sqrt{7}$, $\sqrt{63} = 3\sqrt{7}$.
$\dfrac{(1 + 4 + 6)\sqrt{7}}{3\sqrt{7}} = \dfrac{11}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{3}$.

### 9 [A]
$\sqrt{63} = 3\sqrt{7}$, $2\sqrt{28} = 4\sqrt{7}$; qavs içi $3\sqrt{7} + 4\sqrt{7} + 3\sqrt{7} = 10\sqrt{7}$.
$\sqrt{7} \cdot 10\sqrt{7} = 70$.

Javob: $70$.

### 10 [B]
$(\sqrt{11} - \sqrt{2})^2 = 13 - 2\sqrt{22}$, $(\sqrt{22} + 1)^2 = 23 + 2\sqrt{22}$.
Yiğindi: $13 + 23 = 36$.

Javob: $36$.

### 11 [D]
$\sqrt{10 - \sqrt{93}} \cdot \sqrt{10 + \sqrt{93}} = \sqrt{100 - 93} = \sqrt{7}$.
$\sqrt{7} \cdot \sqrt{7} = 7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 12 [C]
$\dfrac{65^2 - 17^2}{246} = \dfrac{48 \cdot 82}{246} = \dfrac{48}{3} = 16$, $\sqrt{16} = 4$.
$\sqrt{77 + 4} = \sqrt{81} = 9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 13 [B]
Suratni köpaytiramiz:
$36\sqrt{5} - 6\sqrt{50} + 30\sqrt{2} - 5\sqrt{20} = 36\sqrt{5} - 30\sqrt{2} + 30\sqrt{2} - 10\sqrt{5} = 26\sqrt{5}$.
$\dfrac{26\sqrt{5}}{2\sqrt{5}} = 13$.

Javob: $13$.

### 14 [A]
Maxraj: $(\sqrt{45} - \sqrt{14})^2 = 59 - 2\sqrt{630} = 59 - 6\sqrt{70}$.
Surat: $118 - 12\sqrt{70} = 2(59 - 6\sqrt{70})$, demak kasr $2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 15 [B]
$16 + 8\sqrt{7} + 2\sqrt{17} + \sqrt{119} = 8(2 + \sqrt{7}) + \sqrt{17}(2 + \sqrt{7}) = (8 + \sqrt{17})(2 + \sqrt{7})$.
$2 + \sqrt{7}$ qisqaradi: $(8 - \sqrt{17})(8 + \sqrt{17}) = 64 - 17 = 47$.

Javob: $47$.

### 16 [B]
$a = \sqrt{100}$, $b = \sqrt{9 \cdot 13} = \sqrt{117}$, $c = \sqrt{16 \cdot 8} = \sqrt{128}$.
$100 < 117 < 128$, demak $a < b < c$.

Javob: $a < b < c$.

### 17 [A]
$2\sqrt{7} = \sqrt{28} > \sqrt{25} = 5$, ya'ni $2\sqrt{7} - 5 > 0$.
Demak, $\sqrt{(2\sqrt{7} - 5)^2} = 2\sqrt{7} - 5$.

Javob: $-5 + 2\sqrt{7}$.

### 18 [D]
$14 + 2\sqrt{33} = (\sqrt{11} + \sqrt{3})^2$, $4 + 2\sqrt{3} = (\sqrt{3} + 1)^2$.
$(\sqrt{11} + \sqrt{3}) - (\sqrt{3} + 1) = \sqrt{11} - 1$.

Javob: $-1 + \sqrt{11}$.

### 19 [D]
$47 \pm 12\sqrt{11} = 36 + 11 \pm 2 \cdot 6\sqrt{11} = (6 \pm \sqrt{11})^2$, $6 - \sqrt{11} > 0$.
Yiğindi: $(6 + \sqrt{11}) + (6 - \sqrt{11}) = 12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 20 [C]
Içki ildiz: $43 + 12\sqrt{7} = (6 + \sqrt{7})^2$, ya'ni $\sqrt{43 + 12\sqrt{7}} = 6 + \sqrt{7}$.
Taşqi ildiz ostida: $38 + 11\sqrt{7} - 6 - \sqrt{7} = 32 + 10\sqrt{7} = (5 + \sqrt{7})^2$.
Natija: $5 + \sqrt{7}$.

Javob: $5 + \sqrt{7}$.
