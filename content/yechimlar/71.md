### 8 [A]

Javob: $0{,}2$.

### 10 [B]
$\sin2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$, $2\sin\alpha$ ga qisqartiramiz:
$\dfrac{1+\cos\alpha}{1-\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{\frac23}{\frac43}=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 11 [D]
$\dfrac{2\cos^2\alpha+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{2\sin^2\alpha+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{2\cos\alpha(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)}{2\sin\alpha(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}=\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac14$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

### 12 [C]
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\dfrac19$, ya'ni $1+\sin2\alpha=\dfrac19$.
$\sin2\alpha=-\dfrac89$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{8}{9}$.

### 13 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}\alpha+\operatorname{ctg}\alpha=\dfrac{1}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{2}{\sin2\alpha}$.
$\dfrac{2}{\sin2\alpha}=-8 \Rightarrow \sin2\alpha=-\dfrac14$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{4}$.

### 14 [B]

$\operatorname{tg}2\alpha=\dfrac{2\cdot(-\frac43)}{1-\frac{16}9}=\dfrac{-\frac83}{-\frac79}=\dfrac{24}{7}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{24}{7}$.

### 15 [D]
$\cos2\alpha=(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)$, qisqartiramiz:
$(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)-(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)=2\sin\alpha$.

Javob: $2\sin\alpha$.

### 16 [C]
Umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{\sin3\alpha\cos\alpha+\cos3\alpha\sin\alpha}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=\dfrac{\sin4\alpha}{\frac12\sin2\alpha}$.
$\sin4\alpha=2\sin2\alpha\cos2\alpha$, demak natija $4\cos2\alpha$.

Javob: $4\cos2\alpha$.

### 17 [D]
$1-\cos2\alpha=2\sin^2\alpha$, $1+\cos2\alpha=2\cos^2\alpha$, $\sin2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$:
$\dfrac{2\sin\alpha(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}{2\cos\alpha(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)}=\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}\alpha$.

### 18 [B]
$\sin\dfrac{\pi}{16}\cos\dfrac{\pi}{16}=\dfrac12\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}$, keyin $\dfrac12\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}\cos\dfrac{\pi}{8}=\dfrac14\sin\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{8}$.

### 19 [C]
Umumiy köpaytuvçini çiqaramiz: $\sin x\cos x(\cos^2x-\sin^2x)=\dfrac12\sin2x\cos2x=\dfrac14\sin4x$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}$: $\dfrac14\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}$.

### 20 [A]
$\cos^4x-\sin^4x=(\cos^2x-\sin^2x)(\cos^2x+\sin^2x)=\cos2x$.
$\cos210^\circ=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 21 [B]

$1-\dfrac12\sin^2 45^\circ=1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

### 22 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}x+\operatorname{ctg}x=\dfrac{2}{\sin2x}=\dfrac{2}{\sin45^\circ}=\dfrac{2}{\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}=2\sqrt2$.

Javob: $2\sqrt{2}$.

### 23 [C]
$\sin162^\circ=\sin18^\circ$, $\cos162^\circ=-\cos18^\circ$, $\operatorname{tg}324^\circ=-\operatorname{tg}36^\circ$.
$\sin18^\circ+\cos18^\circ\operatorname{tg}36^\circ=\dfrac{\sin18^\circ\cos36^\circ+\cos18^\circ\sin36^\circ}{\cos36^\circ}=\dfrac{\sin54^\circ}{\cos36^\circ}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 24 [A]

Javob: $-\dfrac{23}{27}$.

### 25 [B]

$\cos4\alpha=2\cos^22\alpha-1=\dfrac{98}{81}-1=\dfrac{17}{81}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{17}{81}$.

### 26 [B]

$\sin36^\circ\cos36^\circ\cos72^\circ=\dfrac12\sin72^\circ\cos72^\circ=\dfrac14\sin144^\circ=\dfrac14\sin36^\circ$, demak $P=\dfrac14$.
Ifoda $-P=-\dfrac14$.

### 27 [D]

### 28 [B]
$\sin40^\circ=\cos50^\circ=\cos(2\cdot25^\circ)=2\cos^2 25^\circ-1=2a^2-1$.

Javob: $2a^2-1$.

### 29 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}x-\operatorname{ctg}x=\dfrac{\sin^2x-\cos^2x}{\sin x\cos x}=-\dfrac{\cos2x}{\frac12\sin2x}=-2\operatorname{ctg}2x$.
$-2\operatorname{ctg}45^\circ=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

### 30 [C]

$\dfrac{\sin40^\circ+\sqrt3\cos40^\circ}{\sin40^\circ\cos40^\circ}=\dfrac{2\sin(40^\circ+60^\circ)}{\frac12\sin80^\circ}=\dfrac{2\sin80^\circ}{\frac12\sin80^\circ}=4$.
