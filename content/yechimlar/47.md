# 47. Ratsional körsatkiçli daraja va uning xossalari

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Darajaning xossalari: $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$, $a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$, $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$.
Surat: $x^{-3}y^2 \cdot x^2y^{-8} = x^{-1}y^{-6}$. Maxraj: $x^{-2}y^{-3}$.
$\dfrac{x^{-1}y^{-6}}{x^{-2}y^{-3}} = x^{1}y^{-3} = \dfrac{x}{y^3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x}{y^3}$.

### 2 [C]
Içkaridan boşlab $\sqrt[n]{x^m} = x^{\frac{m}{n}}$ formulasini qöllaymiz.
$x^2\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{5}{2}}$, $\sqrt[3]{x^{\frac{5}{2}}} = x^{\frac{5}{6}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{5}{6}} = x^{\frac{11}{6}}$, $\sqrt[4]{x^{\frac{11}{6}}} = x^{\frac{11}{24}}$.
$x^2 \cdot x^{\frac{11}{24}} = x^{\frac{59}{24}}$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{59}{24}}$.

### 3 [D]
Surat: $x\sqrt[5]{x} = x^{\frac{6}{5}}$, kvadrat ildiz: $x^{\frac{3}{5}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{3}{5}} = x^{\frac{8}{5}}$, kub ildiz: $x^{\frac{8}{15}}$.
Maxraj: $x\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}$, kub ildiz: $x^{\frac{1}{2}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}$, 4-daraja ildiz: $x^{\frac{3}{8}}$.
$\dfrac{8}{15} - \dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{64 - 45}{120} = \dfrac{19}{120}$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{19}{120}}$.

### 4 [B]
Toq darajali ildiz: $\sqrt[n]{a^n} = a$; juft darajali: $\sqrt[n]{a^n} = |a|$. $a > 0$ da hammasi $a$ ga teng.
$-2a + 3a + a = 2a$.

Javob: $2a$.

### 5 [D]
$a < 0$: $\sqrt[5]{a^5} = a$, $\sqrt[6]{a^6} = |a| = -a$, $\sqrt[7]{a^7} = a$.
$2a - (-a) + a = 4a$.

Javob: $4a$.

### 6 [A]
$\sqrt[5]{-a^5} = -a$, $\sqrt[3]{(-b)^3} = -b$, $\sqrt[6]{(a - b)^6} = |a - b| = b - a$ (çunki $a < b$).
$2(-a) - b + 3(b - a) = -5a + 2b$.

Javob: $-5a + 2b$.

### 7 [A]
$4 < \sqrt{19} < 5$. Toq daraja: $\sqrt[3]{(\sqrt{19} - 5)^3} = \sqrt{19} - 5$.
Juft daraja: $|4 - \sqrt{19}| = \sqrt{19} - 4$, $|\sqrt{19} - 1| = \sqrt{19} - 1$.
Yiğindi: $3\sqrt{19} - 10$.

Javob: $3\sqrt{19} - 10$.

### 8 [B]
$243 = 3^5$, $256 = 4^4$.
$243^{\frac{2}{5}} = 3^2 = 9$, $256^{\frac{1}{4}} = 4$. $9 - 4 = 5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 9 [A]
$\sqrt{\sqrt[4]{256}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$, $\sqrt[4]{\sqrt{256}} = \sqrt[4]{16} = 2$.
$2 - 2 = 0$.

Javob: $0$.

### 10 [D]
Ildizlarni birlaştiramiz: $2\sqrt[3]{20 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2} \cdot \dfrac{5}{2}} = 2\sqrt[3]{125} = 10$.
$\dfrac{4 - 1}{3} = 1$. $10 - 1 = 9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 11 [B]
Ildiz ostidan köpaytuvçi çiqaramiz: $\sqrt[4]{512} = \sqrt[4]{256 \cdot 2} = 4\sqrt[4]{2}$, $\sqrt[4]{162} = \sqrt[4]{81 \cdot 2} = 3\sqrt[4]{2}$.
$\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2} + 4\sqrt[4]{2}}{3\sqrt[4]{2}} = \dfrac{5}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{3}$.

### 12 [B]
Surat: $\sqrt[4]{64 \cdot 36} = \sqrt[4]{48^2} = \sqrt{48} = 4\sqrt{3}$, unda $4\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{3} = 12$.
Maxraj: $3^{\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2}} = 3^1 = 3$.
$\dfrac{12}{3} = 4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 13 [C]
Bir xil darajali ildizlarni köpaytiramiz: $\sqrt[4]{(\sqrt{21} + \sqrt{5})(\sqrt{21} - \sqrt{5})} = \sqrt[4]{21 - 5} = \sqrt[4]{16} = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 14 [B]
$7 - 4\sqrt{3} = (2 - \sqrt{3})^2$, $97 + 56\sqrt{3} = (7 + 4\sqrt{3})^2 = (2 + \sqrt{3})^4$.
Ifoda: $(2 - \sqrt{3})(2 + \sqrt{3}) = 4 - 3 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 15 [D]
$3 + \sqrt{8} = (1 + \sqrt{2})^2$, $17 - 6\sqrt{8} = 17 - 12\sqrt{2} = (3 - 2\sqrt{2})^2 = (\sqrt{2} - 1)^4$.
Ifoda: $\sqrt{1 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \sqrt{\sqrt{2} - 1} = \sqrt{2 - 1} = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [C]
$33 - 8\sqrt{17} = (\sqrt{17} - 4)^2$, demak $\sqrt[4]{33 - 8\sqrt{17}} = \sqrt{\sqrt{17} - 4}$.
Kasr $1$ ga teng.

Javob: $1$.

### 17 [A]
Ikkala sonni $15$-darajaga kötaramiz: $(\sqrt[5]{4})^{15} = 4^3 = 64$, $(\sqrt[3]{3})^{15} = 3^5 = 243$.
$64 < 243$, demak $\sqrt[5]{4} < \sqrt[3]{3}$.

Javob: $<$.

### 18 [D]
Bir xil darajali ildizga keltiramiz: $\sqrt{2\sqrt[3]{5}} = \sqrt[6]{2^3 \cdot 5} = \sqrt[6]{40}$, $\sqrt[3]{4\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt[6]{4^2 \cdot 3} = \sqrt[6]{48}$.
$\sqrt[6]{40} < \sqrt[6]{48}$, minus işorasi bilan tengsizlik almaşadi: $-\sqrt[6]{40} > -\sqrt[6]{48}$.

Javob: $>$.

### 19 [C]
$20$-darajaga kötaramiz: $P^{20} = 5^5 = 3125$, $Q^{20} = 7^4 = 2401$, $R^{20} = 2800$.
$2401 < 2800 < 3125$.

Javob: $Q < R < P$.

### 20 [A]
Surat va maxrajni $\sqrt[5]{2^4} = \sqrt[5]{16}$ ga köpaytiramiz: $\dfrac{\sqrt[5]{16}}{\sqrt[5]{32}} = \dfrac{\sqrt[5]{16}}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt[5]{16}}{2}$.

### 21 [B]
Avval $\sqrt[4]{8} + \sqrt{3}$ ga köpaytiramiz: maxraj $\sqrt{8} - 3$. Keyin $\sqrt{8} + 3$ ga köpaytiramiz: maxraj $8 - 9 = -1$.
$\dfrac{(\sqrt[4]{8} + \sqrt{3})(\sqrt{8} + 3)}{-1}$.

Javob: $-(\sqrt[4]{8} + \sqrt{3})(\sqrt{8} + 3)$.

### 22 [C]
$t = x^{\frac{1}{4}}$ deymiz. Surat: $t^5 - t = t(t - 1)(t + 1)(t^2 + 1)$.
Maxraj: $t^4 + t^3 + t^2 + t = t(t + 1)(t^2 + 1)$.
Qisqartirsak $t - 1$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{1}{4}} - 1$.

### 23 [A]
Qavs içi: $\dfrac{n^2 + 27n - 729 - n^2}{n + 27} = \dfrac{27(n - 27)}{n + 27}$. $n = t^3$ da: $\dfrac{27(t - 3)(t^2 + 3t + 9)}{(t + 3)(t^2 - 3t + 9)}$.
Bölinuvçi kasr $\dfrac{t^2 + 3t + 9}{t^2 - 3t + 9}$, unga bölsak: $27\dfrac{t - 3}{t + 3}$.

Javob: $27\dfrac{t - 3}{t + 3}$.

### 24 [D]
$187 \cdot 205 = (196 - 9)(196 + 9) = 196^2 - 81$.
$\sqrt[4]{196^2} = \sqrt{196} = 14$.

Javob: $14$.

### 25 [B]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi formulasini ketma-ket qöllaymiz: $(\sqrt[8]{7} - 1)(\sqrt[8]{7} + 1) = \sqrt[4]{7} - 1$, $(\sqrt[4]{7} - 1)(\sqrt[4]{7} + 1) = \sqrt{7} - 1$.
$\sqrt{7} - 1 = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{7} + 1} \Rightarrow a = 7 - 1 = 6$.

Javob: $6$.

## 2-test

### 1 [C]
Surat: $x^{-3}y^{-15} \cdot x^4y^{-2} = xy^{-17}$. Maxraj: $x^{-4}y$.
$\dfrac{xy^{-17}}{x^{-4}y} = x^5y^{-18} = \dfrac{x^5}{y^{18}}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{x^5}{y^{18}}$.

### 2 [D]
$x\sqrt[4]{x} = x^{\frac{5}{4}}$, kub ildiz: $x^{\frac{5}{12}}$, $x^2 \cdot x^{\frac{5}{12}} = x^{\frac{29}{12}}$, 5-daraja ildiz: $x^{\frac{29}{60}}$.
$x \cdot x^{\frac{29}{60}} = x^{\frac{89}{60}}$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{89}{60}}$.

### 3 [A]
Surat: $x\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}$, kub ildiz $x^{\frac{1}{2}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}$, 4-daraja ildiz $x^{\frac{3}{8}}$.
Maxraj: $x\sqrt[3]{x} = x^{\frac{4}{3}}$, 5-daraja ildiz $x^{\frac{4}{15}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{4}{15}} = x^{\frac{19}{15}}$, kvadrat ildiz $x^{\frac{19}{30}}$.
$\dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{19}{30} = \dfrac{45 - 76}{120} = -\dfrac{31}{120}$.

Javob: $x^{-\frac{31}{120}}$.

### 4 [D]
$a > 0$ da barça ildizlar $a$ ga teng: $4a - 2a - a = a$.

Javob: $a$.

### 5 [B]
$a < 0$: $\sqrt[3]{a^3} = a$, $\sqrt[8]{a^8} = -a$, $\sqrt[9]{a^9} = a$.
$-2a + 3(-a) - a = -6a$.

Javob: $-6a$.

### 6 [C]
$\sqrt[7]{-a^7} = -a$, $\sqrt[5]{(-b)^5} = -b$, $\sqrt[8]{(a - b)^8} = b - a$.
$-a - 3b + 2(b - a) = -3a - b$.

Javob: $-3a - b$.

### 7 [A]
$3 < \sqrt{13} < 4$. $\sqrt[7]{(\sqrt{13} - 5)^7} = \sqrt{13} - 5$, $|3 - \sqrt{13}| = \sqrt{13} - 3$, $|\sqrt{13} - 2| = \sqrt{13} - 2$.
Yiğindi: $3\sqrt{13} - 10$.

Javob: $3\sqrt{13} - 10$.

### 8 [B]
$1296 = 6^4$, $32 = 2^5$: $6^3 - 2^2 = 216 - 4 = 212$.

Javob: $212$.

### 9 [D]
$3125 = 5^5$. Ikkala ifoda ham $3125^{\frac{1}{10}} = \sqrt{5}$ ga teng.
Ayirma $0$.

Javob: $0$.

### 10 [C]
$4\sqrt[3]{14 \cdot \dfrac{7}{2} \cdot 7} = 4\sqrt[3]{343} = 28$.
$\dfrac{6 - 3}{5} = \dfrac{3}{5}$. $28 - \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{137}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{137}{5}$.

### 11 [A]
$\sqrt[4]{567} = \sqrt[4]{81 \cdot 7} = 3\sqrt[4]{7}$, $\sqrt[4]{4375} = \sqrt[4]{625 \cdot 7} = 5\sqrt[4]{7}$, $\sqrt[4]{1792} = \sqrt[4]{256 \cdot 7} = 4\sqrt[4]{7}$.
$\dfrac{8\sqrt[4]{7}}{4\sqrt[4]{7}} = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 12 [B]
Surat: $\sqrt[4]{216 \cdot 150} = \sqrt[4]{180^2} = \sqrt{180} = 6\sqrt{5}$, unda $6\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{5} = 30$.
Maxraj: $5^{\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2}} = 5$. $\dfrac{30}{5} = 6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 13 [D]
$\sqrt[5]{(\sqrt{256})^2 - (\sqrt{13})^2} = \sqrt[5]{256 - 13} = \sqrt[5]{243} = 3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 14 [C]
$9 - 4\sqrt{5} = (\sqrt{5} - 2)^2$, $161 + 72\sqrt{5} = (9 + 4\sqrt{5})^2 = (\sqrt{5} + 2)^4$.
Ifoda: $(\sqrt{5} - 2)(\sqrt{5} + 2) = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 15 [B]
$31 - 8\sqrt{15} = (4 - \sqrt{15})^2$, demak $\sqrt[8]{31 - 8\sqrt{15}} = \sqrt[4]{4 - \sqrt{15}}$.
Ifoda: $\sqrt[4]{(4 + \sqrt{15})(4 - \sqrt{15})} = \sqrt[4]{16 - 15} = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [D]
$51 - 10\sqrt{26} = (\sqrt{26} - 5)^2$, demak maxraj $\sqrt{\sqrt{26} - 5}$ ga teng.

Javob: $1$.

### 17 [A]
$\sqrt[6]{64} = \sqrt[6]{2^6} = 2$, $\sqrt[4]{16} = 2$.

Javob: $=$.

### 18 [D]
$\sqrt{4\sqrt[3]{3}} = \sqrt[6]{4^3 \cdot 3} = \sqrt[6]{192}$, $\sqrt[3]{6\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt[6]{36 \cdot 5} = \sqrt[6]{180}$.
$\sqrt[6]{192} > \sqrt[6]{180}$, minus bilan: $-\sqrt[6]{192} < -\sqrt[6]{180}$.

### 19 [B]
$12$-darajaga kötaramiz: $P^{12} = 4^4 = 256$, $Q^{12} = 15^2 = 225$, $R^{12} = 240$.
$225 < 240 < 256$.

Javob: $Q < R < P$.

### 20 [B]
Surat va maxrajni $\sqrt[3]{49}$ ga köpaytiramiz: $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{49}}{\sqrt[3]{343}} = \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{49}}{7}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{49}}{7}$.

### 21 [C]
$\sqrt[4]{10} + \sqrt{3}$ ga köpaytirsak maxraj $\sqrt{10} - 3$; yana $\sqrt{10} + 3$ ga köpaytirsak maxraj $10 - 9 = 1$.

Javob: $(\sqrt[4]{10} + \sqrt{3})(\sqrt{10} + 3)$.

### 22 [D]
$t = x^{\frac{1}{3}}$: surat $t^4 - 27t = t(t - 3)(t^2 + 3t + 9)$, maxraj $t(t^2 + 3t + 9)$.
Qisqartirsak $t - 3$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{1}{3}} - 3$.

### 23 [D]

$\dfrac{t^2 + 2t + 4}{t^2 - 2t + 4}$ ga bölsak: $8\dfrac{t - 2}{t + 2}$.

Javob: $8\dfrac{t - 2}{t + 2}$.

### 24 [A]

$\sqrt[4]{256^2} = \sqrt{256} = 16$.

Javob: $16$.

### 25 [A]

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
Surat: $x^{-8}y^{-2} \cdot x^3y^{-2} = x^{-5}y^{-4}$. Maxraj: $x^{-3}y^{12}$.
$\dfrac{x^{-5}y^{-4}}{x^{-3}y^{12}} = x^{-2}y^{-16} = \dfrac{1}{x^2y^{16}}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{x^2y^{16}}$.

### 2 [A]
$x^2\sqrt[3]{x} = x^{\frac{7}{3}}$, 5-daraja ildiz $x^{\frac{7}{15}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{7}{15}} = x^{\frac{22}{15}}$, kvadrat ildiz $x^{\frac{11}{15}}$.
$x^3 \cdot x^{\frac{11}{15}} = x^{\frac{56}{15}}$.

Javob: $x^{\frac{56}{15}}$.

### 3 [B]
Surat: $x\sqrt[3]{x} = x^{\frac{4}{3}}$, kvadrat ildiz $x^{\frac{2}{3}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{2}{3}} = x^{\frac{5}{3}}$, 5-daraja ildiz $x^{\frac{1}{3}}$.
Maxraj: $x\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}$, 4-daraja ildiz $x^{\frac{3}{8}}$, $x \cdot x^{\frac{3}{8}} = x^{\frac{11}{8}}$, kub ildiz $x^{\frac{11}{24}}$.
$\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{11}{24} = -\dfrac{3}{24} = -\dfrac{1}{8}$.

Javob: $x^{-\frac{1}{8}}$.

### 4 [A]
$a > 0$ da barça ildizlar $a$: $3a + 2a - 4a = a$.

Javob: $a$.

### 5 [C]
$a < 0$: $\sqrt[7]{a^7} = a$, $\sqrt[4]{a^4} = -a$, $\sqrt[5]{a^5} = a$.
$3a - 2a - 2a = -a$.

Javob: $-a$.

### 6 [D]
$\sqrt[3]{-a^3} = -a$, $\sqrt[7]{(-b)^7} = -b$, $\sqrt[4]{(a - b)^4} = b - a$.

Javob: $-4a - b$.

### 7 [C]
$5 < \sqrt{29} < 6$. $\sqrt[5]{(\sqrt{29} - 6)^5} = \sqrt{29} - 6$, $|5 - \sqrt{29}| = \sqrt{29} - 5$, $|\sqrt{29} - 2| = \sqrt{29} - 2$.

Javob: $3\sqrt{29} - 13$.

### 8 [B]

### 9 [D]

### 10 [A]

### 11 [C]

### 13 [B]
