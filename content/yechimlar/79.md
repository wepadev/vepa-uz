# 79. Hosila (1-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Darajali funksiya hosilasi: $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$.
$$f'(x)=4\cdot\dfrac52x^{\frac52-1}=10x^{\frac32}.$$

Javob: $10x^{\frac32}$.

### 2 [B]
$(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$ formulasini $n=-\dfrac12$ uçun qöllaymiz:
$$f'(x)=4\cdot\left(-\dfrac12\right)x^{-\frac12-1}=-2x^{-\frac32}.$$

Javob: $-2x^{-\frac32}$.

### 3 [D]
Ildizni daraja körinişida yozamiz: $\sqrt[3]{x}=x^{\frac13}$.
$$f'(x)=\dfrac13x^{\frac13-1}=\dfrac13x^{-\frac23}=\dfrac{1}{3\sqrt[3]{x^2}}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{3\sqrt[3]{x^2}}$.

### 4 [B]
Yiğindining hosilasi hosilalar yiğindisiga teng, $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$, $x'=1$.
$\left(\dfrac{x^5}{5}\right)'=x^4$, $\left(\dfrac{2x^3}{3}\right)'=2x^2$.
Demak, $f'(x)=x^4-2x^2+1$.

Javob: $x^4-2x^2+1$.

### 5 [A]
Har bir qöşiluvçining hosilasini olamiz ($\sqrt[3]{3}$ va $3^3=27$ — özgarmas sonlar).
$\left(\dfrac{1}{10}x^{10}\right)'=x^9$, $(\sqrt[3]{3}\,x)'=\sqrt[3]{3}$, $(27x^2)'=54x$.
$f'(x)=x^9+\sqrt[3]{3}-54x$.

Javob: $x^9+\sqrt[3]{3}-54x$.

### 6 [D]
$a,b,c$ — özgarmaslar, özgarmasning hosilasi $0$.
$(ax^2)'=2ax$, $(bx)'=b$, $c'=0$.
$f'(x)=2ax+b$.

Javob: $2ax+b$.

### 7 [C]
Daraja körinişiga keltiramiz: $2x\sqrt x=2x^{\frac32}$, $\dfrac{3}{x^2}=3x^{-2}$.
$(2x^{\frac32})'=3x^{\frac12}=3\sqrt x$, $(3x^{-2})'=-6x^{-3}$, $5'=0$.
$y'=3\sqrt x-(-6x^{-3})=3\sqrt x+\dfrac{6}{x^3}$.

Javob: $3\sqrt x+\dfrac{6}{x^3}$.

### 8 [A]
$(\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$, $\left(\dfrac1x\right)'=-\dfrac{1}{x^2}$.
$(2\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}$, $\left(-\dfrac4x\right)'=\dfrac{4}{x^2}$, $1'=0$.
$y'=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}+\dfrac{4}{x^2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}+\dfrac{4}{x^2}$.

### 9 [C]
$f'(x)=\dfrac13+\dfrac{3}{x^2}$, çunki $\left(-\dfrac3x\right)'=\dfrac{3}{x^2}$.
$x_0=3$ da: $f'(3)=\dfrac13+\dfrac39=\dfrac13+\dfrac13=\dfrac23$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3}$.

### 10 [B]
$f(x)=2x^{\frac32}-x^{-\frac12}$, $f'(x)=3x^{\frac12}+\dfrac12x^{-\frac32}=3\sqrt x+\dfrac{1}{2x\sqrt x}$.
$f'(4)=3\cdot2+\dfrac{1}{2\cdot4\cdot2}=6+\dfrac{1}{16}=\dfrac{97}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{97}{16}$.

### 11 [B]
Hosilani topamiz: $f'(x)=-6x+18$.
$-6x_0+18=0 \Rightarrow x_0=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 12 [A]
Köpaytma hosilasi: $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$.
$f'(x)=(2x-1)(x^3+1)+(x^2-x)\cdot3x^2$
$=2x^4+2x-x^3-1+3x^4-3x^3=5x^4-4x^3+2x-1$.

Javob: $5x^4-4x^3+2x-1$.

### 13 [A]
Avval soddalaştiramiz: $x(x-2)(x+2)=x(x^2-4)=x^3-4x$.
$f'(x)=3x^2-4$.

Javob: $3x^2-4$.

### 14 [D]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$: $f'(x)=2(x^2+1)+(2x+3)\cdot2x$.
$f'(2)=2\cdot5+7\cdot4=10+28=38$.

Javob: $38$.

### 15 [C]
$f(x)=x^{\frac32}-3x^{\frac12}$, $f'(x)=\dfrac32\sqrt x-\dfrac{3}{2\sqrt x}$.
$f'(4)=\dfrac32\cdot2-\dfrac{3}{4}=3-\dfrac34=\dfrac94$.

Javob: $\dfrac{9}{4}$.

### 16 [D]
Bölinma hosilasi: $\left(\dfrac uv\right)'=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$.
$$y'=\dfrac{1\cdot(x-1)-(x+1)\cdot1}{(x-1)^2}=\dfrac{-2}{(x-1)^2}.$$

Javob: $-\dfrac{2}{(x-1)^2}$.

### 17 [A]
Bölinma hosilasi formulasi bilan:
$$f'(x)=\dfrac{(2x-1)-x\cdot2}{(2x-1)^2}=-\dfrac{1}{(2x-1)^2}.$$
$f'(2)=-\dfrac{1}{3^2}=-\dfrac19$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{9}$.

### 18 [C]
$(\log_a x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln a}$, $(x^2)'=2x$.
$y'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln2}-2x$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{x\ln2}-2x$.

### 19 [A]
Körsatkiçli funksiya hosilasi: $(a^x)'=a^x\ln a$; $(x^3)'=3x^2$.
$y'=10^x\ln10+3x^2$.

Javob: $10^x\ln10+3x^2$.

### 20 [D]
$(\ln x)'=\dfrac1x$, $(e^x)'=e^x$.
$y'=\dfrac1x+e^x$.

Javob: $\dfrac1x+e^x$.

## 2-test

### 1 [B]
$(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$ formulasi bilan:
$$f'(x)=9\cdot\dfrac23x^{\frac23-1}=6x^{-\frac13}.$$

Javob: $6x^{-\frac13}$.

### 2 [C]
$n=-\dfrac13$ uçun $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$:
$$f'(x)=9\cdot\left(-\dfrac13\right)x^{-\frac13-1}=-3x^{-\frac43}.$$

Javob: $-3x^{-\frac43}$.

### 3 [A]
$5\sqrt[5]{x^3}=5x^{\frac35}$.
$$f'(x)=5\cdot\dfrac35x^{\frac35-1}=3x^{-\frac25}=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt[5]{x^2}}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt[5]{x^2}}$.

### 4 [B]
Har bir hadning hosilasi: $(2x^3)'=6x^2$, $(3x^2)'=6x$, $\left(\dfrac12x\right)'=\dfrac12$, $5'=0$.
$f'(x)=6x^2-6x+\dfrac12$.

Javob: $6x^2-6x+\dfrac12$.

### 5 [A]
$\sqrt2$ va $\dfrac{1}{\ln2}$ — özgarmas köpaytuvçilar.
$(\sqrt2x^2)'=2\sqrt2x$, $\left(\dfrac{1}{\ln2}x\right)'=\dfrac{1}{\ln2}$, $(4x^4)'=16x^3$.
$f'(x)=2\sqrt2x-\dfrac{1}{\ln2}+16x^3$.

Javob: $2\sqrt2x-\dfrac{1}{\ln2}+16x^3$.

### 6 [A]
$c$ — özgarmas son, $x$ böyiça hosila olamiz.
$\left(\dfrac xc\right)'=\dfrac1c$, $(4x^4)'=16x^3$, $(c^3x^2)'=2c^3x$.
$f'(x)=\dfrac1c-16x^3+2c^3x$.

Javob: $\dfrac1c-16x^3+2c^3x$.

### 7 [D]
Soddalaştiramiz: $\dfrac{x^3}{\sqrt x}=x^{\frac52}$.
$(x^{\frac52})'=\dfrac52x^{\frac32}=\dfrac52x\sqrt x$, $(4\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt x}$, $\left(\dfrac x3\right)'=\dfrac13$.
$y'=\dfrac52x\sqrt x-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt x}+\dfrac13$.

Javob: $\dfrac52x\sqrt x-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt x}+\dfrac13$.

### 8 [C]
Daraja körinişida: $y=x^{-1}+x^{-2}-\dfrac13x^{-3}$.
$y'=-x^{-2}-2x^{-3}-\dfrac13\cdot(-3)x^{-4}=-\dfrac{1}{x^2}-\dfrac{2}{x^3}+\dfrac{1}{x^4}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{x^2}-\dfrac{2}{x^3}+\dfrac{1}{x^4}$.

### 9 [D]
$f'(x)=3x^2-6x$.
$f'(3)=27-18=9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 10 [A]
$f(x)=2x^{\frac32}-3x^{-2}+5$, $f'(x)=3\sqrt x+\dfrac{6}{x^3}$.
$f'(4)=3\cdot2+\dfrac{6}{64}=6+\dfrac{3}{32}=\dfrac{195}{32}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{195}{32}$.

### 11 [C]
$f'(x)=2x+5$.
$2x_0+5=0 \Rightarrow x_0=-\dfrac52$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{5}{2}$.

### 12 [B]
Köpaytma hosilasi $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$:
$f'(x)=2x(x^2+x+1)+(x^2+1)(2x+1)$
$=2x^3+2x^2+2x+2x^3+x^2+2x+1=4x^3+3x^2+4x+1$.

Javob: $4x^3+3x^2+4x+1$.

### 13 [B]
Kublar yiğindisi formulasi: $(x+1)(x^2-x+1)=x^3+1$.
$f'(x)=3x^2$.

Javob: $3x^2$.

### 14 [D]
$u=1+2x-3x^2$, $v=5x^2-4x+\dfrac12$; $u'=2-6x$, $v'=10x-4$.
$x_0=0$ da: $u=1$, $v=\dfrac12$, $u'=2$, $v'=-4$.
$f'(0)=u'v+uv'=2\cdot\dfrac12+1\cdot(-4)=1-4=-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 15 [A]
$f(x)=x^{\frac52}+x^{\frac12}$, $f'(x)=\dfrac52x^{\frac32}+\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$.
$f'(1)=\dfrac52+\dfrac12=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 16 [A]
Bölinma hosilasi, $(\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$:
$$y'=\dfrac{\frac{1}{2\sqrt x}(\sqrt x+1)-\sqrt x\cdot\frac{1}{2\sqrt x}}{(\sqrt x+1)^2}=\dfrac{\frac{1}{2\sqrt x}}{(\sqrt x+1)^2}=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x(\sqrt x+1)^2}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x(\sqrt x+1)^2}$.

### 17 [C]
$\left(\dfrac uv\right)'=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$:
$$f'(x)=\dfrac{2(3-x)-(2x-1)(-1)}{(3-x)^2}=\dfrac{5}{(3-x)^2}.$$
$f'(2)=\dfrac{5}{1}=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 18 [D]
$(\lg x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln10}$, $(\ln x)'=\dfrac1x$.
$y'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln10}+\dfrac1x=\dfrac{1+\ln10}{x\ln10}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1+\ln10}{x\ln10}$.

### 19 [B]
$(a^x)'=a^x\ln a$, $(\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$.
$y'=3^x\ln3+\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$.

Javob: $3^x\ln3+\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$.

### 20 [C]
$(x^2)'=2x$, $(\ln x)'=\dfrac1x$, $(e^x)'=e^x$.
$y'=2x+\dfrac1x+e^x$.

Javob: $2x+\dfrac1x+e^x$.

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
$(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$:
$$f'(x)=\dfrac57\cdot\dfrac{14}{5}x^{\frac{14}{5}-1}=2x^{\frac95}.$$

Javob: $2x^{\frac95}$.

### 2 [A]
$n=-\dfrac32$ uçun:
$$f'(x)=6\cdot\left(-\dfrac32\right)x^{-\frac32-1}=-9x^{-\frac52}.$$

Javob: $-9x^{-\frac52}$.

### 3 [D]
$f(x)=2x^{-3}$, $(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$:
$f'(x)=2\cdot(-3)x^{-4}=-\dfrac{6}{x^4}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{6}{x^4}$.

### 4 [C]
$\left(\dfrac{x^4}{4}\right)'=x^3$, $(x^3)'=3x^2$, $(3x^2)'=6x$, $7'=0$.
$f'(x)=x^3-3x^2+6x$.

Javob: $x^3-3x^2+6x$.

### 5 [A]
$\ln3$ — özgarmas köpaytuvçi.
$\left(\dfrac x3\right)'=\dfrac13$, $(4x^4)'=16x^3$, $(x^2\ln3)'=2x\ln3$.
$f'(x)=\dfrac13-16x^3+2x\ln3$.

Javob: $\dfrac13-16x^3+2x\ln3$.

### 6 [D]
$\sqrt a$ va $\ln b$ — özgarmaslar, $\dfrac14$ ning hosilasi $0$.
$(\sqrt a\,x)'=\sqrt a$, $(x^5\ln b)'=5x^4\ln b$.
$f'(x)=\sqrt a+5x^4\ln b$.

Javob: $\sqrt a+5x^4\ln b$.

### 7 [B]
$x^2\sqrt x=x^{\frac52}$, $(x^{\frac52})'=\dfrac52x\sqrt x$.
$(6\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{6}{2\sqrt x}=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt x}$, $(2x)'=2$.
$y'=\dfrac52x\sqrt x+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt x}-2$.

Javob: $\dfrac52x\sqrt x+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt x}-2$.

### 8 [D]
$y=\pi x^{-2}+x^{\frac54}$.
$y'=-2\pi x^{-3}+\dfrac54x^{\frac14}=-\dfrac{2\pi}{x^3}+\dfrac54\sqrt[4]{x}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{2\pi}{x^3}+\dfrac54\sqrt[4]{x}$.

### 9 [C]
$f(x)=2x^2-3x^{-2}$, $f'(x)=4x+\dfrac{6}{x^3}$.
$f'(-1)=-4+\dfrac{6}{-1}=-4-6=-10$.

Javob: $-10$.

### 10 [C]
$f'(x)=\dfrac{4}{2\sqrt x}-2x=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt x}-2x$.
$f'(4)=\dfrac22-8=-7$.

Javob: $-7$.

### 11 [A]
$f'(x)=8x-6$.
$8x_0-6=0 \Rightarrow x_0=\dfrac34$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

### 12 [D]
Qavsni oçamiz: $f(x)=x^{\frac52}+x^{\frac32}+x^{\frac12}$.
$f'(x)=\dfrac52x^{\frac32}+\dfrac32x^{\frac12}+\dfrac12x^{-\frac12}$.
Umumiy maxraj $2\sqrt x$: $f'(x)=\dfrac{5x^2+3x+1}{2\sqrt x}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5x^2+3x+1}{2\sqrt x}$.

### 13 [C]
Kvadratlar ayirmasi: $(x-\sqrt2)(x+\sqrt2)=x^2-2$, demak $f(x)=x^4-2x^2$.
$f'(x)=4x^3-4x$.

Javob: $4x^3-4x$.

### 14 [A]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$: $f'(x)=(2x-2)(3x-1)+3(x^2-2x+3)$.
$f'(1)=0\cdot2+3\cdot2=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 15 [B]
$f(x)=x^{\frac43}-2x^{\frac13}$, $f'(x)=\dfrac43x^{\frac13}-\dfrac23x^{-\frac23}$.
$f'(1)=\dfrac43-\dfrac23=\dfrac23$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3}$.

### 16 [D]
Bölinma hosilasi formulasi:
$$f'(x)=\dfrac{(2x+3)(2x-1)-2(x^2+3x+4)}{(2x-1)^2}.$$
Suratda: $4x^2+4x-3-2x^2-6x-8=2x^2-2x-11$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2x^2-2x-11}{(2x-1)^2}$.

### 17 [D]
$$f'(x)=\dfrac{2x(x+1)-x^2}{(x+1)^2}=\dfrac{x^2+2x}{(x+1)^2}.$$
$f'(2)=\dfrac{4+4}{9}=\dfrac89$.

Javob: $\dfrac{8}{9}$.

### 18 [B]
$(x^2)'=2x$, $(\ln x)'=\dfrac1x$, $(\log_3x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln3}$.
$y'=2x+\dfrac1x-\dfrac{1}{x\ln3}$.

Javob: $2x+\dfrac1x-\dfrac{1}{x\ln3}$.

### 19 [A]
$(x^5)'=5x^4$, $(5^x)'=5^x\ln5$.
$y'=5x^4+5^x\ln5$.

Javob: $5x^4+5^x\ln5$.

### 20 [B]
$(e^x)'=e^x$, $(\ln x)'=\dfrac1x$.
$y'=3e^x-\dfrac2x$.

Javob: $3e^x-\dfrac2x$.
