# 64. Logarifmik tenglamalar

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Logarifm ta'rifi: $a^x=b\Leftrightarrow x=\log_ab$.
$2^x=11\Rightarrow x=\log_211$.

Javob: $\log_211$.

### 2 [C]
$\log_3x=3\Rightarrow x=3^3=27$.

Javob: $27$.

### 3 [A]
$2x-1=3^2=9\Rightarrow x=5$ ($2x-1>0$ bajariladi).

Javob: $5$.

### 4 [D]
$\log_a t=0\Leftrightarrow t=1$: $(x-4)^2=1$.
$x-4=\pm1\Rightarrow x=3$ yoki $x=5$.

Javob: $\{3;5\}$.

### 5 [B]
Ta'rif böyiça $\log_3\dfrac13+\log_3x=3$.
$-1+\log_3x=3\Rightarrow\log_3x=4\Rightarrow x=81$.

Javob: $81$.

### 6 [A]
Taşqi logarifmdan ketma-ket oçamiz: $\log_2(\log_3(x+2))=1$.
$\log_3(x+2)=2\Rightarrow x+2=9\Rightarrow x=7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 7 [C]
Şartlar: $x-2>0$, $x-2\ne1$. Ta'rifdan $(x-2)^2=5x-14$.
$x^2-9x+18=0\Rightarrow x=3$ yoki $x=6$.
$x=3$ da asos $1$ ga teng — mos emas.

Javob: $6$.

### 8 [B]
Asosiy ayniyat $a^{\log_ab}=b$ ($b>0$): $x+4=10\Rightarrow x=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 9 [D]
Aniqlaniş sohasi $x>1$.
$x=3$, $x=5$ yoki $\log_3(x-1)=0\Rightarrow x=2$. Hammasi sohaga kiradi.

Javob: $\{2;3;5\}$.

### 10 [A]
Aniqlaniş sohasi $x>1$.
$x-5=0\Rightarrow x=5$ yoki $\log_2(x-1)=0\Rightarrow x=2$.

Javob: $\{2;5\}$.

### 11 [C]
$t=\log_3x$: $t^2-3t+2=0\Rightarrow t=1$ yoki $t=2$.
$x=3$ yoki $x=9$.

Javob: $\{3;9\}$.

### 12 [A]
$u=\dfrac{\lg x+3}{\lg x}$ desak, ikkinçi had $\dfrac4u$: $u+\dfrac4u=5\Rightarrow u=1$ yoki $u=4$.
$u=1$ bölişi mumkin emas ($3\ne0$). $u=4$: $\lg x+3=4\lg x\Rightarrow\lg x=1$.

Javob: $10$.

### 13 [B]
Ta'rif: $3^{2x}+18=3^{x+2}=9\cdot3^x$.
$t=3^x$: $t^2-9t+18=0\Rightarrow t=3$ yoki $t=6$.
$x=1$ yoki $x=\log_36$.

Javob: $\{1;\log_36\}$.

### 14 [B]
$3\cdot3^{2x}+6=9\cdot3^x$. $t=3^x$: $3t^2-9t+6=0\Rightarrow t^2-3t+2=0$.
$t=1$ yoki $t=2$, ya'ni $x=0$ yoki $x=\log_32$.

Javob: $\{0;\log_32\}$.

### 15 [D]
Şart $x>0$. Yiğindi formulasi: $\log_3(x(x+8))=2\Rightarrow x^2+8x=9$.
$x^2+8x-9=0\Rightarrow x=1$ yoki $x=-9$; faqat $x=1$ mos.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [D]
$1=\log_33$, demak $\log_3\dfrac{x-2}{x}=\log_3\dfrac{3(x-5)}{x}$.
$x-2=3x-15\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{13}{2}$.
Tekşiruv: $\dfrac{x-2}{x}>0$, $\dfrac{x-5}{x}>0$ — bajariladi.

Javob: $\dfrac{13}{2}$.

### 17 [A]
$\log_9x=\dfrac12\log_3x$, demak $\dfrac32\log_3x=6\Rightarrow\log_3x=4$.
$x=81$.

Javob: $81$.

### 18 [D]
$t=x^2-7x+19>0$, $L=\log_3t$: $L+\dfrac L2+\dfrac L4=\dfrac74L=\dfrac72\Rightarrow L=2$.
$t=9$: $x^2-7x+10=0\Rightarrow x=2$ yoki $x=5$.

Javob: $\{2;5\}$.

### 19 [C]
Ikkala tomonni önli logarifmlaymiz, $t=\lg x$: $(t-2)t=15$.
$t^2-2t-15=0\Rightarrow t=5$ yoki $t=-3$.

Javob: $\{10^5;10^{-3}\}$.

### 20 [B]
Logarifmlaymiz: $(3\lg x+3)\lg x=0$.
$\lg x=0\Rightarrow x=1$; $\lg x=-1\Rightarrow x=10^{-1}$.

Javob: $\{1;10^{-1}\}$.

### 21 [C]
$u=|x-3|>0$. $\log_{\sqrt2}u=2\log_2u$. Ikkala tomonni $\log_2$ ga olamiz:
$2\log_2u\cdot\log_2u=\log_24=2\Rightarrow\log_2^2u=1\Rightarrow u=2$ yoki $u=\dfrac12$.
$|x-3|=2\Rightarrow x=1;5$; $|x-3|=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\dfrac52;\dfrac72$.

Javob: $\Bigl\{1;\dfrac52;\dfrac72;5\Bigr\}$.

### 22 [C]
$L=\log_3x$: $\log_9(27x)=\dfrac{3+L}{2}$, $\log_{27}(9x)=\dfrac{2+L}{3}$.
$(3+L)(2+L)=30\Rightarrow L^2+5L-24=0\Rightarrow L=3$ yoki $L=-8$.

Javob: $\{3^{-8};3^3\}$.

### 23 [D]
Birinçi tenglamadan $xy=9$. Ikkinçisini bölamiz: $\dfrac{x^2y}{xy}=x=3$.
$y=3$.

Javob: $\{(3;3)\}$.

### 24 [C]
$2^{x^2-xy}=1\Rightarrow x(x-y)=0$. $x=0$ da $13x-1<0$ — mos emas, demak $y=x$.
$\log_2(x+3)^2=\log_2(13x-1)\Rightarrow x^2+6x+9=13x-1\Rightarrow x^2-7x+10=0$.
$x=2$ yoki $x=5$.

Javob: $\{(2;2);(5;5)\}$.

### 25 [D]
$\log_2x$ ösuvçi, $-x+4$ kamayuvçi — grafiklar köpi bilan bir marta kesişadi.
$x=1$ da $0<3$, $x=4$ da $2>0$, demak kesişiş bor.

Javob: $1$.

### 26 [A]
$s=-x>0$ desak: $\log_3s=s^2+2$.
Lekin $\log_3s<s<s^2+2$ barça $s>0$ uçun, demak tenglik bajarilmaydi.

Javob: $0$.

### 27 [B]
$2\log_4x=\log_2x=t$: $\sqrt{1+t}+\sqrt{9-t}=4$.
Kvadratga kötaramiz: $10+2\sqrt{(1+t)(9-t)}=16\Rightarrow(1+t)(9-t)=9$.
$t(8-t)=0\Rightarrow t=0$ yoki $t=8$, ya'ni $x=1$ yoki $x=256$.

Javob: $\{1;256\}$.

### 28 [D]
Arifmetik kvadrat ildiz manfiy bölmaydi, $\sqrt{\dots}=-1$ ning yeçimi yöq.

Javob: $\varnothing$.

### 29 [D]
$u=\log_{4x}(x+1)$: $u+\dfrac1u=2\Rightarrow u=1$.
$4x=x+1\Rightarrow x=\dfrac13$ (asos $\dfrac43\ne1$).

Javob: $\dfrac13$.

### 30 [A]
$u=\log_{x+2}(x+8)$, ikkinçi had $\dfrac1u+1$: $u+\dfrac1u=\dfrac52\Rightarrow u=2$ yoki $u=\dfrac12$.
$u=2$: $(x+2)^2=x+8\Rightarrow x^2+3x-4=0\Rightarrow x=1$ ($x=-4$ mos emas).
$u=\dfrac12$: $(x+8)^2=x+2$ — diskriminant manfiy.

Javob: $1$.

### 31 [B]
Yiğindi: $\log_x\Bigl(\dfrac{6x}{x+3}\cdot(x+3)\Bigr)=\log_x(6x)=1+\log_x6=2$.
$\log_x6=1\Rightarrow x=6$ — bitta ildiz.

Javob: $1$.

### 32 [A]
$\dfrac12\log_5x^2=\log_5|x|$, şartlar $x>-4$, $x\ne0$: $(x+4)|x|=5$.
$x>0$: $x^2+4x-5=0\Rightarrow x=1$.
$-4<x<0$: $x^2+4x+5=0$ — yeçim yöq.

Javob: $1$.

### 33 [B]
$xy=15$, $x+y=64^{\frac12}=8$.
Yeçimlar $(3;5)$ va $(5;3)$. $x_1x_2=3\cdot5=15$.

Javob: $15$.
