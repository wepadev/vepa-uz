Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}}{2}$.

$\operatorname{ctg}15^\circ=\dfrac{1+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}{\frac12}=2+\sqrt3$.

Javob: $2+\sqrt3$.

### 4 [C]

$\sin^2\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{1-\frac{12}{13}}{2}=\dfrac1{26}$, $\sin\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{26}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{26}}{26}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{26}}{26}$.

### 10 [D]

### 11 [C]

### 13 [B]

### 15 [B]

### 19 [B]
