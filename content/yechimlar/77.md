# 77. Trigonometrik tenglamalar (2-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Hammasini bir tomonga ötkazib, sinuslar ayirmasi formulasini qöllaymiz: $\sin7x-\sin x=2\cos4x\sin3x=0$.
$\sin3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$; $\cos4x=0\Rightarrow4x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 2 [A]
Sinuslar yiğindisi: $\sin5x+\sin x=2\sin3x\cos2x=0$.
$\sin3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$; $\cos2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 3 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}5x=\operatorname{tg}x\Rightarrow5x=x+\pi k\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi k}{4}$, bunda $\cos x\ne0$, $\cos5x\ne0$.
$k=4m$ da $x=\pi m$ — yaraydi; $k=4m+2$ da $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi m$ — tangens aniqlanmagan.
$k$ toq bölganda $x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$ — yaraydi.

Javob: $\pi n; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 4 [B]
$\sin x+\sin3x=2\sin2x\cos x$, demak $2\sin2x\cos x=2\sin2x\Rightarrow\sin2x(\cos x-1)=0$.
$\sin2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$; $\cos x=1$ ning yeçimlari $2\pi n$ bu tüplamga kiradi.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 5 [C]
$\cos x+\cos5x=2\cos3x\cos2x$, demak tenglama $\cos3x(2\cos2x+1)=0$.
$\cos3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.
$\cos2x=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow2x=\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 6 [A]
$\cos2x=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x\right)$, ya'ni $\sin4x=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x\right)$.
$4x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.
$4x=\pi-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 7 [B]
$-\operatorname{ctg}x=\operatorname{tg}\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, demak $\operatorname{tg}4x=\operatorname{tg}\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.
$4x=x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.
Bu nuqtalarda $\operatorname{tg}4x$ va $\operatorname{ctg}x$ aniqlangan.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 8 [D]
Ikkiga bölamiz: $\dfrac12\sin x+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos x=\dfrac12$, ya'ni $\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)=\dfrac12$.
$x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$ yoki $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$; $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 9 [C]
$\sin5x-\sin3x=2\cos4x\sin x=0$.
$\sin x=0$: $[0;\pi]$ da $0$ va $\pi$ — $2$ ta.
$\cos4x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi k}{4}$: $\dfrac{\pi}{8},\dfrac{3\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{8},\dfrac{7\pi}{8}$ — $4$ ta. Jami $6$ ta.

Javob: $6$.

### 10 [A]
$\sin3x-\sin x=2\cos2x\sin x=0$.
$\sin x=0$: $0,\pi,2\pi$ (yiğindi $3\pi$).
$\cos2x=0$: $\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{3\pi}{4},\dfrac{5\pi}{4},\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ (yiğindi $4\pi$). Jami $7\pi$.

Javob: $7\pi$.

### 11 [D]
$\cos2x=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x\right)$: $6x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$ yoki $6x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.
$\left(0;\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ da ildizlar: $\dfrac{\pi}{16},\dfrac{\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{16}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{5\pi}{16}=\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$.

### 12 [A]
$\sin2x+\cos2x=\sqrt2\sin\left(2x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$, demak $\sin\left(2x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$2x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$ yoki $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n$, ya'ni $x=\dfrac{\pi}{24}+\pi n$ yoki $x=\dfrac{5\pi}{24}+\pi n$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da: $\dfrac{\pi}{24},\dfrac{25\pi}{24},\dfrac{5\pi}{24},\dfrac{29\pi}{24}$, yiğindi $\dfrac{60\pi}{24}=\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$.

### 13 [B]
Surat nol: $\sin x=\dfrac12$, ya'ni $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$ yoki $x=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
Maxraj nol bölmasligi kerak: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$ da $\cos x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ — çiqarilib taşlanadi.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.

### 14 [D]
Bir jinsli tenglama, $\cos x=0$ yeçim emas, $\cos^2x$ ga bölamiz: $2\operatorname{tg}^2x-3\operatorname{tg}x-5=0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=\dfrac{3\pm7}{4}$: $\operatorname{tg}x=\dfrac52$ yoki $\operatorname{tg}x=-1$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \operatorname{arctg}\dfrac52+\pi n$.

### 15 [D]
Daraja pasaytiriş: $\dfrac{1+\cos4x}{2}+\dfrac{1+\cos8x}{2}=1\Rightarrow\cos4x+\cos8x=0$.
$2\cos6x\cos2x=0$: $\cos6x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}$; $\cos2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$ — bular birinçi tüplamga kiradi.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}$.

### 16 [B]
Köpaytmani yiğindiga keltiramiz: $\dfrac12(\cos2x+\cos8x)=\dfrac12(\cos4x+\cos8x)$, ya'ni $\cos4x-\cos2x=0$.
$-2\sin3x\sin x=0$. $\sin3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$ ($\sin x=0$ ning yeçimlari ham şu yerda).

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 17 [D]
$\cos7x\cos3x=\dfrac12(\cos4x+\cos10x)$, $\cos^2 5x=\dfrac12(1+\cos10x)$.
Tenglama: $\cos4x=1\Rightarrow4x=2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 18 [C]
$\cos^2 2x-\sin^2 2x=\cos4x$, demak $\cos3x+\cos5x=\cos4x$, ya'ni $2\cos4x\cos x=\cos4x$.
$\cos4x(2\cos x-1)=0$.
$\cos4x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$; $\cos x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

### 19 [D]
Logarifm ta'rifi: $\cos x=\sin x$, asos şarti $0<\sin x\ne1$, va $\cos x>0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=1$ va $\sin x>0$, $\cos x>0$ — I çorak: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

### 20 [A]
$t=\cos x$, öng tomon $t+\dfrac32>0$. Kvadratga oşiramiz: $3+2t=t^2+3t+\dfrac94\Rightarrow t^2+t-\dfrac34=0$.
$t=\dfrac12$ yoki $t=-\dfrac32$ (yaroqsiz).
$\cos x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

### 21 [C]
$u=9^{\cos^2x}$, unda $9^{\sin^2x}=9^{1-\cos^2x}=\dfrac9u$.
$u+\dfrac9u=6\Rightarrow(u-3)^2=0\Rightarrow u=3\Rightarrow\cos^2x=\dfrac12$.
$\cos2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 22 [B]
$\sin^3x=4\sin x\cos x\Rightarrow\sin x(\sin^2x-4\cos x)=0$.
$\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=\pi n$.
$1-\cos^2x-4\cos x=0\Rightarrow\cos^2x+4\cos x-1=0\Rightarrow\cos x=-2\pm\sqrt5$; faqat $\sqrt5-2$ yaraydi.

Javob: $\pi n; \pm\arccos(\sqrt5-2)+2\pi n$.

### 23 [D]
Ikkala tomonni $2$ ga bölib, kosinus ayirmasi körinişiga keltiramiz: $\cos\left(4x-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\cos\left(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$.
$4x-\dfrac{\pi}{6}=2x-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\pi n$.
$4x-\dfrac{\pi}{6}=-2x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 24 [C]
$t=\sin x+\cos x$, $t^2=1+\sin2x$. Tenglama: $t^2=\sqrt2t\Rightarrow t=0$ yoki $t=\sqrt2$.
$t=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{tg}x=-1\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
$\sqrt2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\sqrt2\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

### 25 [C]
$t=\sin x+\cos x$, $\sin2x=t^2-1$: $t^2-3t+2=0\Rightarrow t=1$ yoki $t=2$ ($|t|\le\sqrt2$, yaroqsiz).
$\sqrt2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=1\Rightarrow x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$ yoki $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.
$x=2\pi n$; $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 26 [A]
$\cos2x=(\cos x-\sin x)(\cos x+\sin x)$, demak $(\cos x-\sin x)(\cos x+\sin x-1)=0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=1$: $[0;\pi]$ da $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
$\sin x+\cos x=1$: $x=0$ va $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Jami $3$ ta.

Javob: $3$.

### 27 [C]
Arifmetik progressiyada örta had qöşnilarning örta arifmetigi: $2=\cos^2x+2\cos x$.
$\cos^2x+2\cos x-2=0\Rightarrow\cos x=-1\pm\sqrt3$; faqat $\sqrt3-1$ yaraydi.

Javob: $\pm\arccos(\sqrt3-1)+2\pi n$.

## 2-test

### 1 [A]
Kosinuslar ayirmasi: $\cos7x-\cos3x=-2\sin5x\sin2x=0$.
$\sin5x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$; $\sin2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{5}; \dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 2 [C]
Kosinuslar yiğindisi: $\cos7x+\cos3x=2\cos5x\cos2x=0$.
$\cos5x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$; $\cos2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi n}{5}; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 3 [A]
$\operatorname{ctg}4x=\operatorname{ctg}x\Rightarrow4x=x+\pi k\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi k}{3}$, bunda $\sin x\ne0$.
$k$ uçga karrali bölsa $\sin x=0$ — çiqarilib taşlanadi. Qolgani: $x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 4 [D]
$\sin x-\sin7x=-2\cos4x\sin3x$, demak $\sin3x(2\cos4x+\sqrt3)=0$.
$\sin3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.
$\cos4x=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Rightarrow4x=\pm\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{5\pi}{24}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \pm\dfrac{5\pi}{24}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 5 [B]
$\sin9x-\sin x=2\cos5x\sin4x$, demak $2\cos5x\sin4x=\sin4x\Rightarrow\sin4x(2\cos5x-1)=0$.
$\sin4x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.
$\cos5x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{15}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{4}; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{15}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{5}$.

### 6 [B]
$-\sin2x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2x\right)$, demak $\cos6x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2x\right)$.
$6x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.
$6x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; -\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 7 [D]
$\operatorname{ctg}x=\operatorname{tg}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$: $5x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}$.
Bu nuqtalarda $\operatorname{tg}5x$ va $\operatorname{ctg}x$ aniqlangan.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}$.

### 8 [C]
$\cos x-\sin x=\sqrt2\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$, demak $\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.
$x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$: $x=2\pi n$; $x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $2\pi n; -\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 9 [D]
$-\sin x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+x\right)$: $5x=\pm\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+x\right)+2\pi n$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$: $[0;\pi]$ da $\dfrac{\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{8}$.
$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$: $\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{7\pi}{12},\dfrac{11\pi}{12}$. Jami $5$ ta.

Javob: $5$.

### 10 [A]
$\cos x=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$: $3x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$ yoki $3x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
$[0;\pi]$ da: $\dfrac{\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{8},\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{5\pi}{8}+\dfrac{2\pi}{8}=\pi$.

Javob: $\pi$.

### 11 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}x=\operatorname{tg}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$: $3x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$ (bu nuqtalarda funksiyalar aniqlangan).
Eng kiçik musbat ildiz $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$, eng katta manfiy $-\dfrac{\pi}{8}$.
Köpaytma: $-\dfrac{\pi^2}{64}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi^{2}}{64}$.

### 12 [B]
$\sqrt3\sin x+\cos x=2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$, demak $\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ yoki $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ ($+2\pi n$): $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ yoki $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da faqat şu ikkitasi, yiğindi $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

### 13 [D]
Surat nol: $\cos x=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
$x=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ da $\sin x=\dfrac12$, maxraj nol — çiqarilib taşlanadi.
Qoladi $x=-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n=\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.

### 14 [C]
$1=\sin^2x+\cos^2x$: $\sin^2x+4\cos^2x-5\sin x\cos x=0$. $\cos^2x$ ga bölamiz: $\operatorname{tg}^2x-5\operatorname{tg}x+4=0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=1$ yoki $\operatorname{tg}x=4$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \operatorname{arctg}4+\pi n$.

### 15 [D]
Daraja pasaytiramiz: $\dfrac32-\dfrac12(\cos2x+\cos4x+\cos6x)=\dfrac32\Rightarrow\cos2x+\cos4x+\cos6x=0$.
$\cos2x+\cos6x=2\cos4x\cos2x$, demak $\cos4x(2\cos2x+1)=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$; $\cos2x=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 16 [A]
$\sin6x\cos2x=\dfrac12(\sin8x+\sin4x)$, $\sin3x\cos5x=\dfrac12(\sin8x-\sin2x)$.
Tenglama: $\sin4x+\sin2x=0\Rightarrow2\sin3x\cos x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$; $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.

### 17 [C]
$2\sin x\cos4x=\sin5x+\sin(-3x)=\sin5x-\sin3x$.
Tenglama: $\sin5x=\sin5x-\sin3x\Rightarrow\sin3x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 18 [D]
$\sin3x\sin9x=\dfrac12(\cos6x-\cos12x)$, $\sin^2 6x=\dfrac12(1-\cos12x)$.
Tenglama: $\cos6x=1\Rightarrow6x=2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 19 [B]
Ta'rif böyiça $2\sin x=\cos x$, şartlar: $0<\cos x\ne1$, $2\sin x>0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=\dfrac12$, $\sin x>0$, $\cos x>0$ — I çorak.
$x=\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac12+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac12+2\pi n$.

### 20 [B]
$s=\sin x$, öng tomon $s+2>0$. Kvadratga oşiramiz: $4-2s^2=s^2+4s+4\Rightarrow3s^2+4s=0$.
$s=0$ yoki $s=-\dfrac43$ (yaroqsiz).
$\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=\pi n$.

Javob: $\pi n$.

### 21 [D]
$\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2x\right)=\sin2x$, $4^{\sin x}=2^{2\sin x}$. Daraja körsatkiçlarini tenglaymiz: $2\sin x=\sin2x$.
$2\sin x=2\sin x\cos x\Rightarrow\sin x(1-\cos x)=0$.
$\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=\pi n$ ($\cos x=1$ ham şu yerga kiradi).

Javob: $\pi n$.

### 22 [C]
Guruhlaymiz: $2\cos x(\operatorname{tg}x-1)-(\operatorname{tg}x-1)=0\Rightarrow(\operatorname{tg}x-1)(2\cos x-1)=0$.
$\operatorname{tg}x=1\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$; $\cos x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

### 23 [A]
$\operatorname{ctg}^2x=\dfrac1{\sin^2x}-1$, $u=\sin^2x$: $\dfrac1u-1+4u=3\Rightarrow4u^2-4u+1=0$.
$u=\dfrac12$, ya'ni $\sin^2x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\cos2x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 24 [D]
$1-\sin2x=(\cos x-\sin x)^2$, $t=\cos x-\sin x$: $t^2=t\Rightarrow t=0$ yoki $t=1$.
$t=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{tg}x=1\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
$\sqrt2\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=1\Rightarrow x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=2\pi n$ yoki $x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; 2\pi n; -\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 25 [B]
$t=\sin x-\cos x$, $\sin2x=1-t^2$: $1-t^2+2t-1=0\Rightarrow t(2-t)=0$.
$t=2$ mumkin emas ($|t|\le\sqrt2$), demak $t=0$: $\operatorname{tg}x=1$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 26 [C]
$2\sin^2 2x=1-\cos4x$: $\cos^2 4x-\cos4x=0\Rightarrow\cos4x=0$ yoki $\cos4x=1$.
$\cos4x=0$: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi k}{4}$, $[0;\pi]$ da $4$ ta.
$\cos4x=1$: $x=\dfrac{\pi k}{2}$, ya'ni $0,\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi$ — $3$ ta. Jami $7$ ta.

Javob: $7$.

### 27 [A]
Örta had: $2\cdot\dfrac14=\sin^2x+\cos2x=\sin^2x+1-2\sin^2x=1-\sin^2x$.
$\sin^2x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\cos2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
Kosinuslar ayirmasi: $\cos8x-\cos2x=-2\sin5x\sin3x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$; $x=\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi n}{5}$.

### 2 [C]
Sinuslar yiğindisi: $\sin7x+\sin3x=2\sin5x\cos2x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$; $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{5}; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 3 [B]
$5x=3x+\pi k\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi k}{2}$, bunda $\sin5x\ne0$, $\sin3x\ne0$.
$k$ juft bölsa $x=\pi m$, sinuslar nol — çiqarilib taşlanadi. $k$ toq: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$ — yaraydi.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.

### 4 [D]
$\sin5x-\sin x=2\cos3x\sin2x$, demak $\cos3x(2\sin2x-\sqrt3)=0$.
$\cos3x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.
$\sin2x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Rightarrow2x=(-1)^n\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=(-1)^n\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; (-1)^n\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 5 [A]
$\cos3x-\cos x=-2\sin2x\sin x$, demak $\sin2x(1-2\sin x)=0$.
$\sin2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$; $\sin x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=(-1)^n\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; (-1)^n\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

### 6 [C]
$\cos3x=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-3x\right)$: $5x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-3x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.
Yoki $5x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+3x+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 7 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}x=\operatorname{ctg}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$: $3x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.
Bu nuqtalarda $\operatorname{ctg}3x$ va $\operatorname{tg}x$ aniqlangan.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 8 [B]
$\sin x-\cos x=\sqrt2\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$, demak $\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$ yoki $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.
$x=\dfrac{7\pi}{12}+2\pi n$; $x=\dfrac{11\pi}{12}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7\pi}{12}+2\pi n; \dfrac{11\pi}{12}+2\pi n$.

### 9 [B]
$\cos4x-\cos2x=-2\sin3x\sin x=0$. $\sin3x=0$ ($\sin x=0$ ham kiradi): $x=\dfrac{\pi k}{3}$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da $k=0,1,\dots,6$ — $7$ ta.

Javob: $7$.

### 10 [D]
$\cos4x=1-2\sin^2 2x$: $2\sin^2 2x+\sin2x-1=0\Rightarrow\sin2x=\dfrac12$ yoki $\sin2x=-1$.
$x\in[0;\pi]$ da $2x\in[0;2\pi]$: $2x=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\dfrac{5\pi}{6},\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, ya'ni $x=\dfrac{\pi}{12},\dfrac{5\pi}{12},\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{\pi+5\pi+9\pi}{12}=\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$.

### 11 [C]
$\sin x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$: $5x=\pm\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)+2\pi n$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$ yoki $x=-\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.
$\left(0;\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ da: $\dfrac{\pi}{12},\dfrac{3\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$. Yiğindi: $\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{5\pi}{12}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 12 [D]
$\sqrt2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=1\Rightarrow x=2\pi n$ yoki $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.
$[0;4\pi]$ da: $0,2\pi,4\pi,\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$ — $5$ ta.

Javob: $5$.

### 13 [B]
Surat nol: $\sin x=-\dfrac12$, ya'ni $x=-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$ yoki $x=\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
$x=\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ da $\operatorname{tg}x=\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$ — maxraj nol, çiqarilib taşlanadi.
$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ da $\operatorname{tg}x=-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$ — yaraydi.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.

### 14 [C]
$\sin^2x=\dfrac{1-\cos2x}{2}$, $\sin x\cos x=\dfrac12\sin2x$: $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos2x-\dfrac12\sin2x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$\sqrt3\cos2x+\sin2x=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{tg}2x=-\sqrt3\Rightarrow2x=-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.
$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$, bu $\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$ bilan bir xil tüplam.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 15 [C]
Daraja pasaytiriş: $\dfrac{1-\cos4x}{2}+\dfrac{1-\cos6x}{2}=1\Rightarrow\cos4x+\cos6x=0$.
$2\cos5x\cos x=0$: $\cos5x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$; $\cos x=0$ ning yeçimlari ($\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$) şu tüplamga kiradi.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{\pi n}{5}$.

### 16 [A]
$\sin2x\cos5x=\dfrac12(\sin7x-\sin3x)$, $\sin3x\cos4x=\dfrac12(\sin7x-\sin x)$.
Tenglama: $\sin3x=\sin x\Rightarrow2\cos2x\sin x=0$.
$x=\pi n$; $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 17 [A]
$\cos9x\cos x=\dfrac12(\cos8x+\cos10x)$, $\cos^2 5x=\dfrac12(1+\cos10x)$.
Tenglama: $\cos8x=1\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 18 [C]
$\cos^2 3x-\sin^2 3x=\cos6x$: $\sin9x-\sin3x+\cos6x=0$.
$\sin9x-\sin3x=2\cos6x\sin3x$, demak $\cos6x(2\sin3x+1)=0$.
$\cos6x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}$; $\sin3x=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{6}; (-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

### 19 [A]
Ta'rif böyiça $\sin2x=\sin x$, şartlar: $0<\sin2x\ne1$, $\sin x>0$.
$2\sin x\cos x=\sin x$, $\sin x\ne0$ bölgani uçun $\cos x=\dfrac12$.
$\sin x>0$ bölişi uçun $x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$; bunda $\sin2x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ — şart bajariladi.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

### 20 [D]
$t=\cos x\ge0$ bölişi kerak. Kvadratga oşiramiz: $2t+2=4t^2\Rightarrow2t^2-t-1=0$.
$t=1$ yoki $t=-\dfrac12$ (yaroqsiz).
$\cos x=1\Rightarrow x=2\pi n$.

Javob: $2\pi n$.

### 21 [A]
$u=4^{\cos^2x}$, $4^{\sin^2x}=\dfrac4u$: $u+\dfrac4u=5\Rightarrow u^2-5u+4=0\Rightarrow u=1$ yoki $u=4$.
$\cos^2x=0$ yoki $\cos^2x=1$, ya'ni $\sin2x=0$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 22 [C]
Guruhlaymiz: $2\cos x(\sin x+1)-(\sin x+1)=0\Rightarrow(\sin x+1)(2\cos x-1)=0$.
$\sin x=-1\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$; $\cos x=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

### 23 [A]
Formula: $\operatorname{tg}a+\operatorname{tg}b=\dfrac{\sin(a+b)}{\cos a\cos b}$, demak $\dfrac{\sin2x}{\cos(x+\frac{\pi}{4})\cos(x-\frac{\pi}{4})}=0$.
Maxraj $\dfrac12\cos2x$ ga teng. $\sin2x=0\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$, bunda $\cos2x=\pm1\ne0$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 24 [B]
$t=\sin x+\cos x$, $1+\sin2x=t^2$: $t^2=t\Rightarrow t=0$ yoki $t=1$.
$t=0\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
$\sqrt2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=1\Rightarrow x=2\pi n$ yoki $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; 2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 25 [A]
$t=\sin x+\cos x$, $\sin2x=t^2-1$: $t^2-1+t+1=0\Rightarrow t(t+1)=0$.
$t=0\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
$\sqrt2\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=-1\Rightarrow x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$ yoki $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n$, ya'ni $x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$; $x=\pi+2\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; -\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \pi+2\pi n$.

### 26 [C]
Guruhlaymiz: $\sin x(2\cos x+1)-(2\cos x+1)=0\Rightarrow(\sin x-1)(2\cos x+1)=0$.
$\sin x=1$: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. $\cos x=-\dfrac12$: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3},\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da $3$ ta ildiz.

Javob: $3$.
