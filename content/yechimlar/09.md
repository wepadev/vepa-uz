# 9. Oddiy kasrlar 2-qism

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
Köp qavatli kasrni eng pastdan yuqoriga qarab hisoblaymiz:
$3 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2}$; $\dfrac{5}{5/2} = 2$.
$4 + 2 = 6$; $\dfrac{6}{6} = 1$.
$1 + 1 = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 2 [C]
Amallarni ički qavsdan boşlab bajaramiz:
$\dfrac{3}{5}:\dfrac{9}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{10}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$; $\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
$\dfrac{2}{7} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{13}{21}$; $\dfrac{13}{21}:\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{26}{21}$.
$3 - \dfrac{26}{21} = \dfrac{37}{21}$; $\dfrac{37}{21} - \dfrac{7}{6} = \dfrac{74}{42} - \dfrac{49}{42} = \dfrac{25}{42}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{25}{42}$.

### 3 [B]
Qavslar içini umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{1}{78} + \dfrac{1}{79} = \dfrac{79 + 78}{78\cdot79} = \dfrac{157}{78\cdot79}$, $\dfrac{1}{78} - \dfrac{1}{79} = \dfrac{1}{78\cdot79}$.
Bölinma: $157$.

Javob: $157$.

### 4 [D]
Surat: $\left(2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\right):\left(\dfrac{5}{3} + \dfrac{13}{6}\right) = \dfrac{7}{4}:\dfrac{23}{6} = \dfrac{7}{4}\cdot\dfrac{6}{23} = \dfrac{21}{46}$.
Maxraj: $\left(\dfrac{9}{2} - \dfrac{11}{4}\right)\cdot\left(\dfrac{11}{6} - \dfrac{4}{3}\right) = \dfrac{7}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{7}{8}$.
$\dfrac{21}{46}:\dfrac{7}{8} = \dfrac{21}{46}\cdot\dfrac{8}{7} = \dfrac{12}{23}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{12}{23}$.

### 5 [A]
Har bir qavsni hisoblaymiz (butun va kasr qismlarni alohida ayiramiz):
$80\tfrac{3}{4} - 79\tfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, $81\tfrac{1}{4} - 80\tfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
$82\tfrac{1}{4} - 79\tfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{5}{2}$, $81\tfrac{1}{4} - 79\tfrac{1}{4} = 2$.
$\dfrac{\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{5}{2}\cdot2} = \dfrac{3/4}{5} = \dfrac{3}{20}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{20}$.

### 6 [A]
Har bir qavsni kasr körinişida yozamiz: $1 + \dfrac{1}{k} = \dfrac{k+1}{k}$.
$\dfrac{7}{6}\cdot\dfrac{8}{7}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}\cdots\dfrac{72}{71}$ — qöşni surat va maxrajlar qisqaradi.
Qoladi: $\dfrac{72}{6} = 12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 7 [D]
Har bir qavs: $1 - \dfrac{2}{k} = \dfrac{k-2}{k}$.
$\dfrac{5}{7}\cdot\dfrac{6}{8}\cdot\dfrac{7}{9}\cdots\dfrac{58}{60}$. Suratdagi $7, \ldots, 58$ maxrajdagilar bilan qisqaradi.
Qoladi: $\dfrac{5\cdot6}{59\cdot60} = \dfrac{30}{3540} = \dfrac{1}{118}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{118}$.

### 8 [C]
Kasrlarni maxraji $80$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{30}{80}$, $\dfrac{7}{16} = \dfrac{35}{80}$.
Ular orasida: $\dfrac{31}{80}, \dfrac{32}{80}, \dfrac{33}{80}, \dfrac{34}{80}$.

Javob: $4$.

### 9 [A]
Maxraji $72$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{45}{72}$, $\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{48}{72}$.
Orasidagi kasrlar: $\dfrac{46}{72}$ va $\dfrac{47}{72}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{93}{72} = \dfrac{31}{24}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{31}{24}$.

### 10 [B]
Butun qismni ajratamiz: $\dfrac{22}{9} = 2 + \dfrac{4}{9}$, demak $a = 2$.
$\dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{1}{9/4} = \dfrac{1}{2 + \frac{1}{4}}$, demak $b = 2$, $c = 4$.
$100a + 10b + c = 200 + 20 + 4 = 224$.

Javob: $224$.

### 11 [D]
Har bir sonni $1 + \dfrac{1}{n}$ körinişda yozamiz:
$a = 1 + \dfrac{1}{77}$, $b = 1 + \dfrac{1}{7}$, $c = 1 + \dfrac{1}{777}$.
Maxraj qança katta bölsa, kasr şunça kiçik: $\dfrac{1}{777} < \dfrac{1}{77} < \dfrac{1}{7}$.
Demak, $c < a < b$.

Javob: $c<a<b$.

### 12 [C]
Har bir qöşiluvçini ayirma körinişida yozamiz: $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)} = \dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+1}$.
Yiğindida örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{16} = \dfrac{3}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{16}$.

### 13 [B]
Maxrajdagi köpaytuvçilar ayirmasi $3$, şuning uçun $\dfrac{1}{k(k+3)} = \dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+3}\right)$.
Örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{43}\right) = \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{39}{172} = \dfrac{13}{172}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{13}{172}$.

### 14 [D]
Surat va maxrajni köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz:
$k(k+3) + 2 = k^2 + 3k + 2 = (k+1)(k+2)$, $(k+1)(k+4) + 2 = k^2 + 5k + 6 = (k+2)(k+3)$.
Har bir köpaytuvçi $\dfrac{k+1}{k+3}$ ga teng ($k = 3, 5, \ldots, 43$):
$\dfrac{4}{6}\cdot\dfrac{6}{8}\cdots\dfrac{44}{46} = \dfrac{4}{46} = \dfrac{2}{23}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{23}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [B]
Köp qavatli kasrni pastdan yuqoriga qarab hisoblaymiz:
$4 - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{11}{3}$; $\dfrac{3}{11/3} = \dfrac{9}{11}$.
$2 + \dfrac{9}{11} = \dfrac{31}{11}$; $\dfrac{4}{31/11} = \dfrac{44}{31} = 1\dfrac{13}{31}$.
$3 + 1\dfrac{13}{31} = 4\dfrac{13}{31}$.

Javob: $4\dfrac{13}{31}$.

### 2 [D]
Amallarni ički qavsdan boşlab bajaramiz:
$\dfrac{2}{3}:\dfrac{8}{9} = \dfrac{3}{4}$; $\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
$\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$; $\dfrac{1}{2}:\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
$5 - \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{13}{3}$; $\dfrac{13}{3} - \dfrac{7}{3} = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 3 [C]
Qavslar içini umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{1}{124} + \dfrac{1}{125} = \dfrac{249}{124\cdot125}$, $\dfrac{1}{124} - \dfrac{1}{125} = \dfrac{1}{124\cdot125}$.
Bölinma: $249$.

Javob: $249$.

### 4 [C]
Surat: $\dfrac{7}{5}:\left(3\dfrac{1}{4} + 1\dfrac{3}{4}\right) = \dfrac{7}{5}:5 = \dfrac{7}{25}$.
Maxraj: $\left(\dfrac{17}{6} - \dfrac{4}{3}\right)\cdot\left(\dfrac{5}{2} - \dfrac{17}{10}\right) = \dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{6}{5}$.
$\dfrac{7}{25}:\dfrac{6}{5} = \dfrac{7}{25}\cdot\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{7}{30}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{30}$.

### 5 [D]
Har bir qavsni hisoblaymiz:
$120\tfrac{1}{2} - 119\tfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4}$, $121\tfrac{1}{4} - 120\tfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
$122\tfrac{1}{4} - 119\tfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{11}{4}$, $121\tfrac{1}{4} - 119\tfrac{1}{4} = 2$.
$\dfrac{\frac{5}{4}\cdot\frac{3}{4}}{\frac{11}{4}\cdot2} = \dfrac{15/16}{11/2} = \dfrac{15}{88}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{15}{88}$.

### 6 [B]
Har bir qavs: $1 + \dfrac{1}{k} = \dfrac{k+1}{k}$.
$\dfrac{8}{7}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}\cdots\dfrac{91}{90}$ — qöşni surat va maxrajlar qisqaradi.
Qoladi: $\dfrac{91}{7} = 13$.

Javob: $13$.

### 7 [A]
Har bir qavs: $1 - \dfrac{2}{k} = \dfrac{k-2}{k}$.
$\dfrac{6}{8}\cdot\dfrac{7}{9}\cdots\dfrac{70}{72}$, qisqarişdan keyin qoladi:
$\dfrac{6\cdot7}{71\cdot72} = \dfrac{42}{5112} = \dfrac{7}{852}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{852}$.

### 8 [A]
Maxraji $120$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{50}{120}$, $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{60}{120}$.
Orasidagi kasrlar: $\dfrac{51}{120}, \ldots, \dfrac{59}{120}$ — $9$ ta.

Javob: $9$.

### 9 [C]
Maxraji $90$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{72}{90}$, $\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{75}{90}$.
Orasidagi kasrlar: $\dfrac{73}{90}$ va $\dfrac{74}{90}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{147}{90} = \dfrac{49}{30}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{49}{30}$.

### 10 [C]
Butun qismni ajratamiz: $\dfrac{23}{6} = 3 + \dfrac{5}{6}$, demak $a = 3$.
$\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{1}{6/5} = \dfrac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{5}}$, demak $b = 1$, $c = 5$.
$100a + 10b + c = 300 + 10 + 5 = 315$.

Javob: $315$.

### 11 [B]
Har bir sonni $1 + \dfrac{1}{n}$ körinişda yozamiz:
$a = 1 + \dfrac{1}{555}$, $b = 1 + \dfrac{1}{5}$, $c = 1 + \dfrac{1}{55}$.
$\dfrac{1}{555} < \dfrac{1}{55} < \dfrac{1}{5}$, demak $a < c < b$.

Javob: $a<c<b$.

### 12 [A]
$\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)} = \dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+1}$ dan foydalanamiz, örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{5} - \dfrac{1}{20} = \dfrac{3}{20}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{20}$.

### 13 [B]
Köpaytuvçilar ayirmasi $4$: $\dfrac{1}{k(k+4)} = \dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+4}\right)$.
Örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{1}{51}\right) = \dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{16}{51} = \dfrac{4}{51}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{51}$.

### 14 [D]
Köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz: $k(k+3) + 2 = (k+1)(k+2)$, $(k+1)(k+4) + 2 = (k+2)(k+3)$.
Har bir köpaytuvçi $\dfrac{k+1}{k+3}$ ($k = 5, 7, \ldots, 45$):
$\dfrac{6}{8}\cdot\dfrac{8}{10}\cdots\dfrac{46}{48} = \dfrac{6}{48} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{8}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [A]
Köp qavatli kasrni pastdan yuqoriga qarab hisoblaymiz:
$3 - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{11}{4}$; $\dfrac{6}{11/4} = \dfrac{24}{11}$.
$5 + \dfrac{24}{11} = \dfrac{79}{11}$; $\dfrac{7}{79/11} = \dfrac{77}{79}$.
$2 + \dfrac{77}{79} = 2\dfrac{77}{79}$.

Javob: $2\dfrac{77}{79}$.

### 2 [D]
Amallarni ički qavsdan boşlab bajaramiz:
$\dfrac{1}{2}:\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{3}{5}$; $\dfrac{3}{5} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{7}{20}$.
$\dfrac{3}{8} + \dfrac{7}{20} = \dfrac{29}{40}$; $\dfrac{29}{40}:\dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{29}{16}$.
$4 - \dfrac{29}{16} = \dfrac{35}{16}$; $\dfrac{35}{16} - \dfrac{28}{16} = \dfrac{7}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{16}$.

### 3 [B]
Qavslar içini umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{1}{299} + \dfrac{1}{300} = \dfrac{599}{299\cdot300}$, $\dfrac{1}{299} - \dfrac{1}{300} = \dfrac{1}{299\cdot300}$.
Bölinma: $599$.

Javob: $599$.

### 4 [D]
Surat: $\dfrac{5}{2}:\left(4\dfrac{1}{5} + 1\dfrac{4}{5}\right) = \dfrac{5}{2}:6 = \dfrac{5}{12}$.
Maxraj: $\left(3\dfrac{3}{4} - 2\dfrac{1}{4}\right)\cdot\left(1\dfrac{7}{8} - 1\dfrac{3}{8}\right) = \dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
$\dfrac{5}{12}:\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{5}{12}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{5}{9}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{9}$.

### 5 [C]
Har bir qavsni hisoblaymiz:
$200\tfrac{3}{5} - 199\tfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{7}{5}$, $201\tfrac{1}{5} - 200\tfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{3}{5}$.
$202\tfrac{1}{5} - 199\tfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{13}{5}$, $201\tfrac{1}{5} - 199\tfrac{1}{5} = 2$.
$\dfrac{\frac{7}{5}\cdot\frac{3}{5}}{\frac{13}{5}\cdot2} = \dfrac{21/25}{26/5} = \dfrac{21}{130}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{21}{130}$.

### 6 [A]
Har bir qavs: $1 + \dfrac{1}{k} = \dfrac{k+1}{k}$.
$\dfrac{10}{9}\cdot\dfrac{11}{10}\cdots\dfrac{99}{98}$ — qöşni surat va maxrajlar qisqaradi.
Qoladi: $\dfrac{99}{9} = 11$.

Javob: $11$.

### 7 [A]
Har bir qavs: $1 - \dfrac{2}{k} = \dfrac{k-2}{k}$.
$\dfrac{8}{10}\cdot\dfrac{9}{11}\cdots\dfrac{88}{90}$, qisqarişdan keyin qoladi:
$\dfrac{8\cdot9}{89\cdot90} = \dfrac{72}{8010} = \dfrac{4}{445}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{445}$.

### 8 [D]
Maxraji $120$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{7}{15} = \dfrac{56}{120}$, $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{60}{120}$.
Orasidagi kasrlar: $\dfrac{57}{120}, \dfrac{58}{120}, \dfrac{59}{120}$.

Javob: $3$.

### 9 [C]
Maxraji $84$ ga keltiramiz: $\dfrac{3}{7} = \dfrac{36}{84}$, $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{42}{84}$.
Orasidagi kasrlar: $\dfrac{37}{84}, \ldots, \dfrac{41}{84}$.
Yiğindi: $\dfrac{37 + 38 + 39 + 40 + 41}{84} = \dfrac{195}{84} = \dfrac{65}{28}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{65}{28}$.

### 10 [B]
Butun qismni ajratamiz: $\dfrac{29}{13} = 2 + \dfrac{3}{13}$, demak $a = 2$.
$\dfrac{3}{13} = \dfrac{1}{13/3} = \dfrac{1}{4 + \frac{1}{3}}$, demak $b = 4$, $c = 3$.
$100a + 10b + c = 200 + 40 + 3 = 243$.

Javob: $243$.

### 11 [C]
Har bir sonni $1 + \dfrac{1}{n}$ körinişda yozamiz:
$a = 1 + \dfrac{1}{44}$, $b = 1 + \dfrac{1}{444}$, $c = 1 + \dfrac{1}{4}$.
$\dfrac{1}{444} < \dfrac{1}{44} < \dfrac{1}{4}$, demak $b < a < c$.

Javob: $b<a<c$.

### 12 [A]
$\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)} = \dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+1}$ dan foydalanamiz, örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{6} - \dfrac{1}{18} = \dfrac{2}{18} = \dfrac{1}{9}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{9}$.

### 13 [C]
Köpaytuvçilar ayirmasi $5$: $\dfrac{1}{k(k+5)} = \dfrac{1}{5}\left(\dfrac{1}{k} - \dfrac{1}{k+5}\right)$.
Örtadagi hadlar qisqaradi:
$\dfrac{1}{5}\left(\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{52}\right) = \dfrac{1}{5}\cdot\dfrac{25}{52} = \dfrac{5}{52}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{52}$.

### 14 [B]
Köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz: $k(k+3) + 2 = (k+1)(k+2)$, $(k+1)(k+4) + 2 = (k+2)(k+3)$.
Har bir köpaytuvçi $\dfrac{k+1}{k+3}$ ($k = 7, 9, \ldots, 47$):
$\dfrac{8}{10}\cdot\dfrac{10}{12}\cdots\dfrac{48}{50} = \dfrac{8}{50} = \dfrac{4}{25}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{25}$.
