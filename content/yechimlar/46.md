# 46. Modulli tengsizliklar

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
Modul har doim manfiy emas: $|3x + 3| \ge 0 > -2$.
Tengsizlik barça $x$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 2 [C]
Modul manfiy sondan kiçik böla olmaydi, çunki $|a| \ge 0$.
Yeçim yöq.

Javob: $\varnothing$.

### 3 [A]
$|a| \le 0$ faqat $a = 0$ da: $3x + 6 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -2$.

Javob: $\{-2\}$.

### 4 [B]
Modul har doim manfiy emas, tengsizlik barça $x$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 5 [D]
$|a| \le 0$ faqat $a = 0$ da: $x^2 - 7x + 6 = 0$, $x = 1$ yoki $x = 6$.

Javob: $\{1, 6\}$.

### 6 [C]
$|3x| \ge 6 \Rightarrow |x| \ge 2$, ya'ni $x \le -2$ yoki $x \ge 2$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -2] \cup [2; +\infty)$.

### 7 [A]
$|a| < 9 \Leftrightarrow -9 < a < 9$: $-9 < 3x + 6 < 9 \Rightarrow -15 < 3x < 3 \Rightarrow -5 < x < 1$.

Javob: $x \in (-5; 1)$.

### 8 [D]
$|a| \ge 6 \Leftrightarrow a \ge 6$ yoki $a \le -6$.
$(x + 2)^2 \ge 16 \Rightarrow x \ge 2$ yoki $x \le -6$; $(x + 2)^2 \le 4 \Rightarrow -4 \le x \le 0$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -6] \cup [-4; 0] \cup [2; +\infty)$.

### 9 [B]
$|a| \le |b| \Leftrightarrow a^2 - b^2 \le 0 \Leftrightarrow (a - b)(a + b) \le 0$.
$(-x - 4)(5x + 2) \le 0 \Rightarrow (x + 4)(5x + 2) \ge 0$.
Nollar $-4$ va $-\dfrac{2}{5}$, ifoda ulardan taşqarida musbat.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -4] \cup \Bigl[-\dfrac{2}{5}; +\infty\Bigr)$.

### 10 [A]
$|a| > |b| \Leftrightarrow (a - b)(a + b) > 0$: $(-x^2 + x + 2)(x^2 + x - 2) > 0$.
$-(x - 2)(x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 1) > 0 \Rightarrow (x + 2)(x + 1)(x - 1)(x - 2) < 0$.
Nollar $-2, -1, 1, 2$; işoralar öngdan çapga $+, -, +, -, +$.

Javob: $x \in (-2; -1) \cup (1; 2)$.

### 11 [D]
$(a - b)(a + b) \le 0$: $a - b = x^2 - x - 6 = (x - 3)(x + 2)$, $a + b = x^2 + 5x = x(x + 5)$.
$(x + 5)(x + 2)x(x - 3) \le 0$; nollar $-5, -2, 0, 3$, işoralar öngdan çapga $+, -, +, -, +$.

Javob: $x \in [-5; -2] \cup [0; 3]$.

### 12 [A]
$|a| \le 0$ faqat $a = 0$ da: $x^2 - 8x + 7 = 0$, $x = 1$ yoki $x = 7$.
Yiğindi $8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 13 [C]
$(a - b)(a + b) < 0$: $a - b = x^2 - 2x - 15 = (x - 5)(x + 3)$, $a + b = x^2 + 6x - 7 = (x + 7)(x - 1)$.
Nollar $-7, -3, 1, 5$; yeçim $(-7; -3) \cup (1; 5)$.
Butun yeçimlar: $-6, -5, -4, 2, 3, 4$, yiğindi $-15 + 9 = -6$.

Javob: $-6$.

### 14 [D]
$x^2 = |x|^2$, $t = |x|$: $t^2 - 5t + 4 \ge 0 \Rightarrow t \le 1$ yoki $t \ge 4$.
$|x| \le 1$ yoki $|x| \ge 4$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -4] \cup [-1; 1] \cup [4; +\infty)$.

### 15 [B]
$t = |x - 1| \ge 0$, maxraj $t + 1 > 0$: $8 > (t - 1)(t + 1) = t^2 - 1 \Rightarrow t^2 < 9 \Rightarrow t < 3$.
$|x - 1| < 3 \Rightarrow -2 < x < 4$.

Javob: $x \in (-2; 4)$.

### 16 [D]
$|x + 2| \ge 0$, köpaytma manfiy bölişi uçun $|x + 2| > 0$ va $25 - x^2 < 0$.
$x^2 > 25 \Rightarrow |x| > 5$ ($x = -2$ bu oraliqqa kirmaydi).

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -5) \cup (5; +\infty)$.

### 17 [A]
Surat $|x - 5| \ge 0$, kasr manfiy bölişi uçun $x \ne 5$ va maxraj manfiy: $(x - 1)(x - 5) < 0$.

Javob: $x \in (1; 5)$.

### 18 [C]
$|a| > b \Leftrightarrow a > b$ yoki $a < -b$.
$3x - 2 > x + 4 \Rightarrow x > 3$; $3x - 2 < -x - 4 \Rightarrow x < -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl(-\infty; -\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr) \cup (3; +\infty)$.

### 19 [B]
$x^2 - 2x - 5 = (x - 1)^2 - 6$. $t = |x - 1|$: $t \ge t^2 - 6 \Rightarrow t^2 - t - 6 \le 0 \Rightarrow -2 \le t \le 3$.
$|x - 1| \le 3 \Rightarrow -2 \le x \le 4$.

Javob: $x \in [-2; 4]$.

### 20 [A]
Nollar $-4$ va $2$ böyiça:
$-4 \le x \le 2$ da yiğindi $6 \le 10$, bajariladi. $x > 2$ da $2x + 2 \le 10 \Rightarrow x \le 4$. $x < -4$ da $-2x - 2 \le 10 \Rightarrow x \ge -6$.

Javob: $x \in [-6; 4]$.

### 21 [B]
Maxraj $|x + 1| - 1$ nolga $x = 0$ va $x = -2$ da teng.
$-2 < x < 0$ da maxraj manfiy, surat musbat, kasr manfiy, yaramaydi.
$x > 0$: $x + 2 \ge x$, doim töğri. $x < -2$: $-x - 2 \ge -x - 2$, doim töğri.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -2) \cup (0; +\infty)$.

### 22 [D]
$t = |x - 2| \ge 0$, $t \ne 2$: $\dfrac{15}{t - 2} - t \ge 0 \Rightarrow \dfrac{(t - 5)(t + 3)}{t - 2} \le 0$.
$t + 3 > 0$, demak $2 < t \le 5$.
$2 < |x - 2| \le 5$: $x \in (4; 7]$ yoki $x \in [-3; 0)$.

Javob: $x \in [-3; 0) \cup (4; 7]$.

### 23 [D]
$x \ne 2$, $x \ne -3$. Tengsizlik: $4|x + 3| \le |x - 2| \Leftrightarrow 16(x + 3)^2 \le (x - 2)^2$.
$(3x + 14)(5x + 10) \le 0 \Rightarrow -\dfrac{14}{3} \le x \le -2$. $x = -3$ ni çiqaramiz.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl[-\dfrac{14}{3}; -3\Bigr) \cup (-3; -2]$.

### 24 [C]
$x^2 - 9x + 8 = (x - 1)(x - 8)$, $x \ne 8$ da kasr $x - 1$ ga teng.
$|x - 1| \ge 3 \Rightarrow x \ge 4$ yoki $x \le -2$, va $x \ne 8$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -2] \cup [4; 8) \cup (8; +\infty)$.

### 25 [A]
$|x^2 - 4| > 0$, ya'ni $x \ne \pm 2$, va $-x^2 + 3x + 10 > 0 \Rightarrow (x - 5)(x + 2) < 0$, $x \in (-2; 5)$.
Butun yeçimlar ($2$ siz): $-1, 0, 1, 3, 4$. $a = 5$, $b = 7$.
$a \cdot b = 35$.

Javob: $35$.

## 2-test

### 1 [D]
$|5x - 10| \ge 0 > -7$, barça $x$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 2 [B]
Modul manfiy sondan kiçik böla olmaydi.

Javob: $\varnothing$.

### 3 [B]
$5x - 5 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 1$.

Javob: $\{1\}$.

### 4 [A]
Modul har doim manfiy emas.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 5 [C]
$x^2 + x - 12 = 0$: $x = -4$ yoki $x = 3$.

Javob: $\{-4, 3\}$.

### 6 [D]
$|4x| \ge 20 \Rightarrow |x| \ge 5$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -5] \cup [5; +\infty)$.

### 7 [B]
$-8 < 4x - 12 < 8 \Rightarrow 4 < 4x < 20 \Rightarrow 1 < x < 5$.

Javob: $x \in (1; 5)$.

### 8 [A]
$(x - 3)^2 - 25 \ge 24 \Rightarrow (x - 3)^2 \ge 49 \Rightarrow x \ge 10$ yoki $x \le -4$.
$(x - 3)^2 - 25 \le -24 \Rightarrow (x - 3)^2 \le 1 \Rightarrow 2 \le x \le 4$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -4] \cup [2; 4] \cup [10; +\infty)$.

### 9 [C]
$(a - b)(a + b) \le 0$: $(2x + 6)(4x - 2) \le 0 \Rightarrow (x + 3)(2x - 1) \le 0$.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl[-3; \dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr]$.

### 10 [D]
$(a - b)(a + b) > 0$: $(-x^2 + x + 2)(x^2 + x - 12) > 0 \Rightarrow (x - 2)(x + 1)(x + 4)(x - 3) < 0$.
Nollar $-4, -1, 2, 3$; işoralar öngdan çapga $+, -, +, -, +$.

Javob: $x \in (-4; -1) \cup (2; 3)$.

### 11 [B]
$(a - b)(a + b) \le 0$: $(x^2 - 3x - 4)(x^2 + 3x - 4) \le 0 \Rightarrow (x - 4)(x + 1)(x + 4)(x - 1) \le 0$.
Nollar $-4, -1, 1, 4$.

Javob: $x \in [-4; -1] \cup [1; 4]$.

### 12 [C]
$x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$: $x = 2$ yoki $x = -5$, yiğindi $-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 13 [C]
$a - b = x^2 - 4x - 12 = (x - 6)(x + 2)$, $a + b = x^2 + 6x = x(x + 6)$, köpaytma manfiy.
Nollar $-6, -2, 0, 6$; yeçim $(-6; -2) \cup (0; 6)$.
Butun yeçimlar: $-5, -4, -3, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$, yiğindi $-12 + 15 = 3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 14 [A]
$t = |x|$: $t^2 - 10t + 21 \ge 0 \Rightarrow t \le 3$ yoki $t \ge 7$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -7] \cup [-3; 3] \cup [7; +\infty)$.

### 15 [D]
$t = |x - 3|$: $21 > (t - 2)(t + 2) = t^2 - 4 \Rightarrow t^2 < 25 \Rightarrow t < 5$.
$|x - 3| < 5 \Rightarrow -2 < x < 8$.

Javob: $x \in (-2; 8)$.

### 16 [A]
Köpaytma manfiy: $x \ne 3$ va $36 - x^2 < 0$, ya'ni $|x| > 6$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -6) \cup (6; +\infty)$.

### 17 [C]
$x \ne -1$ va maxraj manfiy: $(x + 1)(x + 5) < 0$.

Javob: $x \in (-5; -1)$.

### 18 [D]
$4x + 3 > 2x + 5 \Rightarrow x > 1$; $4x + 3 < -2x - 5 \Rightarrow x < -\dfrac{4}{3}$.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl(-\infty; -\dfrac{4}{3}\Bigr) \cup (1; +\infty)$.

### 19 [B]
$x^2 - 6x - 11 = (x - 3)^2 - 20$. $t = |x - 3|$: $t^2 - t - 20 \le 0 \Rightarrow t \le 5$.
$|x - 3| \le 5 \Rightarrow -2 \le x \le 8$.

Javob: $x \in [-2; 8]$.

### 20 [C]
$1 \le x \le 7$ da yiğindi $6 \le 10$. $x > 7$: $2x - 8 \le 10 \Rightarrow x \le 9$. $x < 1$: $8 - 2x \le 10 \Rightarrow x \ge -1$.

Javob: $x \in [-1; 9]$.

### 21 [A]
Maxraj $|x - 4| - 1$ nolga $x = 3$ va $x = 5$ da teng; $3 < x < 5$ da maxraj manfiy, kasr manfiy.
$x > 5$: $x - 3 \ge x - 5$, doim töğri. $x < 3$: $3 - x \ge 3 - x$, doim töğri.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; 3) \cup (5; +\infty)$.

### 22 [B]
$t = |x + 1| \ge 0$, $t \ne 1$: $\dfrac{6}{t - 1} - t \ge 0 \Rightarrow \dfrac{(t - 3)(t + 2)}{t - 1} \le 0$, ya'ni $1 < t \le 3$.
$1 < |x + 1| \le 3$: $x \in (0; 2]$ yoki $x \in [-4; -2)$.

Javob: $x \in [-4; -2) \cup (0; 2]$.

### 23 [A]
$x \ne -2$, $x \ne 4$: $5|x - 4| \le 3|x + 2| \Leftrightarrow 25(x - 4)^2 \le 9(x + 2)^2$.
$(2x - 26)(8x - 14) \le 0 \Rightarrow \dfrac{7}{4} \le x \le 13$, $x = 4$ ni çiqaramiz.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl[\dfrac{7}{4}; 4\Bigr) \cup (4; 13]$.

### 24 [C]
$x \ne -5$ da $\dfrac{x(x + 5)}{x + 5} = x$. $|x| \ge 2$ va $x \ne -5$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -5) \cup (-5; -2] \cup [2; +\infty)$.

### 25 [D]
$x \ne \pm 5$ va $x^2 - 9x + 8 < 0 \Rightarrow 1 < x < 8$.
Butun yeçimlar: $2, 3, 4, 6, 7$. $a = 5$, $b = 22$.
$a \cdot b = 110$.

Javob: $110$.

## 3-test

### 1 [C]
$|4x + 12| \ge 0 > -1$, barça $x$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 2 [A]
Modul manfiy sondan kiçik böla olmaydi.

Javob: $\varnothing$.

### 3 [A]
$4x + 20 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -5$.

Javob: $\{-5\}$.

### 4 [D]
Modul har doim manfiy emas.

Javob: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

### 5 [D]
$x^2 - 10x + 16 = 0$: $x = 2$ yoki $x = 8$.

Javob: $\{2, 8\}$.

### 6 [B]
$|5x| \ge 5 \Rightarrow |x| \ge 1$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -1] \cup [1; +\infty)$.

### 7 [C]
$-25 < 5x + 5 < 25 \Rightarrow -6 < x < 4$.

Javob: $x \in (-6; 4)$.

### 8 [C]
$x^2 - 26 \ge 10 \Rightarrow x^2 \ge 36$; $x^2 - 26 \le -10 \Rightarrow x^2 \le 16$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -6] \cup [-4; 4] \cup [6; +\infty)$.

### 9 [D]
$(a - b)(a + b) \le 0$: $(2x - 6)(6x - 4) \le 0 \Rightarrow (x - 3)(3x - 2) \le 0$.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl[\dfrac{2}{3}; 3\Bigr]$.

### 10 [B]
$(a - b)(a + b) > 0$: $(-x^2 + x)(x^2 + x - 12) > 0 \Rightarrow x(x - 1)(x + 4)(x - 3) < 0$.
Nollar $-4, 0, 1, 3$; işoralar öngdan çapga $+, -, +, -, +$.

Javob: $x \in (-4; 0) \cup (1; 3)$.

### 11 [A]
$a - b = x^2 - 5x = x(x - 5)$, $a + b = x^2 + x - 6 = (x + 3)(x - 2)$, köpaytma $\le 0$.
Nollar $-3, 0, 2, 5$.

Javob: $x \in [-3; 0] \cup [2; 5]$.

### 12 [C]
$x^2 - 12x + 27 = 0$: $x = 3$ yoki $x = 9$, yiğindi $12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 13 [B]
$a - b = x^2 - 6x - 7 = (x - 7)(x + 1)$, $a + b = x^2 + 2x - 3 = (x + 3)(x - 1)$, köpaytma manfiy.
Nollar $-3, -1, 1, 7$; yeçim $(-3; -1) \cup (1; 7)$.
Butun yeçimlar: $-2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6$, yiğindi $18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 14 [D]
$t = |x|$: $t^2 - 8t + 12 \ge 0 \Rightarrow t \le 2$ yoki $t \ge 6$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -6] \cup [-2; 2] \cup [6; +\infty)$.

### 15 [B]
$t = |x + 1|$: $16 > t^2 - 9 \Rightarrow t < 5$.
$|x + 1| < 5 \Rightarrow -6 < x < 4$.

Javob: $x \in (-6; 4)$.

### 16 [A]
Köpaytma manfiy: $x \ne 0$ va $9 - x^2 < 0$, ya'ni $|x| > 3$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -3) \cup (3; +\infty)$.

### 17 [D]
$x \ne 3$ va maxraj manfiy: $x(x - 3) < 0$.

Javob: $x \in (0; 3)$.

### 18 [A]
$5x - 1 > 2x + 6 \Rightarrow x > \dfrac{7}{3}$; $5x - 1 < -2x - 6 \Rightarrow x < -\dfrac{5}{7}$.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl(-\infty; -\dfrac{5}{7}\Bigr) \cup \Bigl(\dfrac{7}{3}; +\infty\Bigr)$.

### 19 [C]
$x^2 + 2x - 29 = (x + 1)^2 - 30$. $t = |x + 1|$: $t^2 - t - 30 \le 0 \Rightarrow t \le 6$.
$|x + 1| \le 6 \Rightarrow -7 \le x \le 5$.

Javob: $x \in [-7; 5]$.

### 20 [D]
$-5 \le x \le 3$ da yiğindi $8 \le 14$. $x > 3$: $2x + 2 \le 14 \Rightarrow x \le 6$. $x < -5$: $-2x - 2 \le 14 \Rightarrow x \ge -8$.

Javob: $x \in [-8; 6]$.

### 21 [B]
Maxraj $|x| - 1$ nolga $x = \pm 1$ da teng; $-1 < x < 1$ da maxraj manfiy, kasr manfiy.
$x > 1$: $x + 1 \ge x - 1$, töğri. $x < -1$: $-x - 1 \ge -x - 1$, töğri.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -1) \cup (1; +\infty)$.

### 22 [C]
$t = |x - 3|$, $t \ne 3$: $\dfrac{(t - 7)(t + 4)}{t - 3} \le 0$, ya'ni $3 < t \le 7$.
$3 < |x - 3| \le 7$: $x \in (6; 10]$ yoki $x \in [-4; 0)$.

Javob: $x \in [-4; 0) \cup (6; 10]$.

### 23 [B]
$x \ne -4$, $x \ne 2$: $3|x - 2| \le |x + 4| \Leftrightarrow 9(x - 2)^2 \le (x + 4)^2$.
$(2x - 10)(4x - 2) \le 0 \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{2} \le x \le 5$, $x = 2$ ni çiqaramiz.

Javob: $x \in \Bigl[\dfrac{1}{2}; 2\Bigr) \cup (2; 5]$.

### 24 [A]
$x^2 - 6x - 7 = (x - 7)(x + 1)$, $x \ne 7$ da kasr $x + 1$.
$|x + 1| \ge 5 \Rightarrow x \ge 4$ yoki $x \le -6$, va $x \ne 7$.

Javob: $x \in (-\infty; -6] \cup [4; 7) \cup (7; +\infty)$.

### 25 [A]
$x \ne \pm 4$ va $x^2 - 11x + 18 < 0 \Rightarrow 2 < x < 9$.
Butun yeçimlar: $3, 5, 6, 7, 8$. $a = 5$, $b = 29$.
$a \cdot b = 145$.

Javob: $145$.
