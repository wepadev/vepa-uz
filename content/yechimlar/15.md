# 15. Daraja va uning xossalari (2-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
Sonlarni bir xil asosli daraja körinişida yozamiz: $729 = 3^6$, demak $a = 6$.
$\dfrac{1}{25} = 5^{-2}$, demak $b = -2$.
$a + b = 6 - 2 = 4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 2 [C]
Bir xil körsatkiçli darajalar bölinmasi: $\dfrac{189^x}{7^x} = \left(\dfrac{189}{7}\right)^x = 27^x$.
$27^x = (3^x)^3 = 4^3 = 64$.

Javob: $64$.

### 3 [D]
Standart şakl $a \cdot 10^n$, bunda $1 \le a < 10$. Vergulni $2$ xona öngga suramiz:
$0{,}0527 = 5{,}27 \cdot 10^{-2}$.

Javob: $5{,}27\cdot10^{-2}$.

### 4 [C]
Sonlarni va $10$ ning darajalarini alohida köpaytiramiz:
$0{,}025 \cdot 32 = 0{,}8$, $10^{-3} \cdot 10^{5} = 10^{2}$.
$0{,}8 \cdot 10^2 = 80 = 8 \cdot 10^1$.

Javob: $8\cdot10^{1}$.

### 5 [B]
Tub köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz: $100^4 = 2^8 \cdot 5^8$, $20^3 = 2^6 \cdot 5^3$.
Bölinma: $2^2 \cdot 5^5$.
Böluvçilar soni $(2+1)(5+1) = 18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 6 [A]
Köpaytuvçilarni guruhlaymiz: $175^2 \cdot 8^2 = (175 \cdot 8)^2 = 1400^2 = 1\,960\,000$.
$1\,960\,000 \cdot 25 = 49\,000\,000$.
Raqamlar yiğindisi: $4 + 9 = 13$.

Javob: $13$.

### 7 [D]
Bir xil asosga keltiramiz: $a = 25^7 = 5^{14}$, $b = 125^5 = 5^{15}$.
$14 < 15$, demak $a < b$.

Javob: $a<b$.

### 8 [D]
Hammasini $3$ asosga keltiramiz: $a = 3^{14}$, $b = 3^{15}$, $c = 3^{12}$.
Körsatkiçlarni taqqoslaymiz: $12 < 14 < 15$.

Javob: $c<a<b$.

### 9 [C]
$108 = 2^2 \cdot 3^3$, demak $108^x = (2^x)^2 \cdot (3^x)^3 = b^2a^3$.

Javob: $a^3b^2$.

### 10 [B]
$3^x$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz: $3^{x+1} + 3^{x-2} = 3^x\left(3 + \dfrac{1}{9}\right) = 18 \cdot \dfrac{28}{9}$.
$2 \cdot 28 = 56$.

Javob: $56$.

### 11 [A]
Har sonni yaqin darajalar orasiga joylaymiz:
$3^5 = 243 < 250 < 3^6$, demak $5 < a < 6$.
$2^3 = 8 < 15 < 16 = 2^4$, demak $3 < b < 4$.
$7^2 = 49 < 50 < 7^3$, demak $2 < c < 3$.
Demak $c < b < a$.

Javob: $c<b<a$.

### 12 [B]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $10^{(x+y)+(2x-y)} = \dfrac{125}{4} \cdot 32\,000$.
$10^{3x} = 1\,000\,000 = 10^6$, demak $3x = 6$.
$x = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 13 [D]
Logarifm körinişida yozamiz: $a = \log_3 4 = 2\log_3 2$, $b = \log_2 343 = 3\log_2 7$, $c = \log_7 9 = 2\log_7 3$.
$\log_3 2 \cdot \log_2 7 \cdot \log_7 3 = 1$ bölgani uçun $abc = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 14 [C]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $2^{x+y} \cdot 5^{x+y} = 80 \cdot 1250$.
$10^{x+y} = 100\,000 = 10^5$.
$x + y = 5$.

Javob: $5$.

## 2-test

### 1 [D]
$1024 = 2^{10}$, demak $a = 10$; $\dfrac{1}{343} = 7^{-3}$, demak $b = -3$.
$a + b = 7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 2 [C]
$\dfrac{250^x}{2^x} = 125^x = (5^x)^3 = 2^3 = 8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 3 [B]
Vergulni $6$ xona öngga suramiz: $0{,}0000063 = 6{,}3 \cdot 10^{-6}$.

Javob: $6{,}3\cdot10^{-6}$.

### 4 [B]
$0{,}008 \cdot 45 = 0{,}36$, $10^{-5} \cdot 10^{9} = 10^4$.
$0{,}36 \cdot 10^4 = 3600 = 3{,}6 \cdot 10^3$.

Javob: $3{,}6\cdot10^{3}$.

### 5 [D]
$108 = 2^2 \cdot 3^3$, $108^3 = 2^6 \cdot 3^9$; $12^2 = 2^4 \cdot 3^2$.
Bölinma: $2^2 \cdot 3^7$.
Böluvçilar soni $(2+1)(7+1) = 24$.

Javob: $24$.

### 6 [A]
Tub köpaytuvçilar: $45^4 = 3^8 \cdot 5^4$, $16^2 = 2^8$, $625 = 5^4$.
Köpaytma: $3^8 \cdot 2^8 \cdot 5^8 = 6561 \cdot 10^8$.
Raqamlar yiğindisi: $6 + 5 + 6 + 1 = 18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 7 [D]
$a = 9^9 = 3^{18}$, $b = 27^6 = 3^{18}$.
Demak $a = b$.

Javob: $a=b$.

### 8 [C]
$5$ asosga keltiramiz: $a = 5^{12}$, $b = 5^9$, $c = 5^{13}$.
$9 < 12 < 13$.

Javob: $b<a<c$.

### 9 [A]
$225 = 5^2 \cdot 3^2$, demak $225^x = (5^x)^2(3^x)^2 = a^2b^2$.

Javob: $a^2b^2$.

### 10 [C]
$5^{x+1} + 5^{x-1} = 5^x\left(5 + \dfrac{1}{5}\right) = 20 \cdot \dfrac{26}{5} = 104$.

Javob: $104$.

### 11 [D]
$5^2 = 25 < 26 < 5^3$, demak $2 < a < 3$.
$2^7 = 128 < 130 < 2^8$, demak $7 < b < 8$.
$3^4 = 81 < 82 < 3^5$, demak $4 < c < 5$.
Demak $a < c < b$.

Javob: $a<c<b$.

### 12 [B]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $12^{3x} = \dfrac{32}{3} \cdot 162 = 32 \cdot 54 = 1728 = 12^3$.
$3x = 3$, $x = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 13 [A]
$a = \log_5 81 = 4\log_5 3$, $b = \log_3 8 = 3\log_3 2$, $c = \log_2 25 = 2\log_2 5$.
$\log_5 3 \cdot \log_3 2 \cdot \log_2 5 = 1$, demak $abc = 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 24$.

Javob: $24$.

### 14 [C]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $3^{x+y} \cdot 2^{x+y} = 324 \cdot 144$.
$6^{x+y} = 46\,656 = 6^6$.
$x + y = 6$.

Javob: $6$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
$161\,051 = 11^5$, demak $a = 5$; $\dfrac{1}{9} = 3^{-2}$, demak $b = -2$.
$a + b = 3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 2 [C]
$\dfrac{294^x}{6^x} = 49^x = (7^x)^2 = 3^2 = 9$.

Javob: $9$.

### 3 [A]
Vergulni $1$ xona öngga suramiz: $0{,}709 = 7{,}09 \cdot 10^{-1}$.

Javob: $7{,}09\cdot10^{-1}$.

### 4 [B]
$0{,}014 \cdot 25 = 0{,}35$, $10^{-4} \cdot 10^{7} = 10^3$.
$0{,}35 \cdot 10^3 = 350 = 3{,}5 \cdot 10^2$.

Javob: $3{,}5\cdot10^{2}$.

### 5 [D]
$120^3 = 2^9 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5^3$, $30^2 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$.
Bölinma: $2^7 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$.
Böluvçilar soni $(7+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 32$.

Javob: $32$.

### 6 [A]
$275^2 \cdot 8^2 = (275 \cdot 8)^2 = 2200^2 = 4\,840\,000$.
$4\,840\,000 \cdot 25 = 121\,000\,000$.
Raqamlar yiğindisi: $1 + 2 + 1 = 4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 7 [C]
$a = 32^7 = 2^{35}$, $b = 8^{12} = 2^{36}$.
$35 < 36$, demak $a < b$.

Javob: $a<b$.

### 8 [A]
$2$ asosga keltiramiz: $a = 2^{20}$, $b = 2^{24}$, $c = 2^{21}$.
$20 < 21 < 24$.

Javob: $a<c<b$.

### 9 [D]
$56 = 2^3 \cdot 7$, demak $56^x = (2^x)^3 \cdot 7^x = a^3b$.

Javob: $a^3b$.

### 10 [C]
$4^{x+2} + 4^{x-1} = 4^x\left(16 + \dfrac{1}{4}\right) = 12 \cdot \dfrac{65}{4} = 195$.

Javob: $195$.

### 11 [D]
$7^4 = 2401 < 2500 < 7^5$, demak $4 < a < 5$.
$3^2 = 9 < 20 < 27 = 3^3$, demak $2 < b < 3$.
$2^6 = 64 < 100 < 128 = 2^7$, demak $6 < c < 7$.
Demak $b < a < c$.

Javob: $b<a<c$.

### 12 [B]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $8^{3x} = 32 \cdot 8192 = 2^5 \cdot 2^{13} = 2^{18} = 8^6$.
$3x = 6$, $x = 2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 13 [A]
$a = \log_2 125 = 3\log_2 5$, $b = \log_5 9 = 2\log_5 3$, $c = \log_3 32 = 5\log_3 2$.
$\log_2 5 \cdot \log_5 3 \cdot \log_3 2 = 1$, demak $abc = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 = 30$.

Javob: $30$.

### 14 [B]
Tengliklarni köpaytiramiz: $2^{x+y} \cdot 3^{x+y} = 288 \cdot 972$.
$6^{x+y} = 279\,936 = 6^7$.
$x + y = 7$.

Javob: $7$.
