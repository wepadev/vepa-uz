# 14. Daraja va uning xossalari (1-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Bir xil köpaytuvçilar köpaytmasi daraja bilan yoziladi: asos $y$, körsatkiç köpaytuvçilar soni $14$.

Javob: $y^{14}$.

### 2 [C]
Darajalarni hisoblaymiz: $5^3 = 125$, $3^4 = 81$, $2^5 = 32$.
$125 - 81 - 32 = 12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 3 [A]
$-1$ ning juft darajasi $1$, toq darajasi $-1$, nolinçi daraja esa $1$ ga teng:
$1 + (-1) + 1 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 4 [B]
$a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$ qoidasidan foydalanamiz:
$\left(\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{10}\right) = \dfrac{7}{12} \cdot \dfrac{3}{10} = \dfrac{7}{40}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{7}{40}$.

### 5 [A]
Manfiy körsatkiç kasrni ağdaradi: $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-2} = \left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)^2 = \dfrac{25}{9}$.
$\dfrac{25}{9} \cdot 9 = 25$.

Javob: $25$.

### 6 [D]
Asoslari bir xil darajalar köpaytirilganda körsatkiçlar qöşiladi: $x^{9+15} = x^{24}$.

Javob: $x^{24}$.

### 7 [D]
Asoslari bir xil darajalar bölinganda körsatkiçlar ayiriladi: $x^{21-13} = x^{8}$.

Javob: $x^{8}$.

### 8 [A]
Köpaytmani darajaga oşirişda har bir köpaytuvçi darajaga oşiriladi, körsatkiçlar köpaytiriladi:
$\left(x^2y^5\right)^3 = x^{6}y^{15}$.

Javob: $x^6y^{15}$.

### 9 [C]
Qavs içini soddalaştiramiz: $\dfrac{x^4y^3z}{x^2yz^2} = x^2y^2z^{-1}$.
Kubga oşiramiz: $x^6y^6z^{-3}$.
Köpaytiramiz: $x^6y^6z^{-3} \cdot x^2y^{-2}z^5 = x^8y^4z^2$.

Javob: $x^8y^4z^2$.

### 10 [B]
$-1$ daraja kasrni ağdaradi: $\dfrac{23}{42} - \dfrac{9}{42} = \dfrac{14}{42} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
$\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-1} = 3$, $3 \cdot 5 = 15$.

Javob: $15$.

### 11 [B]
Har qöşiluvçini alohida hisoblaymiz:
$(-1)^5 = -1$; $\left(-(-1)^4\right)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1$; $\left(-(-1)^7\right)^3 = 1^3 = 1$.
$-1 + 1 + 1 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 12 [C]
Hammasini $2$ ning darajasi körinişida yozamiz: $256^4 = 2^{32}$, $8 = 2^3$, $32^5 = 2^{25}$.
$\dfrac{2^{32}}{2^{3} \cdot 2^{25}} = 2^{32-28} = 2^4$.

Javob: $2^4$.

### 13 [A]
Har bir köpaytuvçini kub körinişida yozamiz: $125 = 5^3$, $x^{24} = (x^8)^3$, $y^9 = (y^3)^3$, $z^{21} = (z^7)^3$.
Demak $125x^{24}y^9z^{21} = \left(5x^8y^3z^7\right)^3$.

Javob: $\left(5x^8y^3z^7\right)^3$.

### 14 [B]
$90 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$, demak $90^3 = 2^3 \cdot 3^6 \cdot 5^3$.
$\dfrac{2^3 \cdot 3^6 \cdot 5^3}{2^2 \cdot 3^4 \cdot 5} = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2 = 450$.

Javob: $450$.

### 15 [B]
Suratda $2^{50}$ ni, maxrajda $2^{49}$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz:
$\dfrac{2^{50}(1 + 1 + 2 + 4)}{2^{49} \cdot 3} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 8}{3} = \dfrac{16}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{16}{3}$.

### 16 [D]
Aralaş sonlarni kasrga aylantiramiz: $\left(-\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^4 = \dfrac{625}{16}$, $\left(\dfrac{6}{5}\right)^2 = \dfrac{36}{25}$, $\left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)^3 = \dfrac{125}{27}$.
$\dfrac{1}{625} \cdot \dfrac{625}{16} \cdot \dfrac{36}{25} \cdot \dfrac{125}{27} = \dfrac{36 \cdot 5}{16 \cdot 27} = \dfrac{180}{432} = \dfrac{5}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5}{12}$.

### 17 [D]
Har kasrni tub asoslarga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{5^6 \cdot 5^4}{5^6 \cdot 5^2} = 5^2 = 25$;
$\dfrac{2^4 \cdot 7^4 \cdot 2^4}{7^4 \cdot 2^6} = 2^2 = 4$;
$\dfrac{4^3 \cdot 3^3}{4^3 \cdot 3^2} = 3$.
Yiğindi: $25 + 4 + 3 = 32$.

Javob: $32$.

### 18 [C]
Juft darajada minus yöqoladi: $\left((-15)^4\right)^5 = 15^{20}$, $\left((-15)^{11}\right)^2 = 15^{22}$.
$15^{20} : 15^{22} = \dfrac{1}{225}$, $\left(-\dfrac{1}{15}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{225}$.
$\dfrac{1}{225} - \dfrac{1}{225} = 0$.

Javob: $0$.

### 19 [A]
$5$ va $7$ ning butun darajalari faqat ikkalasi ham $0$ körsatkiçda teng (ikkalasi $1$ ga teng) böladi.
$x - 2 = 0$, $y - 6 = 0$, ya'ni $x = 2$, $y = 6$.
$x + y = 8$.

Javob: $8$.

### 20 [C]
$\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{-n} = \left(\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^n = \dfrac{25}{64}$.
$\dfrac{25}{64} = \left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)^2$, bundan $b = 5$, $a = 8$, $n = 2$ va yiğindi $15$.
$n = 1$ da yiğindi $25 + 64 + 1 = 90$, demak eng kiçigi $15$.

Javob: $15$.

## 2-test

### 1 [B]
Asos $m$, körsatkiç köpaytuvçilar soni $11$.

Javob: $m^{11}$.

### 2 [C]
$6^3 = 216$, $4^3 = 64$, $2^4 = 16$.
$216 - 64 - 16 = 136$.

Javob: $136$.

### 3 [A]
$(-1)^{15} = -1$, $(-1)^{14} = 1$, $101^0 = 1$.
$-1 + 1 + 1 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 4 [D]
$a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$:
$\left(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{8}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6}\right) = \dfrac{3}{8} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{16}$.

### 5 [B]
$\left(\dfrac{4}{7}\right)^{-2} = \left(\dfrac{7}{4}\right)^2 = \dfrac{49}{16}$.
$\dfrac{49}{16} \cdot 16 = 49$.

Javob: $49$.

### 6 [D]
Körsatkiçlar qöşiladi: $b^{18+5} = b^{23}$.

Javob: $b^{23}$.

### 7 [C]
Körsatkiçlar ayiriladi: $b^{16-7} = b^{9}$.

Javob: $b^{9}$.

### 8 [A]
Körsatkiçlar köpaytiriladi: $\left(m^4n^3\right)^2 = m^8n^6$.

Javob: $m^8n^6$.

### 9 [B]
Qavs içi: $\dfrac{m^2n^5p^2}{mn^2p^4} = mn^3p^{-2}$.
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $m^2n^6p^{-4}$.
Köpaytiramiz: $m^2n^6p^{-4} \cdot m^3n^{-1}p^6 = m^5n^5p^2$.

Javob: $m^5n^5p^2$.

### 10 [D]
Kasrlarni ağdaramiz: $\dfrac{19}{36} - \dfrac{7}{36} = \dfrac{12}{36} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
$\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-1} = 3$, $3 \cdot 6 = 18$.

Javob: $18$.

### 11 [C]
$(-1)^8 = 1$; $\left(-(-1)^5\right)^4 = 1^4 = 1$; $\left(-(-1)^6\right)^3 = (-1)^3 = -1$.
$1 + 1 - 1 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 12 [B]
$128^5 = 2^{35}$, $16 = 2^4$, $16^7 = 2^{28}$.
$\dfrac{2^{35}}{2^{4} \cdot 2^{28}} = 2^{35-32} = 2^3$.

Javob: $2^3$.

### 13 [A]
$512 = 8^3$, $m^{15} = (m^5)^3$, $n^{27} = (n^9)^3$, $p^6 = (p^2)^3$.
Demak $512m^{15}n^{27}p^6 = \left(8m^5n^9p^2\right)^3$.

Javob: $\left(8m^5n^9p^2\right)^3$.

### 14 [A]
$40 = 2^3 \cdot 5$, demak $40^4 = 2^{12} \cdot 5^4$.
$\dfrac{2^{12} \cdot 5^4}{2^7 \cdot 5^2} = 2^5 \cdot 5^2 = 32 \cdot 25 = 800$.

Javob: $800$.

### 15 [D]
Suratda $4^{12}$, maxrajda $4^{11}$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz:
$\dfrac{4^{12}(1 + 1 + 4 + 16)}{4^{11} \cdot 3} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 22}{3} = \dfrac{88}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{88}{3}$.

### 16 [D]
$(-2)^4 = 16$, $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$, $\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^3 = \dfrac{64}{27}$.
$\dfrac{1}{64} \cdot 16 \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} \cdot \dfrac{64}{27} = \dfrac{16 \cdot 9}{4 \cdot 27} = \dfrac{4}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

### 17 [C]
Tub asoslarga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{3^{10} \cdot 3^2}{3^9 \cdot 3^3} = 1$;
$\dfrac{2^4 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 5^4}{2^4 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 5^6} = 1$;
$\dfrac{3^3 \cdot 7^3}{7^3 \cdot 3^2} = 3$.
Yiğindi: $1 + 1 + 3 = 5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 18 [A]
Juft darajalar: $\left((-20)^6\right)^3 = 20^{18}$, $\left((-20)^{10}\right)^2 = 20^{20}$.
$20^{18} : 20^{20} = \dfrac{1}{400}$, $\left(-\dfrac{1}{20}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{400}$.
Ayirma $0$ ga teng.

Javob: $0$.

### 19 [B]
$3$ va $2$ ning butun darajalari faqat körsatkiçlar $0$ bölganda teng: $x - 5 = 0$, $y - 7 = 0$.
$x = 5$, $y = 7$, $x + y = 12$.

Javob: $12$.

### 20 [A]
$\left(\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^n = \dfrac{27}{125} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^3$, bundan $b = 3$, $a = 5$, $n = 3$, yiğindi $11$.
$n = 1$ da yiğindi $27 + 125 + 1 = 153$, demak eng kiçigi $11$.

Javob: $11$.

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
Asos $q$, körsatkiç köpaytuvçilar soni $16$.

Javob: $q^{16}$.

### 2 [A]
$7^3 = 343$, $5^3 = 125$, $3^4 = 81$.
$343 - 125 - 81 = 137$.

Javob: $137$.

### 3 [C]
$(-1)^{20} = 1$, $(-1)^{21} = -1$, $999^0 = 1$.
$1 - 1 + 1 = 1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 4 [D]
$a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$:
$\left(\dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{4}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{8}\right) = \dfrac{9}{20} \cdot \dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{27}{160}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{27}{160}$.

### 5 [C]
$\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{-2} = \left(\dfrac{6}{5}\right)^2 = \dfrac{36}{25}$.
$\dfrac{36}{25} \cdot 25 = 36$.

Javob: $36$.

### 6 [B]
Körsatkiçlar qöşiladi: $y^{12+14} = y^{26}$.

Javob: $y^{26}$.

### 7 [A]
Körsatkiçlar ayiriladi: $y^{25-11} = y^{14}$.

Javob: $y^{14}$.

### 8 [B]
Körsatkiçlar köpaytiriladi: $\left(p^2q^3\right)^5 = p^{10}q^{15}$.

Javob: $p^{10}q^{15}$.

### 9 [D]
Qavs içi: $\dfrac{p^5q^3r^2}{p^2qr^3} = p^3q^2r^{-1}$.
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $p^6q^4r^{-2}$.
Köpaytiramiz: $p^6q^4r^{-2} \cdot pq^{-1}r^4 = p^7q^3r^2$.

Javob: $p^7q^3r^2$.

### 10 [C]
Kasrlarni ağdaramiz: $\dfrac{31}{56} - \dfrac{17}{56} = \dfrac{14}{56} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
$\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{-1} = 4$, $4 \cdot 7 = 28$.

Javob: $28$.

### 11 [A]
$(-1)^7 = -1$; $\left(-(-1)^6\right)^3 = (-1)^3 = -1$; $\left(-(-1)^5\right)^2 = 1^2 = 1$.
$-1 - 1 + 1 = -1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 12 [B]
$32^8 = 2^{40}$, $4 = 2^2$, $8^{12} = 2^{36}$.
$\dfrac{2^{40}}{2^{2} \cdot 2^{36}} = 2^{40-38} = 2^2$.

Javob: $2^2$.

### 13 [D]
$729 = 9^3$, $p^{12} = (p^4)^3$, $q^{30} = (q^{10})^3$, $r^{18} = (r^6)^3$.
Demak $729p^{12}q^{30}r^{18} = \left(9p^4q^{10}r^6\right)^3$.

Javob: $\left(9p^4q^{10}r^6\right)^3$.

### 14 [A]
$75 = 3 \cdot 5^2$, demak $75^3 = 3^3 \cdot 5^6$.
$\dfrac{3^3 \cdot 5^6}{3^2 \cdot 5^4} = 3 \cdot 25 = 75$.

Javob: $75$.

### 15 [C]
Suratda $5^8$, maxrajda $5^7$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz:
$\dfrac{5^8(1 + 1 + 5 + 25)}{5^7 \cdot 3} = \dfrac{5 \cdot 32}{3} = \dfrac{160}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{160}{3}$.

### 16 [B]
$\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^6 = \dfrac{729}{64}$, $\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^3 = \dfrac{64}{27}$, $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$.
$\dfrac{1}{729} \cdot \dfrac{729}{64} \cdot \dfrac{64}{27} \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{9}{27 \cdot 4} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{12}$.

### 17 [D]
Tub asoslarga keltiramiz:
$\dfrac{2^{15} \cdot 2^4}{2^{16} \cdot 2} = 2^2 = 4$;
$\dfrac{2^4 \cdot 3^8 \cdot 2^2}{3^6 \cdot 2^6} = 3^2 = 9$;
$\dfrac{5^3 \cdot 7^3}{5^3 \cdot 7^2} = 7$.
Yiğindi: $4 + 9 + 7 = 20$.

Javob: $20$.

### 18 [B]
Juft darajalar: $\left((-8)^5\right)^4 = 8^{20}$, $\left((-8)^{11}\right)^2 = 8^{22}$.
$8^{20} : 8^{22} = \dfrac{1}{64}$, $\left(-\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{64}$.
Ayirma $0$ ga teng.

Javob: $0$.

### 19 [B]
$7$ va $11$ ning butun darajalari faqat körsatkiçlar $0$ bölganda teng: $x - 4 = 0$, $y - 3 = 0$.
$x = 4$, $y = 3$, $x + y = 7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 20 [A]
$\left(\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^n = \dfrac{81}{256}$. Imkoniyatlar: $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4$ (yiğindi $3 + 4 + 4 = 11$), $\left(\dfrac{9}{16}\right)^2$ (yiğindi $27$), $n = 1$ (yiğindi $338$).
Eng kiçigi $11$.

Javob: $11$.
