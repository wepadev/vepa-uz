# 84. Boşlanğiç funksiya va integral (1-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Darajali funksiya uçun: $\displaystyle\int x^n\,dx=\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C$.
$\displaystyle\int\left(-\dfrac25x+3\right)dx=-\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x+C=-\dfrac15x^2+3x+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{5}x^{2}+3x+C$.

### 2 [A]
Har bir hadni alohida integrallaymiz:
$-6\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{3}+2\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+5x+C=-2x^3+x^2+5x+C$.

Javob: $-2x^{3}+x^{2}+5x+C$.

### 3 [B]
$\displaystyle\int x^n\,dx=\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C$:
$10\cdot\dfrac{x^5}{5}+4\cdot\dfrac{x^4}{4}-6\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x+C=2x^5+x^4-3x^2+x+C$.

Javob: $2x^{5}+x^{4}-3x^{2}+x+C$.

### 4 [A]
$x\sqrt[3]{x^2}=x^{\frac53}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac53}dx=\dfrac{x^{\frac83}}{\frac83}+C=\dfrac38x^2\sqrt[3]{x^2}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{8}x^{2}\sqrt[3]{x^{2}}+C$.

### 5 [A]
$f(x)=2x^{\frac32}-4x^{\frac13}$.
$2\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac52}}{\frac52}-4\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac43}}{\frac43}+C=\dfrac45x^2\sqrt x-3x\sqrt[3]{x}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{5}x^{2}\sqrt{x}-3x\sqrt[3]{x}+C$.

### 6 [D]
$f(x)=3x^{-\frac12}+x^{-\frac34}$.
$3\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac12}}{\frac12}+\dfrac{x^{\frac14}}{\frac14}+C=6\sqrt x+4\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

Javob: $6\sqrt{x}+4\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

### 7 [B]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|+C$, $\displaystyle\int x^{-2}dx=-\dfrac1x+C$.
$4\ln|x|-6\cdot\left(-\dfrac1x\right)+C=4\ln|x|+\dfrac6x+C$.

Javob: $4\ln|x|+\dfrac{6}{x}+C$.

### 8 [C]
$\displaystyle\int a^x\,dx=\dfrac{a^x}{\ln a}+C$.
$\dfrac{2\cdot5^x}{\ln5}+\dfrac{7^x}{\ln7}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\cdot 5^x}{\ln 5}+\dfrac{7^x}{\ln 7}+C$.

### 9 [A]
$\displaystyle\int e^xdx=e^x$, $\displaystyle\int a^xdx=\dfrac{a^x}{\ln a}$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|$.
$5e^x+\dfrac{2\cdot3^x}{\ln3}-4\ln|x|+C$.

Javob: $5e^x+\dfrac{2\cdot 3^x}{\ln 3}-4\ln|x|+C$.

### 10 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\cos^2x}=\operatorname{tg}x+C$.
$-6\cos x+2\operatorname{tg}x+C$.

Javob: $-6\cos x+2\operatorname{tg} x+C$.

### 11 [A]
Umumiy körinişi: $F(x)=2x^3+2x^2-3x+C$.
$F(-1)=2$: $-2+2+3+C=2 \Rightarrow C=-1$.

Javob: $2x^{3}+2x^{2}-3x-1$.

### 12 [B]
$f(x)=-5x^{\frac23}+6x^{\frac12}$, $F(x)=-3x^{\frac53}+4x^{\frac32}+C=-3x\sqrt[3]{x^2}+4x\sqrt x+C$.
$F(1)=0$: $-3+4+C=0 \Rightarrow C=-1$.

Javob: $-3x\sqrt[3]{x^{2}}+4x\sqrt{x}-1$.

### 13 [A]
$F(x)=2\ln|x|+C$.
$F(3)=\ln18$: $2\ln3+C=\ln18 \Rightarrow C=\ln18-\ln9=\ln2$.

Javob: $2\ln|x|+\ln2$.

### 14 [B]
$\displaystyle\int2^x\ln2\,dx=2^x$, demak $F(x)=2^x+3x+C$.
$F(1)=8$: $2+3+C=8 \Rightarrow C=3$.

Javob: $2^x+3x+3$.

### 15 [B]
$F(x)=4\sin x+\cos x+C$.
$F(\pi)=2$: $0-1+C=2 \Rightarrow C=3$.

Javob: $4\sin x+\cos x+3$.

### 16 [C]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{1+x^2}=\operatorname{arctg}x+C$, $F(x)=3\operatorname{arctg}x+C$.
$F(\sqrt3)=\dfrac\pi2$: $3\cdot\dfrac\pi3+C=\dfrac\pi2 \Rightarrow C=-\dfrac\pi2$.

Javob: $3\operatorname{arctg} x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 17 [C]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x+C$, $F(x)=2\arcsin x+C$.
$F\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=0$: $2\cdot\dfrac\pi4+C=0 \Rightarrow C=-\dfrac\pi2$.

Javob: $2\arcsin x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 18 [B]
Qavslarni oçamiz: $(2x-1)(x+3)=2x^2+5x-3$.
$\displaystyle\int(2x^2+5x-3)dx=\dfrac23x^3+\dfrac52x^2-3x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3}x^{3}+\dfrac{5}{2}x^{2}-3x+C$.

### 19 [C]
Kvadratga oşiramiz: $(3x^{-2}-x^{-3})^2=9x^{-4}-6x^{-5}+x^{-6}$.
$9\cdot\dfrac{x^{-3}}{-3}-6\cdot\dfrac{x^{-4}}{-4}+\dfrac{x^{-5}}{-5}+C=-\dfrac{3}{x^3}+\dfrac{3}{2x^4}-\dfrac{1}{5x^5}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{x^{3}}+\dfrac{3}{2x^{4}}-\dfrac{1}{5x^{5}}+C$.

### 20 [D]
$\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt x}=\left(x^{\frac32}\right)^{\frac13}=x^{\frac12}$, demak integral ostida $x^{\frac32}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac32}dx=\dfrac25x^{\frac52}+C=\dfrac25x^2\sqrt x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{5}x^{2}\sqrt{x}+C$.

### 21 [B]
Suratni köpaytuvçilarga ajratamiz: $2x^2+x-6=(2x-3)(x+2)$.
$\displaystyle\int(2x-3)dx=x^2-3x+C$.

Javob: $x^{2}-3x+C$.

### 22 [C]
Jadval integrallari: $\displaystyle\int\cos x\,dx=\sin x$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{1+x^2}=\operatorname{arctg}x$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\cos^2x}=\operatorname{tg}x$.
$-4\sin x+3\operatorname{arctg}x+2\operatorname{tg}x+C$.

Javob: $-4\sin x+3\operatorname{arctg} x+2\operatorname{tg} x+C$.

### 23 [D]
$\operatorname{ctg}^2x=\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}-1$.
$\displaystyle\int2\left(\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}-1\right)dx=-2\operatorname{ctg}x-2x+C$.

Javob: $-2\operatorname{ctg} x-2x+C$.

### 24 [D]
$\left(\sin\dfrac x2-\cos\dfrac x2\right)^2=1-2\sin\dfrac x2\cos\dfrac x2=1-\sin x$.
$\displaystyle\int6(1-\sin x)dx=6x+6\cos x+C$.

Javob: $6x+6\cos x+C$.

### 25 [C]
Integral ostidagi funksiya boşlanğiç funksiyaning hosilasiga teng: $xf(x)=(x^3+e^x)'=3x^2+e^x$.
$f(x)=3x+\dfrac{e^x}{x}$.

Javob: $3x+\dfrac{e^x}{x}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [C]
$\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac43x-5\right)dx=\dfrac43\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}-5x+C=\dfrac23x^2-5x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{3}x^{2}-5x+C$.

### 2 [D]
$\dfrac32\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{3}-4\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+2x+C=\dfrac12x^3-2x^2+2x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}x^{3}-2x^{2}+2x+C$.

### 3 [C]
$-5\cdot\dfrac{x^5}{5}+12\cdot\dfrac{x^4}{4}+2\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}-3x+C=-x^5+3x^4+x^2-3x+C$.

Javob: $-x^{5}+3x^{4}+x^{2}-3x+C$.

### 4 [B]
$x^3\sqrt x=x^{\frac72}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac72}dx=\dfrac{x^{\frac92}}{\frac92}+C=\dfrac29x^4\sqrt x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{9}x^{4}\sqrt{x}+C$.

### 5 [C]
$f(x)=x^{\frac54}-2x^{\frac12}$.
$\dfrac{x^{\frac94}}{\frac94}-2\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac32}}{\frac32}+C=\dfrac49x^2\sqrt[4]{x}-\dfrac43x\sqrt x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{9}x^{2}\sqrt[4]{x}-\dfrac{4}{3}x\sqrt{x}+C$.

### 6 [D]
$f(x)=2x^{-\frac13}-x^{-\frac12}$.
$2\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac23}}{\frac23}-\dfrac{x^{\frac12}}{\frac12}+C=3\sqrt[3]{x^2}-2\sqrt x+C$.

Javob: $3\sqrt[3]{x^{2}}-2\sqrt{x}+C$.

### 7 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x^2}=-\dfrac1x$.
$-2\ln|x|-\dfrac5x+C$.

Javob: $-2\ln|x|-\dfrac{5}{x}+C$.

### 8 [C]
$\displaystyle\int a^xdx=\dfrac{a^x}{\ln a}+C$:
$\dfrac{6^x}{\ln6}-\dfrac{2\cdot3^x}{\ln3}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{6^x}{\ln 6}-\dfrac{2\cdot 3^x}{\ln 3}+C$.

### 9 [C]
$-3e^x+\dfrac{4\cdot7^x}{\ln7}+2\ln|x|+C$.

Javob: $-3e^x+\dfrac{4\cdot 7^x}{\ln 7}+2\ln|x|+C$.

### 10 [B]
$\displaystyle\int\cos x\,dx=\sin x$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\sin^2x}=-\operatorname{ctg}x$.
$-5\sin x-4\operatorname{ctg}x+C$.

Javob: $-5\sin x-4\operatorname{ctg} x+C$.

### 11 [A]
$F(x)=x^4-3x^2+5x+C$.
$F(2)=9$: $16-12+10+C=9 \Rightarrow C=-5$.

Javob: $x^{4}-3x^{2}+5x-5$.

### 12 [A]
$f(x)=-3x^{\frac12}+8x^{\frac13}$, $F(x)=-2x^{\frac32}+6x^{\frac43}+C$.
$F(1)=5$: $-2+6+C=5 \Rightarrow C=1$.

Javob: $-2x\sqrt{x}+6x\sqrt[3]{x}+1$.

### 13 [D]
$F(x)=4\ln|x|+C$.
$F(2)=\ln48$: $4\ln2+C=\ln48 \Rightarrow C=\ln48-\ln16=\ln3$.

Javob: $4\ln|x|+\ln3$.

### 14 [B]
$F(x)=3e^x-x+C$.
$F(0)=5$: $3+C=5 \Rightarrow C=2$.

Javob: $3e^x-x+2$.

### 15 [D]
$F(x)=-3\cos x+2\sin x+C$.
$F(0)=1$: $-3+C=1 \Rightarrow C=4$.

Javob: $-3\cos x+2\sin x+4$.

### 16 [B]
$F(x)=4\operatorname{arctg}x+C$.
$F(-1)=0$: $4\cdot\left(-\dfrac\pi4\right)+C=0 \Rightarrow C=\pi$.

Javob: $4\operatorname{arctg} x+\pi$.

### 17 [D]
$F(x)=-4\arcsin x+C$.
$F\left(\dfrac12\right)=0$: $-4\cdot\dfrac\pi6+C=0 \Rightarrow C=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $-4\arcsin x+\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}$.

### 18 [C]
$(x-3)(1-2x)=-2x^2+7x-3$.
$\displaystyle\int(-2x^2+7x-3)dx=-\dfrac23x^3+\dfrac72x^2-3x+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{2}{3}x^{3}+\dfrac{7}{2}x^{2}-3x+C$.

### 19 [A]
$(x^{-3}-2x^{-2})^2=x^{-6}-4x^{-5}+4x^{-4}$.
$\dfrac{x^{-5}}{-5}-4\cdot\dfrac{x^{-4}}{-4}+4\cdot\dfrac{x^{-3}}{-3}+C=-\dfrac{4}{3x^3}+\dfrac{1}{x^4}-\dfrac{1}{5x^5}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{4}{3x^{3}}+\dfrac{1}{x^{4}}-\dfrac{1}{5x^{5}}+C$.

### 20 [A]
$\sqrt[3]{x^2\sqrt x}=\left(x^{\frac52}\right)^{\frac13}=x^{\frac56}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac56}dx=\dfrac{6}{11}x^{\frac{11}{6}}+C=\dfrac{6}{11}x\sqrt[6]{x^5}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{6}{11}x\sqrt[6]{x^{5}}+C$.

### 21 [B]
$6x^2+x-2=(2x-1)(3x+2)$, demak integral ostida $3x+2$.
$\displaystyle\int(3x+2)dx=\dfrac32x^2+2x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3}{2}x^{2}+2x+C$.

### 22 [A]
Jadval integrallari bilan:
$5\sin x+4\operatorname{arctg}x-3\operatorname{tg}x+C$.

Javob: $5\sin x+4\operatorname{arctg} x-3\operatorname{tg} x+C$.

### 23 [B]
$\operatorname{tg}^2x=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}-1$, demak $3\operatorname{tg}^2x+1=\dfrac{3}{\cos^2x}-2$.
$\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{3}{\cos^2x}-2\right)dx=3\operatorname{tg}x-2x+C$.

Javob: $3\operatorname{tg} x-2x+C$.

### 24 [A]
$\left(\sin\dfrac x2+\cos\dfrac x2\right)^2=1+\sin x$, demak integral ostida $2-\sin x$.
$\displaystyle\int(2-\sin x)dx=2x+\cos x+C$.

Javob: $2x+\cos x+C$.

### 25 [C]
$\dfrac{f(x)}{x^2}=(2x^3-\cos x)'=6x^2+\sin x$.
$f(x)=6x^4+x^2\sin x$.

Javob: $6x^4+x^2\sin x$.

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\left(-\dfrac32x+6\right)dx=-\dfrac32\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+6x+C=-\dfrac34x^2+6x+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{4}x^{2}+6x+C$.

### 2 [A]
$9\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{3}-6\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+7x+C=3x^3-3x^2+7x+C$.

Javob: $3x^{3}-3x^{2}+7x+C$.

### 3 [C]
$15\cdot\dfrac{x^5}{5}-2\cdot\dfrac{x^4}{4}-9\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}+4x+C=3x^5-\dfrac12x^4-\dfrac92x^2+4x+C$.

Javob: $3x^{5}-\dfrac{1}{2}x^{4}-\dfrac{9}{2}x^{2}+4x+C$.

### 4 [C]
$\sqrt[4]{x^3}=x^{\frac34}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac34}dx=\dfrac{x^{\frac74}}{\frac74}+C=\dfrac47x\sqrt[4]{x^3}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{7}x\sqrt[4]{x^{3}}+C$.

### 5 [B]
$f(x)=3x^{\frac43}-5x^{\frac14}$.
$3\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac73}}{\frac73}-5\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac54}}{\frac54}+C=\dfrac97x^2\sqrt[3]{x}-4x\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{9}{7}x^{2}\sqrt[3]{x}-4x\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

### 6 [A]
$f(x)=4x^{-\frac13}+3x^{-\frac34}$.
$4\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac23}}{\frac23}+3\cdot\dfrac{x^{\frac14}}{\frac14}+C=6\sqrt[3]{x^2}+12\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

Javob: $6\sqrt[3]{x^{2}}+12\sqrt[4]{x}+C$.

### 7 [D]
$6\ln|x|+4\cdot\left(-\dfrac1x\right)+C=6\ln|x|-\dfrac4x+C$.

Javob: $6\ln|x|-\dfrac{4}{x}+C$.

### 8 [C]
$\displaystyle\int a^xdx=\dfrac{a^x}{\ln a}+C$:
$\dfrac{3\cdot2^x}{\ln2}+\dfrac{5^x}{\ln5}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3\cdot 2^x}{\ln 2}+\dfrac{5^x}{\ln 5}+C$.

### 9 [A]
$4e^x-\dfrac{5\cdot2^x}{\ln2}+3\ln|x|+C$.

Javob: $4e^x-\dfrac{5\cdot 2^x}{\ln 2}+3\ln|x|+C$.

### 10 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x$, $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\cos^2x}=\operatorname{tg}x$.
$-3\cos x-5\operatorname{tg}x+C$.

Javob: $-3\cos x-5\operatorname{tg} x+C$.

### 11 [D]
$F(x)=x^3+4x^2-2x+C$.
$F(1)=7$: $1+4-2+C=7 \Rightarrow C=4$.

Javob: $x^{3}+4x^{2}-2x+4$.

### 12 [A]
$f(x)=5x^{\frac32}-9x^{\frac12}$, $F(x)=2x^{\frac52}-6x^{\frac32}+C$.
$F(4)=20$: $2\cdot32-6\cdot8+C=20 \Rightarrow 16+C=20 \Rightarrow C=4$.

Javob: $2x^{2}\sqrt{x}-6x\sqrt{x}+4$.

### 13 [B]
$F(x)=2\ln|x|+C$.
$F(5)=\ln100$: $\ln25+C=\ln100 \Rightarrow C=\ln4$.

Javob: $2\ln|x|+\ln4$.

### 14 [D]
$F(x)=5^x-x^2+C$.
$F(1)=7$: $5-1+C=7 \Rightarrow C=3$.

Javob: $5^x-x^{2}+3$.

### 15 [D]
$F(x)=5\sin x-2\cos x+C$.
$F\left(\dfrac\pi2\right)=3$: $5-0+C=3 \Rightarrow C=-2$.

Javob: $5\sin x-2\cos x-2$.

### 16 [B]
$F(x)=\operatorname{arctg}x+C$.
$\operatorname{arctg}\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}=\dfrac\pi6$, demak $\dfrac\pi6+C=\dfrac\pi2 \Rightarrow C=\dfrac\pi3$.

Javob: $\operatorname{arctg} x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

### 17 [B]
$F(x)=6\arcsin x+C$.
$F\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=3\pi$: $6\cdot\dfrac\pi3+C=3\pi \Rightarrow C=\pi$.

Javob: $6\arcsin x+\pi$.

### 18 [C]
$(3x+1)(x-2)=3x^2-5x-2$.
$\displaystyle\int(3x^2-5x-2)dx=x^3-\dfrac52x^2-2x+C$.

Javob: $x^{3}-\dfrac{5}{2}x^{2}-2x+C$.

### 19 [A]
$(2x^{-2}+x^{-4})^2=4x^{-4}+4x^{-6}+x^{-8}$.
$4\cdot\dfrac{x^{-3}}{-3}+4\cdot\dfrac{x^{-5}}{-5}+\dfrac{x^{-7}}{-7}+C=-\dfrac{4}{3x^3}-\dfrac{4}{5x^5}-\dfrac{1}{7x^7}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{4}{3x^{3}}-\dfrac{4}{5x^{5}}-\dfrac{1}{7x^{7}}+C$.

### 20 [D]
$\sqrt{x\sqrt[3]{x^2}}=\left(x^{\frac53}\right)^{\frac12}=x^{\frac56}$, demak integral ostida $x^{\frac{11}{6}}$.
$\displaystyle\int x^{\frac{11}{6}}dx=\dfrac{6}{17}x^{\frac{17}{6}}+C=\dfrac{6}{17}x^2\sqrt[6]{x^5}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{6}{17}x^{2}\sqrt[6]{x^{5}}+C$.

### 21 [B]
$3x^2+5x-2=(3x-1)(x+2)$, demak integral ostida $x+2$.
$\displaystyle\int(x+2)dx=\dfrac12x^2+2x+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}x^{2}+2x+C$.

### 22 [C]
Jadval integrallari bilan:
$-2\sin x-5\operatorname{arctg}x+6\operatorname{tg}x+C$.

Javob: $-2\sin x-5\operatorname{arctg} x+6\operatorname{tg} x+C$.

### 23 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}^2x-2=\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}-3$.
$\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}-3\right)dx=-\operatorname{ctg}x-3x+C$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{ctg} x-3x+C$.

### 24 [C]
$\cos^2\dfrac x2-\sin^2\dfrac x2=\cos x$, demak integral ostida $4\cos x+3$.
$\displaystyle\int(4\cos x+3)dx=4\sin x+3x+C$.

Javob: $4\sin x+3x+C$.

### 25 [D]
$x^2f(x)=(x^4\sin x)'=4x^3\sin x+x^4\cos x$.
$f(x)=4x\sin x+x^2\cos x$.

Javob: $4x\sin x+x^2\cos x$.
