# 75. Trigonometrik funksiyalar va ularning xossalari

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik $[-1;1]$ oraliqda tebranadi, $x=0$ da $y=-1$ (eng kiçik qiymat), $x=\pi$ da $y=1$.
Bu $y=-\cos x$ grafigi. Keltiriş formulasi bilan $\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=-\cos x$.

Javob: $y=\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.

### 2 [A]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik $y=\operatorname{tg}x$ kabi ösuvçi tarmoqlardan iborat, asimptotalar $x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, lekin $x=0$ da $y=-1$.
Demak, $y=\operatorname{tg}x$ grafigi $1$ birlik pastga suriligan: $y=\operatorname{tg}x-1$.

Javob: $y=\operatorname{tg}x-1$.

### 3 [D]
$\cos^2t=\dfrac{1+\cos2t}{2}$, demak $f(x)=\dfrac12+\dfrac12\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}+2\right)$.
$\cos(kx+b)$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{|k|}$: $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{1/2}=4\pi$.

Javob: $4\pi$.

### 4 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}(kx+b)$ ning davri $\dfrac{\pi}{|k|}$.
$T=\dfrac{\pi}{1/3}=3\pi$.

Javob: $3\pi$.

### 5 [A]
Ikki karrali burçak formulasi: $\cos^2 2x-\sin^2 2x=\cos4x$.
Davri: $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 6 [B]
$\sin6x$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\cos4x$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
Yiğindining davri bu davrlarning eng kiçik umumiy karralisi: $\pi$.

Javob: $\pi$.

### 7 [D]
$f(-x)=\dfrac{\sin(-x)}{(-x)^4+1}=\dfrac{-\sin x}{x^4+1}=-f(x)$.
Aniqlaniş sohasi butun son öqi (simmetrik), demak funksiya toq.

Javob: Toq.

### 8 [C]
$\cos^2x=1-\sin^2x$ ni qöyamiz: $f(x)=5\sin^2x+2-2\sin^2x=2+3\sin^2x$.
$\sin^2x\le1$, demak eng katta qiymat $2+3=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 9 [A]
$\sin^2x=1-\cos^2x$: $f(x)=7\cos^2x+4-4\cos^2x=4+3\cos^2x$.
$\cos^2x\ge0$, eng kiçik qiymat $4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 10 [D]
$-1\le\sin4x\le1$, demak $-2\le-2\sin4x\le2$.
$5$ ni qöşsak: $3\le f(x)\le7$.

Javob: $[3; 7]$.

### 11 [A]
Tangens $(90^\circ;180^\circ)$ oraliqda ösuvçi.
$129^\circ<136^\circ$, demak $\operatorname{tg}129^\circ<\operatorname{tg}136^\circ$.

Javob: $a<b$.

### 12 [C]
Kosinus $[0^\circ;180^\circ]$ da kamayuvçi: burçak qança katta bölsa, kosinus şunça kiçik.
$150^\circ>110^\circ>70^\circ>30^\circ$, demak $d<c<b<a$.

Javob: $d<c<b<a$.

### 13 [D]
Taqribiy qiymatlar ($\pi\approx3{,}14$): $\sin2\approx0{,}91$, $\sin3=\sin(\pi-3)\approx0{,}14$.
$\sin5=-\sin(2\pi-5)\approx-\sin1{,}28\approx-0{,}96$, $\sin6=-\sin(2\pi-6)\approx-\sin0{,}28\approx-0{,}28$.
Demak, $c<d<b<a$.

Javob: $c<d<b<a$.

### 14 [B]
$a\cos x+b\sin x$ ning qiymatlar sohasi $\left[-\sqrt{a^2+b^2};\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\right]$.
$\sqrt{4+5}=3$, demak $[-3;3]$.

Javob: $[-3; 3]$.

### 15 [C]
$\sqrt3\sin x-\cos x=2\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin x-\dfrac12\cos x\right)=2\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\in[-2;2]$.
Kub ösuvçi funksiya: $[-8;8]$.

Javob: $[-8; 8]$.

### 16 [A]
$\dfrac{\sin2x}{\sin x}=\dfrac{2\sin x\cos x}{\sin x}=2\cos x$, bunda $\sin x\ne0$.
$\sin x\ne0$ da $\cos x\ne\pm1$, demak $\cos x\in(-1;1)$ va $f(x)\in(-2;2)$.

Javob: $(-2; 2)$.

### 17 [C]
$t=\sin x\in[-1;1]$: $g(t)=2t^2+t-1$, parabola uçi $t=-\dfrac14$.
$g\left(-\dfrac14\right)=\dfrac18-\dfrac14-1=-\dfrac98$, $g(1)=2$, $g(-1)=0$.
Demak, $\left[-\dfrac98;2\right]$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac98; 2\right]$.

### 18 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}x\cdot\operatorname{ctg}x=1$, $a+\dfrac1a\ge2$ ($a>0$) tengsizligi bilan: $\operatorname{tg}^2x+\operatorname{ctg}^2x\ge2$.
Tenglik $\operatorname{tg}^2x=1$ da, qiymat çeksiz katta bölişi mumkin.

Javob: $[2; +\infty)$.

### 19 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}x\cdot\operatorname{ctg}x=1$, lekin bunda $\sin x\ne0$, $\cos x\ne0$, ya'ni $\cos^2x\in(0;1)$.
$f(x)=1+2\cos^2x\in(1;3)$.

Javob: $(1; 3)$.

### 20 [B]
$\cos^4x+\sin^4x=(\cos^2x+\sin^2x)^2-2\sin^2x\cos^2x=1-\dfrac12\sin^2 2x\in\left[\dfrac12;1\right]$.
Teskari son: $f(x)\in[1;2]$.

Javob: $[1; 2]$.

### 21 [A]
$t=\sin x\in[-1;1]$. $[-1;1]\subset\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, bu yerda sinus ösuvçi.
Demak, $\sin t\in[-\sin1;\sin1]$.

Javob: $[-\sin1; \sin1]$.

### 22 [D]
Maxraj: $3\sin^2x+4\cos^2x=3+\cos^2x\in[3;4]$.
$\dfrac{4}{4}=1$ dan $\dfrac43$ gaça: $\left[1;\dfrac43\right]$.

Javob: $\left[1; \dfrac43\right]$.

### 23 [B]
$\cos x-1\le0$, tenglik $x=2\pi k$ da. Köpaytma musbat bölişi uçun $\cos x-1<0$ va $x^2-16<0$.
$-4<x<4$ va $x\ne0$ (bu oraliqda $\cos x=1$ faqat $x=0$ da).
Butun yeçimlar: $\pm1,\pm2,\pm3$ — $6$ ta.

Javob: $6$.

### 24 [C]
Kublar ayirmasi: $\cos^6 3x-\sin^6 3x=(\cos^2 3x-\sin^2 3x)(1-\sin^2 3x\cos^2 3x)=\cos6x\left(1-\dfrac14\sin^2 6x\right)$.
$\cos6x$ ning davri $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, ikkinçi köpaytuvçining davri $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$; $x\to x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ da $\cos6x$ işorasini almaştiradi.
Demak, eng kiçik davr $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

### 25 [B]
$\sin x$ toq, toq funksiyaning toq daraja körsatkiçi toq funksiya beradi, juft daraja — juft.
$3n+2$ toq bölişi uçun $3n$ toq, ya'ni $n$ toq bölişi kerak.

Javob: $n$ toq natural son.

### 26 [D]
$\cos^2x\le1$, eng katta qiymat $2+k^2$.
$2+k^2=30\Rightarrow k^2=28\Rightarrow k=\pm2\sqrt7$.

Javob: $\pm2\sqrt{7}$.

### 27 [C]
$12\cos x+a\sin x$ ning eng kiçik qiymati $-\sqrt{144+a^2}$.
$\sqrt{144+a^2}=13\Rightarrow a^2=25\Rightarrow a=\pm5$.

Javob: $\pm5$.

### 28 [D]
$m=\dfrac{3/2-7\cos x}{2}$, $\cos x\in[-1;1]$ bölgani uçun $m\in\left[\dfrac{3/2-7}{2};\dfrac{3/2+7}{2}\right]=\left[-\dfrac{11}{4};\dfrac{17}{4}\right]$.
Butun qiymatlar: $-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4$, yiğindisi $7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 29 [B]
$\cos x+2>0$ har doim, demak $\log_2(x+1)-2\ge0$.
$\log_2(x+1)\ge2\Rightarrow x+1\ge4\Rightarrow x\ge3$.

Javob: $[3; +\infty)$.

### 30 [C]
Ayirma kosinusi formulasi: $f(x)=\cos(kx-x)=\cos(k-1)x$.
Davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{k-1}=\dfrac{\pi}{5}\Rightarrow k-1=10\Rightarrow k=11$.

Javob: $11$.

## 2-test

### 1 [A]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik kosinusoida şaklida, $x=0$ da $y=0$ (eng katta qiymat), $x=\pm\pi$ da $y=-2$.
Demak, $y=\cos x$ grafigi $1$ birlik pastga suriligan: $y=\cos x-1$.

Javob: $y=\cos x-1$.

### 2 [B]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik kamayuvçi tarmoqlardan iborat, asimptotalar $x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $x=0$ da $y=0$ — bu $y=-\operatorname{tg}x$ grafigi.
Keltiriş formulasi: $\operatorname{ctg}\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=-\operatorname{tg}x$.

Javob: $y=\operatorname{ctg}\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.

### 3 [A]
$\sin^3t$ ning davri $\sin t$ niki bilan bir xil, ya'ni $2\pi$.
$t=\dfrac{x}{3}-\dfrac{\pi}{5}$ bölgani uçun $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{1/3}=6\pi$.

Javob: $6\pi$.

### 4 [C]
$\operatorname{tg}^2t$ ning davri $\pi$.
$t=\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{\pi}{5}$: $T=\dfrac{\pi}{1/2}=2\pi$.

Javob: $2\pi$.

### 5 [B]
$1-2\sin^2 3x=\cos6x$.
Davri: $\dfrac{2\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

### 6 [D]
$\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{3}$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/3}=6$, $\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/4}=8$.
Eng kiçik umumiy karrali: $\text{EKUK}(6;8)=24$.

Javob: $24$.

### 7 [D]
$f(x)=\cos x+\operatorname{tg}x$. $f(-x)=\cos x-\operatorname{tg}x$.
Bu na $f(x)$ ga, na $-f(x)$ ga teng (masalan, $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ da tekşirish mumkin).

Javob: Juft ham emas, toq ham emas.

### 8 [C]
$\cos2x=2\cos^2x-1$: $f(x)=5\cos^2x-1$.
$\cos^2x\le1$, eng katta qiymat $4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 9 [C]
$\cos2x=1-2\sin^2x$: $f(x)=3-6\sin^2x+2\sin^2x=3-4\sin^2x$.
Eng kiçik qiymat $\sin^2x=1$ da: $-1$.

Javob: $-1$.

### 10 [D]
$0\le\sin^2x\le1\Rightarrow0\le3\sin^2x\le3\Rightarrow-2\le f(x)\le1$.

Javob: $[-2; 1]$.

### 11 [B]
$\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{7}=\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{7}$, $\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{5}=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5}$.
$\dfrac{2\pi}{7}>\dfrac{\pi}{5}$ va sinus $\left[0;\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ da ösuvçi, demak $a>b$.

Javob: $a>b$.

### 12 [C]
$d=\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{4}=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}<0$, qolganlari musbat.
$c=\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. $\dfrac{\pi}{8}<\dfrac{\pi}{4}<\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ va sinus ösuvçi: $a<b<c$.
Demak, $d<a<b<c$.

Javob: $d<a<b<c$.

### 13 [A]
$1$ radian $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ da, şuning uçun $\cos1<\sin1<1$.
$\operatorname{tg}1>\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{4}=1$.
Demak, $c<b<a<d$.

Javob: $c<b<a<d$.

### 14 [C]
$a\cos x+b\sin x$ ning qiymatlar sohasi $\left[-\sqrt{a^2+b^2};\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\right]$.
$\sqrt{64+225}=\sqrt{289}=17$.

Javob: $[-17; 17]$.

### 15 [D]
$f(x)=2+3\cos x+4\sin x$. $3\cos x+4\sin x\in[-5;5]$, çunki $\sqrt{9+16}=5$.
$f(x)\in[-3;7]$.

Javob: $[-3; 7]$.

### 16 [B]
$1-\sin2x=\sin^2x-2\sin x\cos x+\cos^2x=(\sin x-\cos x)^2$.
$f(x)=\sin x-\cos x=\sqrt2\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$, maxraj nol bölmasligi uçun $f(x)\ne0$.
Demak, $\left[-\sqrt2;0\right)\cup\left(0;\sqrt2\right]$.

Javob: $\left[-\sqrt2; 0\right)\cup\left(0; \sqrt2\right]$.

### 17 [C]
$t=\sin x\in[-1;1]$: $g(t)=t^2-6t+3$, uçi $t=3$, demak $[-1;1]$ da kamayuvçi.
$g(-1)=10$, $g(1)=-2$.

Javob: $[-2; 10]$.

### 18 [A]
$t=\operatorname{tg}x\ne0$: $f=4t+\dfrac1t$.
$t>0$ da $4t+\dfrac1t\ge2\sqrt{4}=4$; $t<0$ da simmetrik ravişda $f\le-4$.

Javob: $(-\infty; -4]\cup[4; +\infty)$.

### 19 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}x\cdot\operatorname{ctg}x=1$ ($\sin x\ne0$, $\cos x\ne0$): $f(x)=1-\sin^2x=\cos^2x$.
$\cos^2x\in(0;1)$, çunki $0$ va $1$ qiymatlar çiqarilgan.

Javob: $(0; 1)$.

### 20 [A]
$\cos^4x+\sin^4x=1-2\sin^2x\cos^2x=1-\dfrac12\sin^2 2x$.
$\sin^2 2x\in[0;1]$, demak $f(x)\in\left[\dfrac12;1\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac12; 1\right]$.

### 21 [B]
$t=2\sin x\in[-2;2]$. Kosinus juft, $[0;2]$ da kamayuvçi ($2<\pi$).
Demak, $\cos t\in[\cos2;1]$.

Javob: $[\cos2; 1]$.

### 22 [D]
Maxraj: $2\sin^2x+3\cos^2x=2+\cos^2x\in[2;3]$.
$\dfrac63=2$, $\dfrac62=3$: $[2;3]$.

Javob: $[2; 3]$.

### 23 [D]
$3-\sin x>0$ har doim, demak $\log_2x-3<0$.
$\log_2x<3\Rightarrow0<x<8$. Natural yeçimlar: $1,\dots,7$ — $7$ ta.

Javob: $7$.

### 24 [B]
$\sin^4x+\cos^4x=1-\dfrac12\sin^2 2x=1-\dfrac14(1-\cos4x)=\dfrac34+\dfrac14\cos4x$.
Davri: $\dfrac{2\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 25 [C]
$\cos x$ juft funksiya: $(\cos(-x))^{2n+1}=(\cos x)^{2n+1}$.
Bu har qanday $n$ uçun bajariladi.

Javob: har qanday natural $n$.

### 26 [A]
$\sin^2x\le1$, eng kiçik qiymat $3-k^2$.
$3-k^2=-13\Rightarrow k^2=16\Rightarrow k=\pm4$.

Javob: $\pm4$.

### 27 [D]
$a\cos x+6\sin x$ ning eng katta qiymati $\sqrt{a^2+36}$.
$\sqrt{a^2+36}=10\Rightarrow a^2=64\Rightarrow a=\pm8$.

Javob: $\pm8$.

### 28 [C]
$m=2\sin3x-1$, $\sin3x\in[-1;1]$, demak $m\in[-3;1]$.
Butun qiymatlar: $-3,-2,-1,0,1$, yiğindisi $-5$.

Javob: $-5$.

### 29 [A]
$2-\cos x>0$ har doim, demak $3^x-9<0$.
$3^x<3^2\Rightarrow x<2$.

Javob: $(-\infty; 2)$.

### 30 [B]
$\sin kx\cos kx=\dfrac12\sin2kx$, davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{2k}=\dfrac{\pi}{k}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{k}=\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Rightarrow k=8$.

Javob: $8$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik $[-2;2]$ oraliqda tebranadi (amplituda $2$), $x=0$ da $y=2$, $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ da $y=-2$, ya'ni davr $\pi$.
$y=A\cos kx$ uçun $A=2$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{k}=\pi\Rightarrow k=2$.

Javob: $y=2\cos2x$.

### 2 [A]
Çizmadan körinadiki, grafik kamayuvçi tarmoqlardan iborat, asimptotalar $x=k\pi$ — bu kotangens.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ da $y=-1$ (oddiy $\operatorname{ctg}$ da $0$), demak grafik $1$ birlik pastga suriligan: $y=\operatorname{ctg}x-1$.

Javob: $y=\operatorname{ctg}x-1$.

### 3 [D]
$\sin^4t=(\sin^2t)^2$, $\sin^2t=\dfrac{1-\cos2t}{2}$ ning davri $\pi$, $\sin^4t$ niki ham $\pi$.
$t=2x-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$: $T=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 4 [C]
$\operatorname{ctg}^2t$ ning davri $\pi$, $t=2x$ bölgani uçun $T=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

### 5 [D]
Ayirma sinusi formulasi: $f(x)=\sin(5x-2x)=\sin3x$.
Davri: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

### 6 [A]
$\sin4x$ ning davri $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $\cos^3 6x$ ning davri $\dfrac{2\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{x}{2}$ ning davri $2\pi$.
Eng kiçik umumiy karrali: $2\pi$.

Javob: $2\pi$.

### 7 [C]
$\cos(-x)=\cos x$, demak surat $\arccos(\cos x)$ juft funksiya; $\operatorname{tg}x$ esa toq.
Juft funksiyaning toq funksiyaga nisbati toq: $f(-x)=\dfrac{\arccos(\cos x)}{-\operatorname{tg}x}=-f(x)$.

Javob: Toq.

### 8 [A]
$2\sin^2x=1-\cos2x$: $f(x)=1+(\sqrt3-1)\cos2x$.
$\sqrt3-1>0$, eng katta qiymat $\cos2x=1$ da: $1+\sqrt3-1=\sqrt3$.

Javob: $\sqrt{3}$.

### 9 [B]
$\cos2x=2\cos^2x-1$: $f(x)=5\cos^2x-2\cos^2x+1+3=3\cos^2x+4$.
Eng kiçik qiymat $\cos^2x=0$ da: $4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 10 [B]
$2\sin^2x=1-\cos2x$: $f(x)=1+2\cos2x$.
$\cos2x\in[-1;1]$, demak $f(x)\in[-1;3]$.

Javob: $[-1; 3]$.

### 11 [C]
Keltiriş formulasi: $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{5\pi}{14}=\operatorname{tg}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{5\pi}{14}\right)=\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{2\pi}{14}=\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{7}$.
Demak, $a=b$.

Javob: $a=b$.

### 12 [A]
$c=\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{6}=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, $d=\cos\dfrac{4\pi}{3}=-\dfrac12$.
$a$ va $b$ musbat, $\dfrac{\pi}{7}<\dfrac{2\pi}{5}$ va kosinus kamayuvçi: $b<a$.
Demak, $c<d<b<a$.

Javob: $c<d<b<a$.

### 13 [D]
$2$ radian II çorakda ($\dfrac{\pi}{2}<2<\pi$): $\sin2\approx0{,}91>0$, $\cos2\approx-0{,}42$, $\operatorname{tg}2\approx-2{,}19$.
Demak, $\operatorname{tg}2<\cos2<\sin2<2$, ya'ni $d<c<b<a$.

Javob: $d<c<b<a$.

### 14 [A]
$-3\sin x+4\cos x\in[-5;5]$, çunki $\sqrt{9+16}=5$.
$f(x)\in[4-5;4+5]=[-1;9]$.

Javob: $[-1; 9]$.

### 15 [C]
$2\cos^2x=1+\cos2x$: $f(x)=1+\cos2x+2\sqrt2\sin2x$.
$\cos2x+2\sqrt2\sin2x\in[-3;3]$, çunki $\sqrt{1+8}=3$.

Javob: $[-2; 4]$.

### 16 [A]
$\dfrac{\sin2x}{2\cos x}=\dfrac{2\sin x\cos x}{2\cos x}=\sin x$, bunda $\cos x\ne0$.
$\cos x\ne0$ da $\sin x\ne\pm1$, demak $f(x)\in(-1;1)$.

### 17 [D]

Uçi $t=\dfrac12$: $g=-\dfrac14+\dfrac12-1=-\dfrac34$. $g(-1)=-3$, $g(1)=-1$.

Javob: $\left[-3; -\dfrac34\right]$.

### 18 [C]

### 19 [D]

### 20 [B]

### 22 [A]

### 24 [B]
