# 72. Yiğindi va köpaytma uçun formulalar

## 1-test

### 1 [B]
Sinuslar ayirmasi formulasi: $\sin a-\sin b=2\cos\dfrac{a+b}{2}\sin\dfrac{a-b}{2}$.
$\sin16^\circ-\sin54^\circ=2\cos35^\circ\sin(-19^\circ)=-2\cos35^\circ\sin19^\circ$.

Javob: $-2\cos35^\circ\sin19^\circ$.

### 2 [D]
Sinuslar yiğindisi formulasi: $\sin a+\sin b=2\sin\dfrac{a+b}{2}\cos\dfrac{a-b}{2}$.
$\sin75^\circ+\sin15^\circ=2\sin45^\circ\cos30^\circ=2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$.

### 3 [A]
Sinuslar ayirmasi formulasini qöllaymiz: $\sin a-\sin b=2\cos\dfrac{a+b}{2}\sin\dfrac{a-b}{2}$.
$\sin9x-\sin3x=2\cos\dfrac{12x}{2}\sin\dfrac{6x}{2}=2\cos6x\sin3x$.

Javob: $2\cos6x\sin3x$.

### 4 [D]
Kosinuslar ayirmasi formulasi: $\cos a-\cos b=-2\sin\dfrac{a+b}{2}\sin\dfrac{a-b}{2}$.
$\cos10^\circ-\cos110^\circ=-2\sin60^\circ\sin(-50^\circ)=2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin50^\circ=\sqrt3\sin50^\circ$.
Demak, kasr $\dfrac{\sqrt3\sin50^\circ}{\sin50^\circ}=\sqrt3$.

Javob: $\sqrt{3}$.

### 5 [C]
Surat: $\sin50^\circ+\sin10^\circ=2\sin30^\circ\cos20^\circ=\cos20^\circ$.
Maxrajda $\sin70^\circ=\cos20^\circ$, şuning uçun $9\cos20^\circ-5\cos20^\circ=4\cos20^\circ$.
Natija: $\dfrac{\cos20^\circ}{4\cos20^\circ}=\dfrac14$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

### 6 [A]
Surat: $\sin100^\circ+\sin50^\circ=2\sin75^\circ\cos25^\circ$.
Maxraj: $\cos100^\circ+\cos50^\circ=2\cos75^\circ\cos25^\circ$.
Kasr $\operatorname{tg}75^\circ=\operatorname{tg}(45^\circ+30^\circ)=\dfrac{1+\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}{1-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}=\dfrac{3+\sqrt3}{3-\sqrt3}=2+\sqrt3$.

Javob: $2+\sqrt{3}$.

### 7 [D]
Surat: $\sin92^\circ-\sin2^\circ=2\cos47^\circ\sin45^\circ=\sqrt2\cos47^\circ$.
Maxrajda $\sin43^\circ=\cos47^\circ$, demak maxraj $5\sqrt2\cos47^\circ+\sqrt2\cos47^\circ=6\sqrt2\cos47^\circ$.
Natija: $\dfrac{\sqrt2\cos47^\circ}{6\sqrt2\cos47^\circ}=\dfrac16$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{6}$.

### 8 [A]
Surat: $\sin\alpha-\sin5\alpha=2\cos3\alpha\sin(-2\alpha)=-2\cos3\alpha\sin2\alpha$.
Maxraj: $\cos\alpha+\cos5\alpha=2\cos3\alpha\cos2\alpha$.
Bölsak: $-\dfrac{\sin2\alpha}{\cos2\alpha}=-\operatorname{tg}2\alpha$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{tg}2\alpha$.

### 9 [B]
Birinçi kasr: $\sin\alpha-\sin3\alpha=-2\cos2\alpha\sin\alpha$, $\cos\alpha-\cos3\alpha=2\sin2\alpha\sin\alpha$, demak u $-\dfrac{\cos2\alpha}{\sin2\alpha}$ ga teng.
Ikkinçi köpaytuvçi: $\dfrac{1-\cos4\alpha}{2}=\sin^2 2\alpha$.
Köpaytma: $-\cos2\alpha\sin2\alpha=-\dfrac{\sin4\alpha}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sin4\alpha}{2}$.

### 10 [D]
Surat: $\cos\alpha-\cos5\alpha=-2\sin3\alpha\sin(-2\alpha)=2\sin3\alpha\sin2\alpha$.
Maxraj: $\cos\alpha+\cos5\alpha=2\cos3\alpha\cos2\alpha$.
Kasr: $\dfrac{\sin3\alpha\sin2\alpha}{\cos3\alpha\cos2\alpha}=\operatorname{tg}2\alpha\operatorname{tg}3\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}2\alpha\operatorname{tg}3\alpha$.

### 11 [A]
Surat: $\cos(\alpha-\beta)-\cos(\alpha+\beta)=2\sin\alpha\sin\beta$.
Maxraj: $\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)=2\sin\alpha\cos\beta$.
Kasr: $\dfrac{2\sin\alpha\sin\beta}{2\sin\alpha\cos\beta}=\operatorname{tg}\beta$.

Javob: $\operatorname{tg}\beta$.

### 12 [D]

Qisqartirsak: $\dfrac{\cos3\alpha}{\sin3\alpha}=\operatorname{ctg}3\alpha$.

Javob: $\operatorname{ctg}3\alpha$.

### 13 [C]
$\sin3\alpha+\sin7\alpha=2\sin5\alpha\cos2\alpha$, şuning uçun surat $\sin5\alpha(2\cos2\alpha+1)$.
Maxrajga bölsak: $\sin5\alpha$.

Javob: $\sin5\alpha$.

### 14 [A]

### 16 [B]

Natija: $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\sin\dfrac{\pi}{18}=\dfrac12+\sin\dfrac{\pi}{18}$.

Javob: $\dfrac12+\sin\dfrac{\pi}{18}$.

Javob: $\dfrac12(\sin2\alpha+\sin2\beta)$.

### 16 [D]

### 17 [A]

### 18 [C]

Ayirma: $\dfrac12(\cos\alpha+\cos5\alpha)=\cos3\alpha\cos2\alpha$.
$\cos3\alpha$ ga bölsak: $\cos2\alpha$.

Javob: $\cos2\alpha$.

### 19 [B]

### 20 [D]

Javob: $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

### 21 [B]

### 25 [A]

### 26 [A]

### 27 [B]

### 29 [A]
