# 60. Körsatkiçli tengsizliklar

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
Asos $5>1$ bölganda $y=5^x$ ösuvçi: katta darajaga katta qiymat mos keladi.
$1{,}4<2{,}1\Rightarrow5^{1{,}4}<5^{2{,}1}$.

Javob: $<$.

### 2 [D]
Asos $\dfrac94>1$, demak funksiya ösuvçi.
$\dfrac52>\dfrac32\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac94\Bigr)^{\frac52}>\Bigl(\dfrac94\Bigr)^{\frac32}$.

Javob: $>$.

### 3 [B]
$0{,}25^{0{,}5}=\sqrt{0{,}25}=0{,}5$.
$0{,}5<1$.

Javob: $<$.

### 4 [D]
$25\sqrt5=5^2\cdot5^{\frac12}=5^{\frac52}$.
Asos $5>1$, belgi saqlanadi: $x>\dfrac52$.

Javob: $x>\dfrac52$.

### 5 [C]
$\dfrac1{25}=\Bigl(\dfrac15\Bigr)^2$. Asos $\dfrac15<1$ bölgani uçun tengsizlik belgisi almaşadi.
$x\ge2$.

Javob: $x\ge2$.

### 6 [A]
$16=\Bigl(\dfrac14\Bigr)^{-2}$, asos $\dfrac14<1$, belgi almaşadi:
$\dfrac{x-2}{x-5}<-2\Rightarrow\dfrac{x-2+2(x-5)}{x-5}<0\Rightarrow\dfrac{3x-12}{x-5}<0$.
Intervallar usuli: $4<x<5$.

Javob: $4<x<5$.

### 7 [B]
$3^x$ ni qavsdan çiqaramiz: $3^x(1+3+9)=13\cdot3^x<117$.
$3^x<9\Rightarrow x<2$.

Javob: $x<2$.

### 8 [A]
Maxraj $2^x+8>0$ har doim, demak suratni musbat qilamiz.
$5^x>125=5^3\Rightarrow x>3$.

Javob: $x>3$.

### 9 [C]
$2^x-2$ ning işorasi $x-1$ ning işorasi bilan bir xil. $x^2-2x-8=(x-4)(x+2)$.
$(x+2)(x-1)(x-4)<0$, intervallar usuli: $x<-2$ yoki $1<x<4$.

Javob: $(-\infty;-2)\cup(1;4)$.

### 10 [B]
Asos $2>1$: $x^2-6x\le7\Rightarrow x^2-6x-7\le0\Rightarrow(x-7)(x+1)\le0$.
$-1\le x\le7$. Butun sonlar yiğindisi: $-1+0+1+\dots+7=27$.

Javob: $27$.

### 11 [C]
$\Bigl(\dfrac13\Bigr)^x-3>0\Leftrightarrow x<-1$; $2^x-16>0\Leftrightarrow x>4$.
Köpaytma manfiy bölişi uçun ikki qavs har xil işorali: $x<-1$ yoki $x>4$.
Qanoatlantirmaydigan butun sonlar: $-1,0,1,2,3,4$ — $6$ ta.

Javob: $6$.

### 12 [C]
$t=3^x>0$: $t^2-10t+9>0\Rightarrow(t-1)(t-9)>0$.
$t<1$ yoki $t>9$, ya'ni $x<0$ yoki $x>2$.

Javob: $x<0$ yoki $x>2$.

### 13 [A]
$t=3^x>0$, ikkala tomonni $t$ ga köpaytiramiz: $t^2-12t+27<0$.
$(t-3)(t-9)<0\Rightarrow3<t<9\Rightarrow1<x<2$.

Javob: $1<x<2$.

### 14 [D]
$t=3^x>0$, $t\ne9$. Agar $t>9$ bölsa, öng tomon manfiy, çap tomon musbat — tengsizlik bajarilmaydi.
$0<t<9$ da: $2(9-t)\le13(t+1)\Rightarrow5\le15t\Rightarrow t\ge\dfrac13$.
$\dfrac13\le3^x<9\Rightarrow-1\le x<2$.

Javob: $[-1;2)$.

### 15 [B]
Guruhlaymiz: $9^x(3^x-4^x)-81(3^x-4^x)=(3^x-4^x)(9^x-81)\ge0$.
$3^x-4^x\ge0\Leftrightarrow x\le0$; $9^x-81\ge0\Leftrightarrow x\ge2$.
Ikkala qavs bir xil işorali bölişi kerak: ikkalasi $\le0$ bölganda $0\le x\le2$ (ikkalasi musbat bölişi mumkin emas).

Javob: $[0;2]$.

### 16 [D]
Şartlar: $2^x-1\ge0\Rightarrow x\ge0$; $32-2^x>0\Rightarrow x<5$.

Javob: $[0;5)$.

### 17 [C]
$\Bigl(\dfrac13\Bigr)^x$ kamayuvçi, demak eng katta qiymat $x=-2$ da, eng kiçigi $x=1$ da.
$f(-2)=9$, $f(1)=\dfrac13$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac13;9\Bigr]$.

### 18 [B]
$x^2+6x=(x+3)^2-9\ge-9$, ya'ni daraja $[-9;+\infty)$ oraliqdagi qiymatlarni oladi.
$2^t$ ösuvçi: $f\ge2^{-9}=\dfrac1{512}$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac1{512};+\infty\Bigr)$.

### 19 [A]
$t=|x|$: $3^t(2^t-4)-3(2^t-4)=(3^t-3)(2^t-4)>0$.
Ikkala qavs musbat: $t>2$; ikkalasi manfiy: $t<1$.
$|x|<1$ yoki $|x|>2$.

Javob: $(-\infty;-2)\cup(-1;1)\cup(2;+\infty)$.

### 20 [D]
Çap tomon: $3^{x-5}(9-1)=8\cdot3^{x-5}$; öng tomon: $2^{x-2}=8\cdot2^{x-5}$.
$3^{x-5}>2^{x-5}\Rightarrow\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^{x-5}>1\Rightarrow x>5$.

Javob: $x>5$.

## 2-test

### 1 [A]
Asos $2>1$, funksiya ösuvçi: $3{,}6>3{,}1\Rightarrow2^{3{,}6}>2^{3{,}1}$.

Javob: $>$.

### 2 [A]
Asos $\dfrac{11}5>1$, ösuvçi: $\dfrac23<\dfrac53$.

Javob: $<$.

### 3 [D]
Asos $0{,}7<1$, funksiya kamayuvçi; $-1{,}2<0$ bölgani uçun $0{,}7^{-1{,}2}>0{,}7^0=1$.

Javob: $>$.

### 4 [B]
$4\sqrt2=2^{\frac52}$, asos $2>1$: $x>\dfrac52$.

Javob: $x>\dfrac52$.

### 5 [C]
$\dfrac1{16}=\Bigl(\dfrac12\Bigr)^4$, asos $<1$ — belgi almaşadi: $x\ge4$.

Javob: $x\ge4$.

### 6 [B]
$9=\Bigl(\dfrac13\Bigr)^{-2}$, asos $<1$: $\dfrac{4x-7}{x-1}<-2$.
$\dfrac{4x-7+2x-2}{x-1}<0\Rightarrow\dfrac{6x-9}{x-1}<0\Rightarrow1<x<\dfrac32$.

Javob: $1<x<\dfrac32$.

### 7 [D]
$4^x(1+4+16)=21\cdot4^x<84\Rightarrow4^x<4\Rightarrow x<1$.

Javob: $x<1$.

### 8 [C]
Maxraj doim musbat: $3^x>81=3^4\Rightarrow x>4$.

Javob: $x>4$.

### 9 [B]
$4^x-64$ ning işorasi $x-3$ niki bilan bir xil.
$x(x-5)(x-3)<0$, intervallar usuli: $x<0$ yoki $3<x<5$.

Javob: $(-\infty;0)\cup(3;5)$.

### 10 [D]
$x^2-2x\le8\Rightarrow(x-4)(x+2)\le0\Rightarrow-2\le x\le4$.
Yiğindi: $-2-1+0+1+2+3+4=7$.

Javob: $7$.

### 11 [A]
$\Bigl(\dfrac14\Bigr)^x-16>0\Leftrightarrow x<-2$; $5^x-25>0\Leftrightarrow x>2$.
Yeçim: $x<-2$ yoki $x>2$.
Qanoatlantirmaydigan butun sonlar: $-2,-1,0,1,2$ — $5$ ta.

Javob: $5$.

### 12 [C]
$t=5^x$: $t^2-30t+125>0\Rightarrow(t-5)(t-25)>0$.
$t<5$ yoki $t>25$: $x<1$ yoki $x>2$.

Javob: $x<1$ yoki $x>2$.

### 13 [A]
$t=2^x$: $t^2-12t+32<0\Rightarrow4<t<8\Rightarrow2<x<3$.

Javob: $2<x<3$.

### 14 [B]
$t=2^x>0$, $t\ne8$. $t>8$ da öng tomon manfiy — yeçim yöq.
$0<t<8$: $5(8-t)\le31(t+1)\Rightarrow9\le36t\Rightarrow t\ge\dfrac14$.
$\dfrac14\le2^x<8\Rightarrow-2\le x<3$.

Javob: $[-2;3)$.

### 15 [D]
$8^x(2^x-3^x)-64(2^x-3^x)=(2^x-3^x)(8^x-64)\ge0$.
$2^x-3^x\ge0\Leftrightarrow x\le0$; $8^x-64\ge0\Leftrightarrow x\ge2$.
Ikkalasi $\le0$: $0\le x\le2$.

Javob: $[0;2]$.

### 16 [A]
$5^x\ge1\Rightarrow x\ge0$; $125-5^x>0\Rightarrow x<3$.

Javob: $[0;3)$.

### 17 [C]
Kamayuvçi funksiya: $f(0)=1$, $f(2)=\dfrac1{16}$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac1{16};1\Bigr]$.

### 18 [C]
$x^2-2x=(x-1)^2-1\ge-1$, demak $f\ge5^{-1}=\dfrac15$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac15;+\infty\Bigr)$.

### 19 [B]
$t=|x|$: $2^t(5^t-625)-4(5^t-625)=(2^t-4)(5^t-625)>0$.
Ikkalasi musbat: $t>4$; ikkalasi manfiy: $t<2$.
$|x|<2$ yoki $|x|>4$.

Javob: $(-\infty;-4)\cup(-2;2)\cup(4;+\infty)$.

### 20 [B]
$3^{x-2}(9-1)=8\cdot3^{x-2}$, $2^{x+1}=8\cdot2^{x-2}$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^{x-2}>1\Rightarrow x>2$.

Javob: $x>2$.

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
Asos $7>1$, ösuvçi: $0{,}8<1{,}2$.

Javob: $<$.

### 2 [C]
Asos $\dfrac83>1$, ösuvçi: $\dfrac74>\dfrac34$.

Javob: $>$.

### 3 [A]
Asos $0{,}4<1$, kamayuvçi; $0{,}3>0\Rightarrow0{,}4^{0{,}3}<0{,}4^0=1$.

Javob: $<$.

### 4 [B]
$7\sqrt7=7^{\frac32}$, asos $>1$: $x>\dfrac32$.

Javob: $x>\dfrac32$.

### 5 [D]
$\dfrac1{27}=\Bigl(\dfrac13\Bigr)^3$, asos $<1$: $x\ge3$.

Javob: $x\ge3$.

### 6 [D]
$5=\Bigl(\dfrac15\Bigr)^{-1}$, asos $<1$: $\dfrac{3x-5}{x-4}<-1$.
$\dfrac{3x-5+x-4}{x-4}<0\Rightarrow\dfrac{4x-9}{x-4}<0\Rightarrow\dfrac94<x<4$.

Javob: $\dfrac94<x<4$.

### 7 [B]
$5^x(1+5+25)=31\cdot5^x<775\Rightarrow5^x<25\Rightarrow x<2$.

Javob: $x<2$.

### 8 [A]
Maxraj doim musbat: $2^x>32\Rightarrow x>5$.

Javob: $x>5$.

### 9 [C]
$5^x-1$ ning işorasi $x$ niki bilan bir xil; $x^2+x-6=(x+3)(x-2)$.
$(x+3)x(x-2)<0$: $x<-3$ yoki $0<x<2$.

Javob: $(-\infty;-3)\cup(0;2)$.

### 10 [A]
$x^2-8x\le9\Rightarrow(x-9)(x+1)\le0\Rightarrow-1\le x\le9$.
Yiğindi: $-1+0+1+\dots+9=44$.

Javob: $44$.

### 11 [B]
$\Bigl(\dfrac15\Bigr)^x-5>0\Leftrightarrow x<-1$; $2^x-8>0\Leftrightarrow x>3$.
Yeçim: $x<-1$ yoki $x>3$.
Qanoatlantirmaydigan butun sonlar: $-1,0,1,2,3$ — $5$ ta.

Javob: $5$.

### 12 [A]
$t=4^x$: $t^2-20t+64>0\Rightarrow t<4$ yoki $t>16$.
$x<1$ yoki $x>2$.

Javob: $x<1$ yoki $x>2$.

### 13 [C]
$t=5^x$: $t^2-30t+125<0\Rightarrow5<t<25\Rightarrow1<x<2$.

Javob: $1<x<2$.

### 14 [C]
$t=4^x>0$, $t\ne16$. $t>16$ da yeçim yöq (öng tomon manfiy).
$0<t<16$: $5(16-t)\le63(t+1)\Rightarrow17\le68t\Rightarrow t\ge\dfrac14$.
$\dfrac14\le4^x<16\Rightarrow-1\le x<2$.

Javob: $[-1;2)$.

### 15 [A]
$16^x(4^x-5^x)-64(4^x-5^x)=(4^x-5^x)(16^x-64)\ge0$.
$4^x-5^x\ge0\Leftrightarrow x\le0$; $16^x\ge64\Leftrightarrow x\ge\dfrac32$.
Ikkalasi $\le0$: $0\le x\le\dfrac32$.

Javob: $\Bigl[0;\dfrac32\Bigr]$.

### 16 [B]
$4^x\ge1\Rightarrow x\ge0$; $64-4^x>0\Rightarrow x<3$.

Javob: $[0;3)$.

### 17 [D]
Kamayuvçi funksiya: $f(-1)=5$, $f(1)=\dfrac15$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac15;5\Bigr]$.

### 18 [A]
$x^2+4x=(x+2)^2-4\ge-4$, demak $f\ge4^{-4}=\dfrac1{256}$.

Javob: $\Bigl[\dfrac1{256};+\infty\Bigr)$.

### 19 [C]
$t=|x|$: $4^t(2^t-8)-4(2^t-8)=(4^t-4)(2^t-8)>0$.
Ikkalasi musbat: $t>3$; ikkalasi manfiy: $t<1$.
$|x|<1$ yoki $|x|>3$.

Javob: $(-\infty;-3)\cup(-1;1)\cup(3;+\infty)$.

### 20 [B]
$3^{x-6}(9-1)=8\cdot3^{x-6}$, $2^{x-3}=8\cdot2^{x-6}$.
$\Bigl(\dfrac32\Bigr)^{x-6}>1\Rightarrow x>6$.

Javob: $x>6$.
