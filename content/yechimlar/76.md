# 76. Trigonometrik tenglamalar (1-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
Xususiy hol: $\sin t=1\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.
$2x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$, ikkiga bölamiz: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 2 [C]
Formula: $\sin x=a\Rightarrow x=(-1)^n\arcsin a+\pi n$.
$\arcsin\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, demak $x=(-1)^n\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)+\pi n=(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 3 [A]
Formula: $x=(-1)^n\arcsin a+\pi n$, va $\arcsin\left(-\dfrac25\right)=-\arcsin\dfrac25$.
$x=(-1)^{n+1}\arcsin\dfrac25+\pi n$.

Javob: $(-1)^{n+1}\arcsin\dfrac25+\pi n$.

### 4 [D]
Xususiy hol: $\cos t=0\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.
$2x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 5 [B]
Formula: $\cos x=a\Rightarrow x=\pm\arccos a+2\pi n$.
$\arccos\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, demak $x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

### 6 [B]
$2x=\pm\arccos\left(-\dfrac13\right)+2\pi n$, bu yerda $\arccos\left(-\dfrac13\right)=\pi-\arccos\dfrac13$.
Ikkiga bölamiz: $x=\pm\dfrac12\left(\pi-\arccos\dfrac13\right)+\pi n$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac12\left(\pi-\arccos\dfrac13\right)+\pi n$.

### 7 [D]
Formula: $\operatorname{ctg}x=a\Rightarrow x=\operatorname{arcctg}a+\pi n$.
$\operatorname{arcctg}\left(-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\right)=\pi-\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 8 [A]
$2x=90^\circ+360^\circ n\Rightarrow x=45^\circ+180^\circ n$.
$0^\circ<x<240^\circ$ oraliqda: $45^\circ$ va $225^\circ$.

Javob: $\{45^\circ; 225^\circ\}$.

### 9 [B]
$\operatorname{ctg}t=0\Rightarrow t=90^\circ+180^\circ n$: $2x=90^\circ+180^\circ n$, $x=45^\circ+90^\circ n$.
$0^\circ<x<180^\circ$ da: $45^\circ$, $135^\circ$.

Javob: $\{45^\circ; 135^\circ\}$.

### 10 [A]
$\cos t=-1\Rightarrow t=\pi+2\pi n$: $\pi x=\pi+2\pi n$, $x=1+2n$.
$0<x<8$ da: $1;3;5;7$.

Javob: $\{1; 3; 5; 7\}$.

### 11 [D]
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}(2x+1)=2\pi k\Rightarrow2x+1=6k\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{6k-1}{2}$.
$k=1,2,3$ da $x=2{,}5;\ 5{,}5;\ 8{,}5$ — $(0;10)$ da. $k=4$ da $11{,}5$ — tashqarida.
Demak, $3$ ta ildiz.

Javob: $3$.

### 12 [A]
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}(x+2)=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi k\Rightarrow x+2=1+3k\Rightarrow x=3k-1$.
$(0;10)$ da: $2;5;8$, yiğindisi $15$.

Javob: $15$.

### 13 [C]
$\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{6}=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi x}{6}=\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi k\Rightarrow x=\pm4+12k$.
$(1;23)$ da: $4;8;16;20$.
Ayirma: $20-4=16$.

Javob: $16$.

### 14 [D]

$(0;10)$ da: $5$ va $7$.
$25+49=74$.

Javob: $74$.

### 15 [A]

$x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 16 [B]

$x=\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 17 [A]

$4x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 18 [C]

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 19 [B]
$\cos^2x=1-\sin^2x$: $2-2\sin^2x-3\sin x-3=0\Rightarrow2\sin^2x+3\sin x+1=0$.
$\sin x=-1$ yoki $\sin x=-\dfrac12$.
$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$; $x=(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n; (-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

### 20 [B]

$\sin x=0\Rightarrow x=\pi n$; $\sin x=1\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

### 21 [D]

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \operatorname{arctg}\dfrac23+\pi n$.

### 22 [C]

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n; 2\pi n$.

### 23 [C]

### 24 [A]

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{10}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{5}$.

### 25 [B]

$5x=\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{15}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{5}$.

Javob: $\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{15}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{5}$.

### 26 [D]

$x=-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 27 [B]
$\sin x\cos x(\sin^2x-\cos^2x)=-\dfrac12\sin2x\cos2x=-\dfrac14\sin4x$.
$-\dfrac14\sin4x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{8}\Rightarrow\sin4x=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$4x=(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

Javob: $(-1)^{n+1}\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{4}$.

### 28 [C]
$\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}x}{1-\operatorname{tg}^2x}=\dfrac12\operatorname{tg}2x$, demak $\operatorname{tg}2x=1$.
$2x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

### 29 [A]
Kasr nolga teng: surat $\operatorname{tg}x=1$, maxraj $x\ne\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$.
$x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi k$, $[0;6\pi]$ da: $\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{5\pi}{4},\dfrac{9\pi}{4},\dfrac{13\pi}{4},\dfrac{17\pi}{4},\dfrac{21\pi}{4}$ — $6$ ta.
$\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ ni çiqarsak, $5$ ta qoladi.

Javob: $5$.

### 30 [C]
Yeçim bölişi uçun $-1\le\dfrac{a+1}{a-3}\le1$.
$\dfrac{a+1}{a-3}-1=\dfrac4{a-3}\le0\Rightarrow a<3$.
$\dfrac{a+1}{a-3}+1=\dfrac{2a-2}{a-3}\ge0\Rightarrow a\le1$ yoki $a>3$.
Kesişma: $a\le1$.

Javob: $(-\infty; 1]$.
