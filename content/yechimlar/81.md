# 81. Murakkab funksiya hosilasi

## 1-test

### 1 [D]
Murakkab funksiya hosilasi: $\bigl(u^n\bigr)'=nu^{n-1}\cdot u'$. Bu yerda $u=1-4x$, $u'=-4$.
$y'=60(1-4x)^{59}\cdot(-4)=-240(1-4x)^{59}$.

Javob: $-240(1-4x)^{59}$.

### 2 [B]
$y=(2x-1)^{\frac52}$, $(u^n)'=nu^{n-1}u'$, $u'=2$.
$y'=\dfrac52(2x-1)^{\frac32}\cdot2=5\sqrt{(2x-1)^3}$.

Javob: $5\sqrt{(2x-1)^3}$.

### 3 [D]
$(e^u)'=e^u\cdot u'$, $u=3-2x$, $u'=-2$.
$y'=-2e^{3-2x}$.

Javob: $-2e^{3-2x}$.

### 4 [B]
$(a^u)'=a^u\ln a\cdot u'$, $u=3-4x$, $u'=-4$.
$y'=-4\cdot5^{3-4x}\ln5$.

Javob: $-4\cdot5^{3-4x}\ln5$.

### 5 [B]
$(\log_a u)'=\dfrac{u'}{u\ln a}$, $u=2x-7$, $u'=2$.
$y'=\dfrac{2}{(2x-7)\ln5}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{(2x-7)\ln5}$.

### 6 [D]
$(\operatorname{ctg}u)'=-\dfrac{u'}{\sin^2u}$, $u=3x+1$, $u'=3$.
$y'=-\dfrac{3}{\sin^2(3x+1)}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{\sin^2(3x+1)}$.

### 7 [B]
$(\sin kx)'=k\cos kx$, $(\cos kx)'=-k\sin kx$, bu yerda $k=\dfrac14$.
$y'=\dfrac14\cos\dfrac x4-\dfrac14\sin\dfrac x4=\dfrac14\left(\cos\dfrac x4-\sin\dfrac x4\right)$.

Javob: $\dfrac14\left(\cos\dfrac x4-\sin\dfrac x4\right)$.

### 8 [D]
$f(x)=2(3x+1)^{-2}$, $f'(x)=2\cdot(-2)(3x+1)^{-3}\cdot3=-\dfrac{12}{(3x+1)^3}$.
$f'(-1)=-\dfrac{12}{(-2)^3}=\dfrac{12}{8}=\dfrac32$.

Javob: $\dfrac32$.

### 9 [C]
$f'(x)=2^{3x-5}\ln2\cdot3\cdot\log_2e$. Bunda $\ln2\cdot\log_2e=1$.
Demak, $f'(x)=3\cdot2^{3x-5}$, $f'(2)=3\cdot2^1=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 10 [C]
$f'(x)=\cos x-2\sin2x$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi6\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}-2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

### 11 [D]
Köpaytma hosilasi va $(e^{-x})'=-e^{-x}$:
$f'(x)=e^{-x}-xe^{-x}=e^{-x}(1-x)$.
$f'(2)=e^{-2}\cdot(-1)=-\dfrac{1}{e^2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{e^2}$.

### 12 [A]
$(u^6)'=6u^5u'$, $u=x^2-4x+1$, $u'=2x-4=2(x-2)$.
$y'=6(x^2-4x+1)^5\cdot2(x-2)=12(x-2)(x^2-4x+1)^5$.

Javob: $12(x-2)(x^2-4x+1)^5$.

### 13 [B]
$(\ln u)'=\dfrac{u'}{u}$, $u=2-\cos x$, $u'=\sin x$.
$y'=\dfrac{\sin x}{2-\cos x}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sin x}{2-\cos x}$.

### 14 [A]
$(\sqrt u)'=\dfrac{u'}{2\sqrt u}$, $u=4x^2+3x$, $u'=8x+3$.
$y'=\dfrac{8x+3}{2\sqrt{4x^2+3x}}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{8x+3}{2\sqrt{4x^2+3x}}$.

### 15 [C]
$(e^u)'=e^uu'$, $u=\dfrac1x$, $u'=-\dfrac{1}{x^2}$.
$y'=-\dfrac{e^{\frac1x}}{x^2}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{e^{\frac1x}}{x^2}$.

### 16 [A]
$(\cos u)'=-\sin u\cdot u'$, $u=\ln x$, $u'=\dfrac1x$.
$y'=-\dfrac{\sin(\ln x)}{x}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\sin(\ln x)}{x}$.

### 17 [A]
$(\arcsin u)'=\dfrac{u'}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}$, $u=2x$, $u'=2$.
$y'=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1-4x^2}}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1-4x^2}}$.

### 18 [A]
$f'(x)=\dfrac{8-2x}{8x-x^2}$.
$f'(2)=\dfrac{4}{16-4}=\dfrac{4}{12}=\dfrac13$.

Javob: $\dfrac13$.

### 19 [C]
$(e^u)'=e^uu'$: $f'(x)=e^{x^2+4\sin x}(2x+4\cos x)$.
$f'(0)=e^0\cdot(0+4)=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 20 [B]
$(\operatorname{tg}u)'=\dfrac{u'}{\cos^2u}$, $u=\sin x$: $f'(x)=\dfrac{\cos x}{\cos^2(\sin x)}$.
$f'(0)=\dfrac{1}{\cos^20}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 21 [A]
Soddalaştiramiz: $\sin^4x-\cos^4x=(\sin^2x-\cos^2x)(\sin^2x+\cos^2x)=-\cos2x$.
$f'(x)=2\sin2x$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi6\right)=2\sin\dfrac\pi3=2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\sqrt3$.

Javob: $\sqrt3$.

### 22 [A]
$(u^3)'=3u^2u'$, $u=\ln x$, $u'=\dfrac1x$.
$y'=\dfrac{3\ln^2x}{x}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{3\ln^2x}{x}$.

### 23 [D]
$(\operatorname{arctg}u)'=\dfrac{u'}{1+u^2}$, $u=x^2-1$, $u'=2x$.
$1+(x^2-1)^2=x^4-2x^2+2$, demak $y'=\dfrac{2x}{x^4-2x^2+2}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{2x}{x^4-2x^2+2}$.

### 24 [A]
$x=4$ da $x^2-4x+3=3>0$, demak nuqta atrofida $f(x)=x^2-4x+3$.
$f'(x)=2x-4$, $f'(4)=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 25 [B]
$(\ln u)'=\dfrac{u'}{u}$, $u=2+\ln(x^2-8)$, $u'=\dfrac{2x}{x^2-8}$.
$x_0=3$ da $x^2-8=1$, $u=2+0=2$, $u'=6$.
$f'(3)=\dfrac62=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 26 [C]
Logarifmlab hosila olamiz: $\ln y=x^2\ln x$.
$\dfrac{y'}{y}=2x\ln x+x^2\cdot\dfrac1x=x(2\ln x+1)$.
$y'=x^{x^2}\cdot x(2\ln x+1)=x^{x^2+1}(2\ln x+1)$.

Javob: $x^{x^2+1}(2\ln x+1)$.

### 27 [B]
Ikkala tomondan hosila olamiz: $2f'(2x)=3x^2-6x+5$.
$2x=4$, ya'ni $x=2$ da: $2f'(4)=12-12+5=5$, $f'(4)=\dfrac52$.

Javob: $\dfrac52$.

### 28 [D]
Hosila olamiz: $4f'(4x-3)=g'(x^2-2x+5)\cdot(2x-2)$.
$4x-3=5$ va $x^2-2x+5=5$ bir vaqtda $x=2$ da bajariladi.
$x=2$: $4\cdot3=g'(5)\cdot2 \Rightarrow g'(5)=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 29 [C]
Ketma-ket hosila olamiz:
$f'=2\cos2x$, $f''=-4\sin2x$, $f'''=-8\cos2x$, $f^{(4)}=16\sin2x$.

Javob: $16\sin2x$.

### 30 [C]
$(x-1)^2$ ga bölinsa, $x=1$ ikki karrali ildiz: $P(1)=0$ va $P'(1)=0$.
$P(1)=2-1+a+b+4=0 \Rightarrow a+b=-5$.
$P'(x)=10x^4-3x^2+2ax+b$, $P'(1)=7+2a+b=0 \Rightarrow 2a+b=-7$.
Ayiramiz: $a=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.
