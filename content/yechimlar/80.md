# 80. Hosila (2-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Logarifmik funksiya hosilasi: $(\log_a x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln a}$, demak $f'(x)=\dfrac{1}{x\ln2}$.
$x_0=\dfrac{1}{\ln2}$ da: $f'(x_0)=\dfrac{1}{\frac{1}{\ln2}\cdot\ln2}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 2 [B]
$(a^x)'=a^x\ln a$, demak $f'(x)=3^x\ln3$.
$3^{\log_35}=5$ bölgani uçun $f'(\log_35)=5\ln3$.

Javob: $5\ln3$.

### 3 [B]
$\left(\dfrac{10^x}{\ln10}\right)'=\dfrac{10^x\ln10}{\ln10}=10^x$, $\left(-\dfrac2x\right)'=\dfrac{2}{x^2}$, $(3x)'=3$.
$f'(x)=10^x+\dfrac{2}{x^2}+3$.
$f'(-1)=\dfrac{1}{10}+2+3=\dfrac{51}{10}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{51}{10}$.

### 4 [C]
$(a^x)'=a^x\ln a$, $(\cos x)'=-\sin x$.
$y'=3^x\ln3-3\sin x$.

Javob: $3^x\ln3-3\sin x$.

### 5 [A]
$(\operatorname{ctg}x)'=-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}=-(1+\operatorname{ctg}^2x)$.
$y'=1-(1+\operatorname{ctg}^2x)=-\operatorname{ctg}^2x$.

Javob: $-\operatorname{ctg}^2x$.

### 6 [D]
$f'(x)=-3\sqrt3\sin x+2$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi3\right)=-3\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+2=-\dfrac92+2=-\dfrac52$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{5}{2}$.

### 7 [B]
$f'(x)=-\sin x-\dfrac{4}{\pi}x$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi2\right)=-1-\dfrac4\pi\cdot\dfrac\pi2=-1-2=-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 8 [C]
Köpaytma hosilasi $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$, $(\operatorname{tg}x)'=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}$.
$y'=2x\operatorname{tg}x+x^2\cdot\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}=2x\operatorname{tg}x+\dfrac{x^2}{\cos^2x}$.

Javob: $2x\operatorname{tg}x+\dfrac{x^2}{\cos^2x}$.

### 9 [A]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$, $(\log_3x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln3}$.
$y'=2x\log_3x+x^2\cdot\dfrac{1}{x\ln3}=2x\log_3x+\dfrac{x}{\ln3}$.

Javob: $2x\log_3x+\dfrac{x}{\ln3}$.

### 10 [A]
Köpaytma hosilasi: $y'=x'\cdot e^x+x\cdot(e^x)'=e^x+xe^x=(x+1)e^x$.

Javob: $(x+1)e^x$.

### 11 [D]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$: $y'=e^x\sin x+e^x\cos x=e^x(\sin x+\cos x)$.

Javob: $e^x(\sin x+\cos x)$.

### 12 [A]
Bölinma hosilasi $\left(\dfrac uv\right)'=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$:
$$y'=\dfrac{-\sin x(1+2\sin x)-\cos x\cdot2\cos x}{(1+2\sin x)^2}=\dfrac{-\sin x-2(\sin^2x+\cos^2x)}{(1+2\sin x)^2}.$$
$\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$, demak suratda $-2-\sin x$.

Javob: $\dfrac{-2-\sin x}{(1+2\sin x)^2}$.

### 13 [C]
Bölinma hosilasi:
$$y'=\dfrac{e^x\cdot x-e^x\cdot1}{x^2}=\dfrac{e^x(x-1)}{x^2}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{e^x(x-1)}{x^2}$.

### 14 [D]
$f'(x)=2x\cos x-(x^2+2)\sin x$.
$\cos\pi=-1$, $\sin\pi=0$: $f'(\pi)=2\pi\cdot(-1)-0=-2\pi$.

Javob: $-2\pi$.

### 15 [A]
$u=x^2+1+\sin x$, $u'=2x+\cos x$; $v=\cos x$, $v'=-\sin x$.
$x_0=0$ da: $u=1$, $u'=1$, $v=1$, $v'=0$.
$f'(0)=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}=\dfrac{1\cdot1-1\cdot0}{1}=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 16 [B]
$f'(x)=\dfrac{\frac1x\cdot x-\ln x}{x^2}=\dfrac{1-\ln x}{x^2}$.
$f'(e)=\dfrac{1-1}{e^2}=0$, $f'\left(\dfrac1e\right)=\dfrac{1-(-1)}{e^{-2}}=2e^2$.
$f'(e)-f'\left(\dfrac1e\right)=0-2e^2=-2e^2$.

Javob: $-2e^2$.

### 17 [D]
$g(x)$ ni soddalaştiramiz: $(5x-3)(2-x)=-5x^2+13x-6$, demak $g(x)=8x^2-13x+6$.
$f'(x)=4x$, $g'(x)=16x-13$.
$4x=16x-13 \Rightarrow 12x=13 \Rightarrow x=\dfrac{13}{12}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{13}{12}$.

### 18 [B]
$f'(x)=3x^2-12x=3x(x-4)$.
$3x(x-4)\le0$ tengsizlik ildizlar orasida bajariladi: $0\le x\le4$.

Javob: $[0; 4]$.

### 19 [B]
$f'(x)=\cos x$, tenglama: $\sin x=\cos x$.
$\cos x\ne0$ (aks holda $\sin x=\pm1$ bölardi), ikkala tomonni $\cos x$ ga bölamiz: $\operatorname{tg}x=1$.
$x=\dfrac\pi4+\pi n$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

### 20 [A]
$f'(x)=-30x-6x^2=-6x(x+5)$, $g'(x)=6x^2+18>0$ har doim.
Demak, kasr musbat $\Leftrightarrow -6x(x+5)>0 \Leftrightarrow x(x+5)<0$.
Bu $-5<x<0$ da bajariladi.

Javob: $(-5; 0)$.

## 2-test

### 1 [B]
$(\lg x)'=\dfrac{1}{x\ln10}$.
$x_0=\dfrac{1}{100\ln10}$ da: $f'(x_0)=\dfrac{1}{\frac{1}{100\ln10}\cdot\ln10}=100$.

Javob: $100$.

### 2 [A]
$(2^x)'=2^x\ln2$, $2^{\log_27}=7$.
$f'(\log_27)=7\ln2$.

Javob: $7\ln2$.

### 3 [A]
$\dfrac{\lg x}{\lg e}=\ln x$ (asosga ötiş formulasi), demak $f(x)=2\ln x-\dfrac2x+3x-12$.
$f'(x)=\dfrac2x+\dfrac{2}{x^2}+3$.
$f'(2)=1+\dfrac12+3=\dfrac92$.

Javob: $\dfrac{9}{2}$.

### 4 [C]
$(\sin x)'=\cos x$, $(\cos x)'=-\sin x$.
$y'=\cos x-2\sin x$.

Javob: $\cos x-2\sin x$.

### 5 [A]
$y'=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}=\dfrac{\sin^2x-\cos^2x}{\sin^2x\cos^2x}$.
$\sin^2x-\cos^2x=-\cos2x$, $\sin^2x\cos^2x=\dfrac{\sin^22x}{4}$.
$y'=-\dfrac{4\cos2x}{\sin^22x}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{4\cos2x}{\sin^22x}$.

### 6 [B]
$f'(x)=2\cos x-3\sin x$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi2\right)=2\cdot0-3\cdot1=-3$.

Javob: $-3$.

### 7 [A]
$f'(x)=\sqrt2\cos x+\dfrac{\sqrt\pi}{2\sqrt x}$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi4\right)=\sqrt2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt\pi}{2\cdot\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}}=1+1=2$.

Javob: $2$.

### 8 [C]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$, $(\sqrt x)'=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt x}$:
$y'=\dfrac{\cos x}{2\sqrt x}-\sqrt x\sin x$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\cos x}{2\sqrt x}-\sqrt x\sin x$.

### 9 [D]
$y'=4x^3\ln x+x^4\cdot\dfrac1x=4x^3\ln x+x^3$.

Javob: $4x^3\ln x+x^3$.

### 10 [C]
$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$, $(2^x)'=2^x\ln2$:
$y'=2x\cdot2^x+x^2\cdot2^x\ln2$.

Javob: $2x\cdot2^x+x^2\cdot2^x\ln2$.

### 11 [C]
$y'=e^x\operatorname{tg}x+e^x\cdot\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}=e^x\left(\operatorname{tg}x+\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}\right)$.

Javob: $e^x\left(\operatorname{tg}x+\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}\right)$.

### 12 [B]
Bölinma hosilasi:
$$y'=\dfrac{\cos x(1+\cos x)+\sin^2x}{(1+\cos x)^2}=\dfrac{\cos x+1}{(1+\cos x)^2}=\dfrac{1}{1+\cos x}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{1+\cos x}$.

### 13 [D]
$$y'=\dfrac{e^x(1-e^x)-(1+e^x)(-e^x)}{(1-e^x)^2}=\dfrac{e^x-e^{2x}+e^x+e^{2x}}{(1-e^x)^2}=\dfrac{2e^x}{(1-e^x)^2}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{2e^x}{(1-e^x)^2}$.

### 14 [A]
$f'(x)=\dfrac1\pi(2x\sin x+x^2\cos x)$.
$x_0=\dfrac\pi2$ da $\sin x=1$, $\cos x=0$: $f'=\dfrac1\pi\cdot2\cdot\dfrac\pi2=1$.

Javob: $1$.

### 15 [A]
$$f'(x)=\dfrac{\cos x(2-x)-\sin x\cdot(-1)}{(2-x)^2}.$$
$f'(0)=\dfrac{1\cdot2+0}{4}=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 16 [D]
$f'(x)=\ln x+x\cdot\dfrac1x=\ln x+1$.
$f'(e)=2$, $f'\left(\dfrac{1}{e^2}\right)=-2+1=-1$.
$2-(-1)=3$.

Javob: $3$.

### 17 [B]
$f'(x)=5x^4-12x^2$, $g'(x)=53x^2-180$.
$5x^4-65x^2+180=0 \Rightarrow x^4-13x^2+36=0$.
$x^2=4$ yoki $x^2=9$, demak $x=\pm2$, $x=\pm3$.

Javob: $\pm2; \pm3$.

### 18 [C]
$f'(x)=6x^2+6x-12=6(x+2)(x-1)$.
$(x+2)(x-1)>0$ ildizlardan taşqarida: $x<-2$ yoki $x>1$.

Javob: $(-\infty; -2)\cup(1; +\infty)$.

### 19 [B]
$f'(x)=\cos x-\sin x$.
$\sin x+\cos x=\cos x-\sin x \Rightarrow 2\sin x=0 \Rightarrow x=\pi n$.

Javob: $\pi n$.

### 20 [D]
Avval qöşamiz: $f(x)+g(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{x-3}$, hosila ham yiğindi hosilasiga teng.
$$\left(\dfrac{x^2}{x-3}\right)'=\dfrac{2x(x-3)-x^2}{(x-3)^2}=\dfrac{x(x-6)}{(x-3)^2}.$$
$x(x-6)<0$, $x\ne3$: $0<x<6$, $x\ne3$.

Javob: $(0; 3)\cup(3; 6)$.

## 3-test

### 1 [D]
$f'(x)=\dfrac1x+2x$.
$f'(2)=\dfrac12+4=\dfrac92$.

Javob: $\dfrac{9}{2}$.

### 2 [C]
$f'(x)=e^x+1$, $e^{\ln4}=4$.
$f'(\ln4)=4+1=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 3 [D]
$\left(\dfrac{2^x}{\ln2}\right)'=2^x$, $(4\ln x)'=\dfrac4x$, $x'=1$.
$f'(x)=2^x+\dfrac4x-1$, $f'(1)=2+4-1=5$.

Javob: $5$.

### 4 [A]
$(\sin x)'=\cos x$: $y'=1-\cos x$.

Javob: $1-\cos x$.

### 5 [D]
$(\operatorname{tg}x)'=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}=1+\operatorname{tg}^2x$.
$y'=2-(1+\operatorname{tg}^2x)=1-\operatorname{tg}^2x$.

Javob: $1-\operatorname{tg}^2x$.

### 6 [C]
$f'(x)=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}+4$.
$\cos^2\dfrac\pi4=\dfrac12$, demak $f'\left(\dfrac\pi4\right)=2+4=6$.

Javob: $6$.

### 7 [A]
$\cos\dfrac\pi4$ — özgarmas son, hosilasi $0$.
$f'(x)=-\sqrt3\sin x+\dfrac6\pi x$.
$f'\left(\dfrac\pi3\right)=-\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+\dfrac6\pi\cdot\dfrac\pi3=-\dfrac32+2=\dfrac12$.

Javob: $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 8 [C]
$(\operatorname{ctg}x)'=-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}$, köpaytma hosilasi:
$y'=2x\operatorname{ctg}x-\dfrac{x^2}{\sin^2x}$.

Javob: $2x\operatorname{ctg}x-\dfrac{x^2}{\sin^2x}$.

### 9 [B]
$y'=1\cdot\ln x+x\cdot\dfrac1x=\ln x+1$.

Javob: $\ln x+1$.

### 10 [D]
$y'=e^x(x^2-2x+2)+e^x(2x-2)=e^x(x^2-2x+2+2x-2)=x^2e^x$.

Javob: $x^2e^x$.

### 11 [A]
$y'=e^x\operatorname{ctg}x+e^x\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}\right)=e^x\left(\operatorname{ctg}x-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}\right)$.

Javob: $e^x\left(\operatorname{ctg}x-\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}\right)$.

### 12 [A]
Bölinma hosilasi:
$$y'=\dfrac{e^x\sin x-e^x\cos x}{\sin^2x}=\dfrac{e^x(\sin x-\cos x)}{\sin^2x}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{e^x(\sin x-\cos x)}{\sin^2x}$.

### 13 [C]
$$y'=\dfrac{\cos x\cdot e^x-\sin x\cdot e^x}{e^{2x}}=\dfrac{\cos x-\sin x}{e^x}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{\cos x-\sin x}{e^x}$.

### 14 [B]
$f'(x)=(2x-4)\operatorname{tg}x+(x^2-4x+4)\cdot\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}$.
$x_0=0$ da $\operatorname{tg}0=0$, $\cos0=1$: $f'(0)=0+4=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 15 [B]
$u=x+e^x$, $u'=1+e^x$; $v=x-e^x$, $v'=1-e^x$.
$x_0=0$ da: $u=1$, $u'=2$, $v=-1$, $v'=0$.
$f'(0)=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}=\dfrac{2\cdot(-1)-0}{1}=-2$.

Javob: $-2$.

### 16 [D]
$f'(x)=9x^2-9x+2$, $f'(0)=2$.
$g'(x)=\dfrac{2(x-1)-2x}{(x-1)^2}=-\dfrac{2}{(x-1)^2}$, $g'(2)=-2$.
$f'(0)-g'(2)=2+2=4$.

Javob: $4$.

### 17 [B]
$f'(x)=3x^2-4x$, $g'(x)=2x+9$.
$3x^2-6x-9=0 \Rightarrow x^2-2x-3=0 \Rightarrow x=-1$ yoki $x=3$.

Javob: $-1; 3$.

### 18 [A]
$f'(x)=4x^3-16x=4x(x-2)(x+2)$.
Intervallar usuli: ildizlar $-2; 0; 2$, eng öngda işora musbat.
$f'(x)\ge0$: $[-2; 0]\cup[2; +\infty)$.

Javob: $[-2; 0]\cup[2; +\infty)$.

### 19 [D]
$f'(x)=-\sin x$, tenglama: $-\sin x=\sqrt3\cos x$.
$\cos x\ne0$, bölamiz: $\operatorname{tg}x=-\sqrt3$.
$x=-\dfrac\pi3+\pi n$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

### 20 [D]
$f(x)+g(x)=\dfrac{x^2+3-4}{x+1}=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x+1}=x-1$, $x\ne-1$.
Hosila: $(x-1)'=1>0$ — aniqlaniş sohasining hamma nuqtasida.
Demak, $x\ne-1$.

Javob: $(-\infty; -1)\cup(-1; +\infty)$.
