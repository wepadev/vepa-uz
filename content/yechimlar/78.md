# 78. Trigonometrik tengsizliklar

## 1-test

### 1 [A]
Birlik aylanada $\sin x=-\dfrac12$ nuqtalar $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ va $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$. Sinus ulardan pastda bölgan yoy: $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}<x<-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
Davrni qöşamiz.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n; -\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 2 [D]
$t=3x$: $\sin t\ge\dfrac12\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le t\le\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
Uçga bölamiz: $\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{5\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\right]$.

### 3 [D]
$\sin x=-\dfrac14$ nuqtalar: $-\arcsin\dfrac14$ va $-\pi+\arcsin\dfrac14$. Sinus pastda bölgan yoy ular orasida.
Tengsizlik qat'iy emas, uçlar kiradi.

Javob: $\left[-\pi+\arcsin\dfrac14+2\pi n; -\arcsin\dfrac14+2\pi n\right]$.

### 4 [B]
$\cos x=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ nuqtalar $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ va $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. Kosinus ulardan kiçik bölgan yoy (aylananing çap qismi): $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}<x<\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 5 [A]
$t=2x$: $\cos t\ge-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\Rightarrow-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le t\le\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
Ikkiga bölamiz: $-\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n\right]$.

### 6 [D]
$t=x+\dfrac{\pi}{6}$. Kotangens $(0;\pi)$ da kamayuvçi, $\operatorname{ctg}t<\sqrt3\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n<t<\pi+\pi n$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ ni ayiramiz: $\pi n<x<\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{6}+\pi n\right)$.

### 7 [A]
Kotangens $(0;\pi)$ da kamayuvçi, $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{5\pi}{6}=-\sqrt3$.
$\operatorname{ctg}x<-\sqrt3\Rightarrow\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+\pi n<x<\pi+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+\pi n; \pi+\pi n\right)$.

### 8 [C]
Tangens $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ da ösuvçi: $\operatorname{tg}x<3\Rightarrow-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n<x<\operatorname{arctg}3+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n; \operatorname{arctg}3+\pi n\right)$.

### 9 [C]
$\sin x\le\dfrac12$: $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right]$. $\sin x>-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ undan $\left[\dfrac{4\pi}{3};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right]$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{3};\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right]$, ikkinçisi $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right]$ bilan bir xil.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 10 [D]
$\cos x\le\dfrac12$: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right]$. $\cos x>-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ undan $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\right]$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{6};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 11 [B]
Tangens ösuvçi: $\operatorname{tg}\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=-1$, $\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n\le x<\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n\right)$.

### 12 [D]
$\cos x-5<0$ har doim, demak $\cos3x-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}<0$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n<3x<\dfrac{11\pi}{6}+2\pi n\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}<x<\dfrac{11\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{11\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\right)$.

### 13 [C]
$t=\cos x$: $2t^2+11t-6<0$, ildizlar $t=\dfrac{-11\pm13}{4}$: $\dfrac12$ va $-6$.
$-6<t<\dfrac12$, ya'ni $\cos x<\dfrac12$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n<x<\dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 14 [C]
Oraliqlar usuli: $\sin x\le\dfrac12$ yoki $\sin x\ge\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$\sin x\ge\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right]$; $\sin x\le\dfrac12$: $\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{13\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 15 [D]
Köpaytma manfiy: $0<\cos x<\dfrac12$.
Aylanada bu ikki yoy: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ va $\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{2};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 16 [B]
Köpaytma musbat bölişi uçun $\sqrt{\sin x}>0$, ya'ni $\sin x>0$; unda $\sin x+\dfrac12>0$ avtomatik.
$\sin x>0\Rightarrow2\pi n<x<\pi+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(2\pi n; \pi+2\pi n\right)$.

### 17 [A]
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi k\le2x\le\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi k\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi k\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi k$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da $k=0$ va $k=1$: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6};\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right]$ va $\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6};\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}; \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6}; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right]$.

### 18 [D]
Çap tomon $\cos6x$, demak $\cos6x\le\dfrac12$: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi k\le6x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi k$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{\pi k}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{18}+\dfrac{\pi k}{3}$. $k=2$ da $\left[\dfrac{13\pi}{18};\dfrac{17\pi}{18}\right]$, $k=3$ dagi oraliq $\pi$ dan keyin boşlanadi.
Eng katta yeçim $\dfrac{17\pi}{18}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{17\pi}{18}$.

### 19 [A]
Yeçimlar: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi k;\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi k\right]$.
$k=0$: $[0{,}52;\ 2{,}62]$ — butunlar $1;2$. $k=1$: $[6{,}81;\ 8{,}90]$ — butunlar $7;8$. $k=2$ oraliq $10$ dan katta.
Jami $4$ ta.

Javob: $4$.

### 20 [B]
$\sin x\cos x\cos2x=\dfrac12\sin2x\cos2x=\dfrac14\sin4x$, demak $\sin4x\le-\dfrac12$.
$\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi k\le4x\le\dfrac{11\pi}{6}+2\pi k\Rightarrow\dfrac{7\pi}{24}+\dfrac{\pi k}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{11\pi}{24}+\dfrac{\pi k}{2}$.
$[0;\pi]$ da $k=0$ va $k=1$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{24}; \dfrac{11\pi}{24}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{19\pi}{24}; \dfrac{23\pi}{24}\right]$.

### 21 [A]
Asos $4>1$: $0<\cos2x\le1$. Öng tengsizlik doim bajariladi, demak $\cos2x>0$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n<2x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\Rightarrow-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n<x<\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n\right)$.

### 22 [C]
Daraja pasaytiriş: $\dfrac{1+\cos4x}{2}\ge\dfrac12\Rightarrow\cos4x\ge0$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\le4x\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\Rightarrow-\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; \dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\right]$.

### 23 [B]
Kotangens $(0;\pi)$ da kamayuvçi: $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{\pi}{4}=1$, $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{3\pi}{4}=-1$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n<x<\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{4}+\pi n\right)$.

### 24 [C]
$\sin x+3>0$ har doim, demak $\sin x-1\ge0$, ya'ni $\sin x\ge1$.
Bu faqat $\sin x=1$ da: $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right\}$.

## 2-test

### 1 [C]
$\sin x=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ nuqtalar $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ va $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$. Sinus ulardan pastda: $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\le x\le\dfrac{9\pi}{4}$, ya'ni $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 2 [B]
$\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n<\dfrac{x}{2}<\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.
Ikkiga köpaytiramiz: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}+4\pi n<x<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+4\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+4\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{2}+4\pi n\right)$.

### 3 [C]
Kosinus $\dfrac14$ dan katta bölgan yoy $x=0$ atrofida: $-\arccos\dfrac14<x<\arccos\dfrac14$.

Javob: $\left(-\arccos\dfrac14+2\pi n; \arccos\dfrac14+2\pi n\right)$.

### 4 [A]
$\arccos\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, yoy $x=0$ atrofida: $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}<x<\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 5 [D]
$-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n<\dfrac{x}{3}<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.
Uçga köpaytiramiz: $-\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+6\pi n<x<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+6\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+6\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{2}+6\pi n\right)$.

### 6 [B]
$\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\le\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ ni ayiramiz: $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 7 [D]
Tangens ösuvçi, $\operatorname{tg}\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=-1$: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\right)$.

### 8 [A]
$\operatorname{arctg}(-2)=-\operatorname{arctg}2$, tangens ösuvçi: $-\operatorname{arctg}2+\pi n\le x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[-\operatorname{arctg}2+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi n\right)$.

### 9 [C]
$\sin x<\dfrac12$: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right)$. $\sin x\ge-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ undan $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4};\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{4};\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right)$, ikkinçisi $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{4};\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 10 [C]
$\cos x<\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4};\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)$. $\cos x\ge-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ undan $\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{4};\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4};\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{4};\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{7\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 11 [D]
Tangens ösuvçi: $\operatorname{tg}0=0$, $\operatorname{tg}\dfrac{\pi}{4}=1$.
$\pi n\le x<\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n\right)$.

### 12 [B]
$\sin x+3>0$ har doim, demak $\sin x\le-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$\sin x=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ nuqtalar $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ va $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, ular orasida sinus pastda.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n; -\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 13 [B]
$t=\sin x$: $2t^2+7t+3=(2t+1)(t+3)\ge0$. $t+3>0$, demak $t\ge-\dfrac12$.
$\sin x\ge-\dfrac12\Rightarrow-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le x\le\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 14 [A]
Köpaytma manfiy: $-\dfrac12<\cos x<\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
$\cos x<\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6};\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\right)$, undan $\cos x\le-\dfrac12$ qismi $\left[\dfrac{2\pi}{3};\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right]$ çiqariladi.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right)\cup\left(\dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{11\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 15 [B]
Oraliqlar usuli: $\sin x\le0$ yoki $\sin x\ge\dfrac12$.
$\sin x\ge\dfrac12$: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6};\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right]$; $\sin x\le0$: $[\pi;2\pi]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\pi+2\pi n; 2\pi+2\pi n\right]$.

### 16 [D]
Musbat bölişi uçun $\cos x>0$ va $\cos x>\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, ya'ni $\cos x>\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n<x<\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 17 [A]
$\operatorname{tg}x\ge1\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi k\le x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi k$.
$[0;2\pi]$ da $k=0$ va $k=1$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4}; \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{4}; \dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)$.

### 18 [A]
Ayirma kosinusi: çap tomon $\cos4x$, demak $\cos4x\le\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n\le4x\le\dfrac{7\pi}{4}+2\pi n\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{7\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.
Eng kiçik musbat yeçim $\dfrac{\pi}{16}$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\pi}{16}$.

### 19 [B]
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi k<2x<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi k\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi k<x<\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+\pi k$.
$k=0$: $(0{,}79;2{,}36)$ — $1;2$. $k=1$: $(3{,}93;5{,}50)$ — $4;5$. $k=2$: $(7{,}07;8{,}64)$ — $8$.
Jami $5$ ta.

Javob: $5$.

### 20 [A]
$\cos^4x-\sin^4x=(\cos^2x-\sin^2x)(\cos^2x+\sin^2x)=\cos2x<\dfrac12$.
$x\in[0;\pi]$ da $2x\in[0;2\pi]$: $\dfrac{\pi}{3}<2x<\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$, ya'ni $\dfrac{\pi}{6}<x<\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}; \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)$.

### 21 [C]
Asos $\sqrt3>1$: $0<\operatorname{tg}x<\sqrt3$.
$\pi n<x<\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n\right)$.

### 22 [B]
$\sin^4 2x\ge\dfrac14\Rightarrow\sin^2 2x\ge\dfrac12\Rightarrow\dfrac{1-\cos4x}{2}\ge\dfrac12\Rightarrow\cos4x\le0$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\le4x\le\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{3\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; \dfrac{3\pi}{8}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\right]$.

### 23 [D]
Tangens ösuvçi: $\pi n\le3x\le\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.
Uçga bölamiz: $\dfrac{\pi n}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{12}+\dfrac{\pi n}{3}\right]$.

### 24 [A]
$\cos3x-4<0$ har doim, demak $\cos3x+1\le0$, ya'ni $\cos3x=-1$.
$3x=\pi+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\right\}$.

## 3-test

### 1 [B]
$\sin x=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ nuqtalar $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ va $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. Sinus ulardan yuqori bölgan yoy: $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}<x<\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 2 [D]
$\sin t\le0\Rightarrow-\pi+2\pi n\le t\le2\pi n$, $t=4x$.
Törtga bölamiz: $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; \dfrac{\pi n}{2}\right]$.

### 3 [A]
$\cos x=-\dfrac23$ nuqtalar $\arccos\left(-\dfrac23\right)$ va $2\pi-\arccos\left(-\dfrac23\right)$. Kosinus kiçik bölgan yoy ular orasida (aylananing çap qismi), uçlar kiradi.

Javob: $\left[\arccos\left(-\dfrac23\right)+2\pi n; 2\pi-\arccos\left(-\dfrac23\right)+2\pi n\right]$.

### 4 [C]
$\arccos\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, yoy $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 5 [B]
$\cos t\le\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2\pi n\le t\le\dfrac{7\pi}{4}+2\pi n$, $t=4x$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{7\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; \dfrac{7\pi}{16}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\right]$.

### 6 [B]
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n<2x-\dfrac{\pi}{3}<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n\Rightarrow\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n<2x<\dfrac{11\pi}{6}+2\pi n$.
$\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n<x<\dfrac{11\pi}{12}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi n; \dfrac{11\pi}{12}+\pi n\right)$.

### 7 [D]
Kotangens $(0;\pi)$ da kamayuvçi, $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$.
$\pi n<x\le\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n\right]$.

### 8 [A]
Kotangens kamayuvçi: $\operatorname{ctg}x<3\Rightarrow\operatorname{arcctg}3+\pi n<x<\pi+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\operatorname{arcctg}3+\pi n; \pi+\pi n\right)$.

### 9 [D]
$\sin x<-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4};\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)$. $\sin x\ge-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ undan $\left(\dfrac{4\pi}{3};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4};\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{3};\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\right)$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{7\pi}{4}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 10 [B]
$\cos x<0$: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)$. $\cos x\ge-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ undan $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6};\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\right)$ ni olib taşlaydi.
Qoladi: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6};\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right)$.

### 11 [A]
Tangens ösuvçi: $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n\le2x<\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\pi n$.
Ikkiga bölamiz: $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\le x<\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\pi n}{2}\right)$.

### 12 [C]
$\cos2x+3>0$ har doim, demak $\sin3x\le\dfrac12$.
$-\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n\le3x\le\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi n\Rightarrow-\dfrac{7\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}$.

Javob: $\left[-\dfrac{7\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}; \dfrac{\pi}{18}+\dfrac{2\pi n}{3}\right]$.

### 13 [A]
$t=\cos x$: $2t^2+5t-3=(2t-1)(t+3)\le0$. $t+3>0$, demak $t\le\dfrac12$.
$\cos x\le\dfrac12\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 14 [A]
Köpaytma $\le0$: $-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\le\sin x\le-\dfrac12$.
$\sin x\le-\dfrac12$: $\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6};\dfrac{11\pi}{6}\right]$, undan $\sin x<-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ qismi $\left(\dfrac{4\pi}{3};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right)$ çiqariladi.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{7\pi}{6}+2\pi n; \dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{11\pi}{6}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 15 [D]
Köpaytma $\le0$: $-\dfrac12\le\cos x\le0$.
Aylanada ikki yoy: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{2};\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right]$ va $\left[\dfrac{4\pi}{3};\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 16 [B]
Ildiz uçun $\cos x\ge0$. Bunda köpaytma $\le0$ bölişi uçun $\cos x=0$ yoki $\cos x\le\dfrac12$, ya'ni $0\le\cos x\le\dfrac12$.
Ikki yoy: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3};\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ va $\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{2};\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\right]$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+2\pi n; \dfrac{5\pi}{3}+2\pi n\right]$.

### 17 [A]
Umumiy yeçim: $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\pi n\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi n$.
$[-\pi;\pi]$ ga $n=0$ dan $\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{4};\pi\right]$, $n=-1$ dan $\left[-\pi;-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]$ tuşadi.

Javob: $\left[-\pi; -\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]\cup\left[\dfrac{3\pi}{4}; \pi\right]$.

### 18 [C]
Ayirma sinusi: çap tomon $\sin(x-3x)=-\sin2x$, demak $\sin2x\ge\dfrac12$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2\pi k\le2x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi k\Rightarrow\dfrac{\pi}{12}+\pi k\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{12}+\pi k$.
$[-\pi;0]$ da $k=-1$: $\left[-\dfrac{11\pi}{12};-\dfrac{7\pi}{12}\right]$. Eng kiçigi $-\dfrac{11\pi}{12}$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{11\pi}{12}$.

### 19 [D]
$\operatorname{tg}x>0\Rightarrow\pi k<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}+\pi k$.
Oraliqlar: $(0;1{,}57)$, $(3{,}14;4{,}71)$, $(6{,}28;7{,}85)$, $(9{,}42;11{,}0)$. $[1;10]$ dagi butunlar: $1;4;7;10$.
Jami $4$ ta.

Javob: $4$.

### 20 [B]
$\sin2x-\cos2x=\sqrt2\sin\left(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\ge0$.
$0\le2x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\le\pi$ ($[0;\pi]$ uçun mos oraliq): $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\le x\le\dfrac{5\pi}{8}$.

Javob: $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{8}; \dfrac{5\pi}{8}\right]$.

### 21 [C]
Asos $\dfrac13<1$: $0<\sin^2x\le1$. Öng tomon doim bajariladi, demak $\sin x\ne0$.
$x\ne\pi n$.

Javob: $x\ne\pi n$.

### 22 [C]
Kub ösuvçi: $\cos^3 2x>\left(\dfrac12\right)^3\Rightarrow\cos2x>\dfrac12$.
$-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n<2x<\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\pi n\Rightarrow-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n<x<\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n; \dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n\right)$.

### 23 [D]
Kotangens $(0;\pi)$ da kamayuvçi: $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\sqrt3$, $\operatorname{ctg}\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$.
$\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n<x\le\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\pi n; \dfrac{2\pi}{3}+\pi n\right]$.

### 24 [C]
$\sin2x+5>0$ har doim, demak $\sin2x+1\le0$, ya'ni $\sin2x=-1$.
$2x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi n\Rightarrow x=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n$.

Javob: $\left\{-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\pi n\right\}$.
