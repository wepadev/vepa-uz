# 85. Boşlanğiç funksiya va integral (2-qism)

## 1-test

### 1 [C]
Agar $F'=f$ bölsa, $\displaystyle\int f(kx+b)\,dx=\dfrac1kF(kx+b)+C$.
Bu yerda $k=\dfrac34$: $6\cdot\dfrac{1}{\frac34}F\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)=8F\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)$.
Tekşiruv: $\left(8F\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)\right)'=8\cdot\dfrac34f\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)=6f\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)$.

Javob: $8F\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)$.

### 2 [C]
$\displaystyle\int f(kx+b)\,dx=\dfrac1kF(kx+b)+C$, bu yerda $k=-4$.
Demak, $-\dfrac14F(1-4x)$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{4}F(1-4x)$.

### 3 [A]
$\displaystyle\int(kx+b)^n\,dx=\dfrac{(kx+b)^{n+1}}{k(n+1)}+C$, $k=-2$, $n=5$.
$\dfrac{(5-2x)^6}{-2\cdot6}+C=-\dfrac{(5-2x)^6}{12}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\left(5-2x\right)^{6}}{12}+C$.

### 4 [B]
$k=3$, $n=\dfrac25$, $n+1=\dfrac75$:
$\dfrac{(3x-1)^{\frac75}}{3\cdot\frac75}+C=\dfrac{5(3x-1)^{\frac75}}{21}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{5\left(3x-1\right)^{\frac{7}{5}}}{21}+C$.

### 5 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{kx+b}=\dfrac1k\ln|kx+b|+C$.
$4\cdot\dfrac17\ln|7x-2|+C=\dfrac47\ln|7x-2|+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{4}{7}\ln|7x-2|+C$.

### 6 [D]
$\displaystyle\int e^{kx+b}dx=\dfrac1ke^{kx+b}+C$, $k=5$.

Javob: $\dfrac{e^{5x-1}}{5}+C$.

### 7 [B]
$\dfrac{1}{5^{4x+1}}=5^{-(4x+1)}$, $\displaystyle\int a^{u}dx=\dfrac{a^u}{u'\ln a}$, $u'=-4$.
$\dfrac{5^{-(4x+1)}}{-4\ln5}+C=-\dfrac{1}{4\ln5\cdot5^{4x+1}}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{1}{4\ln 5\cdot 5^{4x+1}}+C$.

### 8 [A]
$\displaystyle\int\cos(kx+b)dx=\dfrac1k\sin(kx+b)+C$, bu yerda $k=-\dfrac53$.
$-\dfrac35\sin\left(\dfrac{4-5x}{3}\right)+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{3}{5}\sin\left(\dfrac{4-5x}{3}\right)+C$.

### 9 [D]
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{\cos^2(kx+b)}=\dfrac1k\operatorname{tg}(kx+b)+C$, $k=4$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\operatorname{tg}(4x-2)}{4}+C$.

### 10 [A]
Köpaytmani yiğindiga keltiramiz: $\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\dfrac12\bigl(\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)\bigr)$.
$\sin2x\cos7x=\dfrac12(\sin9x-\sin5x)$.
$\displaystyle\int=\dfrac12\left(-\dfrac{\cos9x}{9}+\dfrac{\cos5x}{5}\right)+C=-\dfrac{\cos9x}{18}+\dfrac{\cos5x}{10}+C$.

Javob: $-\dfrac{\cos 9x}{18}+\dfrac{\cos 5x}{10}+C$.

### 11 [C]
$\sin\alpha\sin\beta=\dfrac12\bigl(\cos(\alpha-\beta)-\cos(\alpha+\beta)\bigr)$.
$\sin8x\sin2x=\dfrac12(\cos6x-\cos10x)$.
$\displaystyle\int=\dfrac{\sin6x}{12}-\dfrac{\sin10x}{20}+C$.

Javob: $\dfrac{\sin 6x}{12}-\dfrac{\sin 10x}{20}+C$.

### 12 [D]
Butun qismini ajratamiz: $\dfrac{9x-2}{3x+2}=\dfrac{3(3x+2)-8}{3x+2}=3-\dfrac{8}{3x+2}$.
$\displaystyle\int=3x-\dfrac83\ln|3x+2|+C$.

Javob: $3x-\dfrac{8}{3}\ln|3x+2|+C$.

### 13 [B]

$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{u'}{u}dx=\ln|u|$, demak $3\ln|x^2+4|+C$.

Javob: $3\ln|x^2+4|+C$.

### 14 [B]

Javob: $\ln|4\sin x-3\cos x|+C$.

### 15 [C]

### 16 [C]

### 21 [B]

### 24 [A]

### 26 [C]

### 27 [C]

### 28 [A]

### 29 [D]

### 30 [D]
